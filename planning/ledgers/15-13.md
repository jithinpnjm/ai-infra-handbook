## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) In `ASP` Table 13-2, `aten::matmul` (`43.00 ms`) + `aten::linear` (`35.50 ms`) = **`78.50 ms`** of GEMM compute while `dispatch` (`18.70 ms`) + `combine` (`12.10 ms`) = **`30.80 ms`** of MoE token-shuffle communication, and in Table 13-3, CUPTI projects asynchronous GPU kernel durations onto host NVTX intervals so `forward` (`60.5 ms`) + `backward` (`58.3 ms`) + `optimizer_step` (`19.2 ms`, `1` host marker bracketing `64` `aten::add_` launches) sum exactly to **`138.0 ms`** (`train_step`); (b) on an NVIDIA L40S (`362 TFLOPS`, `864 GB/s`, ridge `~419 FLOPS/B`) at `h = 4096`, prefill arithmetic intensity is **`62.06 FLOPS/B`** at `s = 64` (memory-bound), **`409.60 FLOPS/B`** at `s = 512` (near Roofline knee / memory-bound), and **`1,365.33 FLOPS/B`** at `s = 4096` (strongly compute-bound), whereas single-token decode (`s = 1`) is **`~1.00 FLOPS/B`** (strictly memory-bandwidth-bound).
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Splitting a `20,000`-token prompt into four `5,000`-token chunks (`ASP` Table 16-5) executes **`[12.5M, 37.5M, 62.5M, 87.5M]`** attention QK operations summing to **`200.0M`** (`200,010,000` exact under `T(N) = N(N+1)/2`) and **`20,000` MLP tokens**, proving chunked prefill does not reduce total FLOPs but caps per-step stall at `87.5M` instead of `200.0M` so decode tokens interleave without head-of-line blocking; (b) for prompts `[6K, 2K, 6K, 2K, 2K, 2K]` across `2` GPUs (`ASP` Tables 16-6 & 16-7), naive FIFO places `[6K, 2K, 6K]` on GPU 1 (**`38,007,000` QK ops, `14,000` MLP tokens**) and `[2K, 2K, 2K]` on GPU 2 (**`6,003,000` QK ops, `6,000` MLP tokens**), whereas Latency-Aware scheduling places `[2K, 2K, 6K]` on each GPU (**`22,005,000` QK ops, `10,000` MLP tokens**), cutting critical-path attention work by **`42.10%`**.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) In Volume 12 Lab 04, replacing single-threaded preprocessing with multi-core `ProcessPoolExecutor` lifts throughput **`7.73x`** from **`13.47 RPS` to `104.17 RPS`** and cuts total `p99` latency **`31.48x`** from **`1,410.20 ms` to `44.80 ms`**, and streaming `100` tokens at `20 tok/s` in `5`-token batches delivers the first chunk in **`0.25 s`** instead of waiting **`5.0 s`** (`20x` faster perceived start); (b) `Llama-3-70B` GQA uses **`327,680 B/tok` (`320 KiB`) in FP16** and **`163,840 B/tok` (`160 KiB`) in FP8**, swapping `4.0 GB` over PCIe Gen4 (`64 GB/s`) takes **`62.5 ms` one-way and `125.0 ms` round-trip**, and `roundup_power2_divisions:[256:1,512:2,1024:4,>:8]` divides `[512, 1024]` into two `256 MB` steps so a `600 MB` request rounds up to **`768 MB`**.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) An `80`-layer `Llama-3-70B` with `TP = 8` executes **`160` AllReduces/token** (**`8,000` AllReduces/s** at `50 tok/s`), **`14` ring steps/call**, and **`28,672` bytes/rank/call** for `B = 1, H = 8192` FP16, and an `XID 62` DB-ECC deadlock on one pod in an `8`-pod deployment blackholes **`12.5%` (`1/8`)** of cluster traffic; (b) summarizing the oldest `25%` (`3,000 -> 300` tokens) at `12,000` tokens (`75%` of `16,000`) shrinks the prompt to **`9,300` tokens** and cuts `T(N)` from `72,006,000` to `43,249,650` (**`39.94%` saved**), while the `70B`/`700B` cascade at `60%` small-model share achieves **`230.0 ms` expected latency and `4.6` relative cost** without fallback, or **`260.0 ms` and `5.2` relative cost** with a `10%` sequential fallback.
