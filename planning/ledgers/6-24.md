## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Generating Unified LDIF, Slurm Associations, and Home Quotas):** `identity_gen.py` validates `uid >= 10000`, unique usernames/UIDs, absolute shell paths, and integer quotas, and outputs `users.ldif` (`posixAccount`/`ldapPublicKey` and `posixGroup` at `gidNumber: 20000`), `slurm_accounts.sh` (`sacctmgr add account/user`), and `homes.sh` (`install -d -m 0700` and `90%` soft / `100%` hard `lfs setquota`).
2. **Exercise 2 (Pull-Based Static Account Sync with `SIGALRM` Timeout Protection):** Each node runs `/usr/sbin/master-sync` (`rsync` over SSH) at boot and when triggered by the master wrapper, which uses `ssh -o connecttimeout=3`, `sleep 0.1`, and `(sleep 600 && kill -ALRM "$$") &` with `trap 'exit' ALRM` so offline nodes never stall the loop.
3. **Exercise 3 (Configuring SSSD, `nsswitch.conf`, and LDAP SSH Key Lookup):** Configure `/etc/sssd/sssd.conf` (`0600`) with `services = nss, pam, ssh`, `cache_credentials = true`, `enumerate = false`, `entry_cache_timeout = 5400`, and `ldap_user_ssh_public_key = sshPublicKey`, and point `sshd_config` `AuthorizedKeysCommand` to `/usr/bin/sss_ssh_authorizedkeys`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Migrating Flat Files to OpenLDAP and Legacy NIS+):** NIS (`ypserv`, `yppasswdd`, `ypxfrd`, `ypbind`, `ypinit -m`) hits RPC limits above `30–40` clients; OpenLDAP (`slappasswd`, `slapd.conf`, `ldap-init.ldif`) imports `/etc/passwd` and `/etc/group` via `migrate_passwd.pl` and `migrate_group.pl`.
5. **Exercise 5 (Tuning Login-Node vs Compute-Node `ulimit`s and Blocking Access):** Login nodes cap `nproc` (`50–100`) without setting an overly low hard `cpu` limit that kills `scp`/`sftp`, whereas compute nodes require `memlock unlimited` (instead of `64` KB) for RDMA and high `nofile` (instead of `1024`); block access via `DenyUsers`, `pam_access.so`, `chsh -s /bin/false`, or `scponly`.
6. **Exercise 6 (Designing an Automated Onboarding, Offboarding, and Nightly Reconciliation Workflow):** On offboarding, lock LDAP and remove SSH keys first, cancel Slurm jobs and zero/remove `sacctmgr` associations (retaining history), revoke tokens, and retire the numeric UID permanently; reconcile LDAP, `sacctmgr`, `/home`, and quotas nightly.
