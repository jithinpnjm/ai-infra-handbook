## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Tunnels, Overlays, Underlays, Recursive Lookups, and the Two Core Virtualization Questions, `CN` Chapter 9):** (a) A **tunnel** is a single virtual point-to-point link encapsulating an inner packet inside an outer header, an **overlay** is a virtual network topology built from tunnels, and the **underlay** is the physical/routed network transporting the outer packets via a **recursive lookup** on the tunnel endpoint (`VTEP`) IP; (b) every virtualization design must answer (1) how data is encapsulated (`MTU` overhead and ASIC/ECMP hash support) and (2) how reachability through the tunnel is advertised (integrated vs separate overlay control plane / VRF).
2. **Exercise 2 (Comparing Encapsulations `GRE`, `IPIP`, `VXLAN`, `Geneve`, `MPLS`, `SRv6`, `WireGuard`, `IPsec` and ECMP Entropy):** `VXLAN` (`UDP 4789`, `50` B overhead, `24`-bit VNI) and `Geneve` (`UDP 6081`, extensible TLV options) hash the inner packet's 5-tuple into the **outer UDP source port** so underlay switches get full ECMP entropy without inspecting inner headers, whereas `GRE` (`24` B, proto `47`), `IPIP` (`20` B), and `IPsec ESP` lack outer L4 ports (requiring `NAT-T` UDP `4500` or inner-header hashing).
3. **Exercise 3 (VXLAN Flood-and-Learn vs. BGP `EVPN` Route Types `1`–`5`, `RD` vs. `RT`, and ARP Suppression):** Using `EVPN_ROUTE_TYPES`: **Type 1** is Ethernet Auto-Discovery (multihoming fast convergence/aliasing), **Type 2** is MAC/IP Advertisement (host reachability, **ARP/ND suppression**, and MAC mobility sequence numbers), **Type 3** is Inclusive Multicast (`IMET`, building the per-VNI BUM replication list), **Type 4** is Ethernet Segment (`DF` election across multihomed leaves sharing a 10-byte `ESI`), and **Type 5** is IP Prefix advertisement; **Route Distinguishers (`RD`s)** make overlapping tenant prefixes unique in MP-BGP, while **Route Targets (`RT`s)** control which VRFs import/export which routes.
4. **Exercise 4 (Asymmetric vs. Symmetric `IRB`, Distributed Anycast Gateway, and `vnifilter` Single-VXLAN Device):** With a **distributed anycast gateway**, every leaf configures the identical gateway IP and MAC on its local SVI so VMs/containers always route at their first-hop leaf; **Asymmetric IRB** (bridge-then-route on ingress, bridge on egress) forces every leaf to configure all `200` tenant L2 VNIs, whereas **Symmetric IRB** (route on ingress into a per-tenant **L3 VNI**, route on egress to the destination L2 VNI) requires only the leaf's `5` local L2 VNIs + `1` L3 VNI = `6` VNIs (`compute_irb_vni_scaling_per_leaf()`).
5. **Exercise 5 (Segment Routing `SR-MPLS` vs. `SRv6` / `uSID` and `SD-WAN`, `CN` Chapter 9):** Segment Routing encodes an explicit path at the ingress node as an ordered stack of **Node SIDs** (globally unique) and **Adjacency SIDs** (locally significant per link) with zero per-flow transit state; in **SR-MPLS** (`simulate_sr_mpls_label_stack()`), `PUSH` imposes the label stack, `POP` removes the top label, and `CONTINUE` swaps with the same label, while **SRv6** uses `128`-bit IPv6 SIDs (`Locator:Function:Arg`) or compressed `16`-bit **`uSID`s**.
6. **Exercise 6 (Virtualization Complexity: Interaction Surfaces, `SRLG` Shared Fate, and Why AI Back-End Fabrics Avoid Overlays):** Two logically redundant overlay tunnels fail simultaneously if their underlay paths traverse the same physical fiber conduit or spine switch (**Shared Risk Link Group `SRLG`**, `check_overlay_srlg_diversity()`); GPU back-end RoCEv2/InfiniBand fabrics avoid VXLAN overlays on the collective path to preserve full payload MTU, hardware RDMA offloads, UDP source-port entropy, and direct lossless `PFC`/`ECN` queue mapping.
