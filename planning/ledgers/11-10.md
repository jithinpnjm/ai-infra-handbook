## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions
1. **Exercise 1:** (a) `65,534` usable IPs in `/16`; (b) `254` usable IPs in `/24`; (c) `8` IPs per `/29` Calico block; (d) `252` usable secondary IPs in `/24` after excluding `.1/32` and `.254/32`.
2. **Exercise 2:** `[("eth0", "cilium", "primary_cluster_network"), ("net1", "roce-sriov-network", "secondary_high_speed_fabric"), ("net2", "roce-sriov-network", "secondary_high_speed_fabric")]`, requiring `nvidia.com/hostdev: 2`.
3. **Exercise 3:** `GPU 2`: `("PCIe Switch 1", "NIC 2", "Rail 3", "Leaf Switch 2", 0)`; `GPU 6`: `("PCIe Switch 3", "NIC 6", "Rail 7", "Leaf Switch 6", 1)`.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) `failing_layer="rdma_or_sriov_device_plugin"` (request `rdma/hca`, `rdma/rdma_shared_device_a`, or `nvidia.com/hostdev` so `/dev/infiniband/` is mounted); (b) `failing_layer="nccl_interface_or_roce_qos_gid"` (set `NCCL_SOCKET_IFNAME=net1`, `NCCL_IB_HCA=mlx5_0,mlx5_1`, `NCCL_IB_GID_INDEX=3`, and `NCCL_IB_TC=106`).
5. **Exercise 5:** `macvlan` + `rdma_shared_device_a` shares the host's Physical Function (`PF`) without hardware isolation, whereas `SriovNetworkNodePolicy` allocates a dedicated PCIe Virtual Function (`VF`) per Pod with hardware-enforced VLAN tagging (`vlan: 100`), MAC spoof protection, rate limiting, and per-VF NUMA hints.
6. **Exercise 6:** (1) `kubectl exec -n <ns> llm-training-ib-master-0 -- ip addr show net1`; (2) `kubectl exec -n <ns> llm-training-ib-master-0 -- ls /dev/infiniband/`; (3) `kubectl logs -n <ns> llm-training-ib-master-0 | grep "NCCL INFO"`.
