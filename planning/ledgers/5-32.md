## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Separating Fault Detection from Budgeted Remediation across `volume-10`):** Node-local detectors (`dcgm-exporter`, `node-problem-detector`) translate hardware/XID telemetry into `NodeCondition` `GPUHealthy=False`, while the cluster-wide `GPUHealthPolicy` operator enforces stabilization windows, disruption budgets, and provenance-safe cordons/drains.
2. **Exercise 2 (Designing the Pure Decision Core `Judge`, `Budget`, and `Plan`):** `judge_node()` classifies False $\ge 2\text{m}$ as `unhealthy`, False $< 2\text{m}$ as `pending` with `wait_for_sec`, True $\ge 5\text{m}$ and absent as `healthy`, and `Unknown` as neither; `compute_budget()` fails safe to `0` on invalid input; and `plan_remediation()` counts all unschedulable nodes (including human cordons) against `budget`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Time-Window Stabilization in a Stateless Level-Triggered Controller):** Instead of sleeping inside `Reconcile`, `Judge` computes remaining `WaitFor` / `HealthyWaitFor` durations from `now.Sub(cond.LastTransitionTime.Time)` and `Reconcile` returns `ctrl.Result{RequeueAfter: requeue + time.Second}`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Provenance Annotations `cordoned-by`, Finalizer Release `releaseAll`, and Human Maintenance Safety):** `cordon()` stamps `platform.example.com/cordoned-by: <policy.Name>` via `client.MergeFrom`, and both `uncordon` and `releaseAll` act strictly on nodes bearing `AnnotationOwner == p.Name`, leaving human maintenance cordons untouched.
5. **Exercise 5 (Safe Node Draining `drain` & `evictable` with `FieldIndexer` and `PodDisruptionBudget` Backoff):** `is_pod_evictable()` skips `Succeeded`/`Failed`, mirror (`kubernetes.io/config.mirror`), and `DaemonSet` pods; `drain()` uses the `spec.nodeName` `FieldIndexer` and `r.SubResource("eviction").Create`, treating `429 TooManyRequests` as a normal PDB block (`EvictionBlocked` event + 15s requeue).
6. **Exercise 6 (Analyzing the 5 `envtest` Scenarios and Designing a Global Circuit Breaker):** `suite_test.go` verifies cordon/recovery, budget exhaustion, dry-run, `RequeueAfter` stabilization, and finalizer cleanup under `envtest` (while live drain and RBAC require a real cluster); a global circuit breaker trips when $\ge X\%$ of matched nodes report unhealthy simultaneously.
