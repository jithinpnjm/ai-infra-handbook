3. **Exercise 3:** Team A is borrowing `18` GPUs (`28 - 10`), can borrow at most `4` more GPUs before hitting its `32`-GPU (`10 + 22`) ceiling, and is preempted (`preempt_triggered=True`) when Team B submits an `8`-GPU job under `reclaimWithinCohort: Any`.
