## NVIDIA DGX vs OEM HGX vs Rack-Scale GB200/GB300 Claims
- **DGX vs OEM HGX (`volume-06/ch1–ch6` & `lab-01, lab-02`):** DGX is built and supported end-to-end by NVIDIA (`Systems/DGX`, `Systems/HGX_Baseboard_0`, `Chassis/DGX`, `Managers/BMC`, `1:1` ratio of `8` GPUs to `8` `400 Gbps` ConnectX-7 NICs = `3.2 Tbps` egress). OEM HGX integrates an NVIDIA HGX baseboard (`8` bolted FRU SXM GPUs at `700 W` each = `5.6 kW`, `4` soldered NVSwitch ASICs at `900 GB/s`, PCIe Gen5 retimers, `HMC`, `ERoT`, `48–54V` class power delivery vs `12V` `> 80 A/kW`) into an OEM host chassis (`iDRAC`, `iLO`, `XClarity`), where the OEM owns the firmware qualification bundle and hardware RMA dispatch.
- **GB300 NVL72 SuperPOD Architecture (`superpod-gb300-ra` & `bcm11-admin`):** Each NVL72 rack contains compute trays, `9` NVLink switch trays (`2` COMe RJ45 ports for NVOS + `1` BMC RJ45 port), and `8` power shelves (`6 x 5.5 kW` PSUs = `33 kW` in `N` redundancy + front BMC port + rear RJ45 ports), connected to an isolated OOB Ethernet fabric of `SN2201` switches rolled up into a per-SU spine VXLAN.
- **BCM `nvsm` Wrapper (`bcm11-admin` §10.13):** `nvsm alerts`, `nvsm health` (writes `.tar.xz` to `/cm/shared/nvsm/<node>/`, `~15` minutes, `--quick`), `nvsm status`, `nvsm stop`, `nvsm info [--history]` (`417 MiB`, `5.9 MiB` examples), and `nvsm versions [--details]`.
