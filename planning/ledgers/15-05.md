## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** Folding BatchNorm with `scale_0 = 1.0 / sqrt(0.25) = 2.0` and `scale_1 = 2.0 / sqrt(1.0) = 2.0` yields **`W_hat = [[4.0, -8.0], [3.0, 6.0]]` and `b_hat = [1.1, 1.5]`**, and horizontal fusion concatenates `W_Q`, `W_K`, and `W_V` into a single `W_QKV` matrix so `X` is read once and CUDA kernel launches drop from **`3` to `1` (`3.0x` reduction)**.
2. **Exercise 2:** In FP32 (`4` bytes), `MAX = (128, 3, 4096, 4096)` requires **`25,769,803,776` bytes (`25.77 GB` / `25.7 GB`)** per layer and `MAX = (128, 8192, 8192)` requires **`34,359,738,368` bytes (`34.36 GB` / `34 GB`)** per layer, whereas bounding `max = (32, 3, 2048, 2048)` in FP16 (`2` bytes) drops the per-layer max buffer to **`0.81 GB`** (`5.61 GB` total with a `4 GB` workspace cap, finishing in `4.2 min` at `6.1 GB` peak VRAM).
3. **Exercise 3:** Naive `max(|X|)` scaling at `T = 102.4` gives **`S = 102.4 / 127 = 0.806299`** whereas the `2048`-bin KL-divergence minimum at bin `512` (`T* = 25.6`) gives **`S = 25.6 / 127 = 0.201575` (`4.0x` finer resolution)**; SmoothQuant at `alpha = 0.5` yields **`s = [8.0, 5.0, 4.0]`**, equalizing both activation and weight channel maxima to **`[8.0, 20.0, 4.0]`**.
4. **Exercise 4:** A `70B` model's weights require **`280.0 GB` in FP32, `140.0 GB` in FP16/BF16, `70.0 GB` in FP8/INT8, `35.0 GB` raw / `36.09 GB` effective in INT4 AWQ (`group_size=128`), and `35.0 GB` raw / `39.38 GB` effective in NVFP4 (`16`-element FP8 microblocks)**; `gpt-oss-120b` (`117B`) at `0.5 B/param` consumes **`58.5 GB`**, fitting on a single `80 GB` GPU.
5. **Exercise 5:** At `TP=8, B=64, S=1, H=8192` in FP16, each `AllReduce` moves **`1,835,008` bytes (`1.75 MiB`)** and all `160` `AllReduce`s (`2` per layer across `80` layers) move **`280.0 MiB`**, taking `0.326 ms` on `900 GB/s` NVLink vs `5.872 ms` on `50 GB/s` InfiniBand (**`18.0x` slower**); at `B=4, S_kv=8192` on a `132`-SM H100, `FlashDecoding`/`XQA` (`256`-token chunks -> `32` splits) increases active thread blocks from **`4` (`3.03%` SM occupancy) to `128` (`96.97%` SM occupancy, `8.0x` speedup)**.
6. **Exercise 6:** Llama-3-70B KV cache consumes **`327,680 B` (`320.0 KB/token`, `1.31 GB` per `4K` seq) in FP16 and `163,840 B` (`160.0 KB/token`, `0.65 GB` per `4K` seq) in FP8**; on a `129`-token sequence, dropping `tokens_per_block` from `128` to `32` cuts wasted slots from **`127/256` (`49.61%`) to `31/160` (`19.38%`)**, and lowering `free_gpu_mem_fraction` from `0.95` to `0.85` on `180 GB` post-weight VRAM triples activation headroom from **`9.0 GB` to `27.0 GB`** while expanding full 4K-sequence capacity from `136` to `244` (and real sessions from `64` to `210`).
