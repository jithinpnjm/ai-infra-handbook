## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Deduplicating Multi-Partition Slurm Metrics):** `slurm_exporter.parse()` tracks `seen` node names so `gpu002` (in both `gpu` and `debug`) is counted once (`40` total GPUs, `11` allocated); scraping every 1 second would flood `slurmctld` with RPCs.
2. **Exercise 2 (Linting Prometheus GPU Alert Rules Against `dcgm-exporter`):** `check_alert_rules.py` verifies YAML structure, balanced brackets, `page|warn|ticket` severities, `summary`/`runbook` annotations, `for:` durations on paging rules (except discrete `GpuRowRemapFailure`), and `DCGM_FI_*` membership in `default-counters.csv`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Configuring SNMP Agents and `snmptrapd` Handlers):** Use `snmpget`, `snmptranslate -Td -Ib`, and `snmpwalk` to query MIBs, `rocommunity public 192.168.0.0` in `snmpd.conf`, and `authCommunity` + `traphandle` in `snmptrapd.conf` to pipe `host`, `ip`, and `oid val` lines to an action script.
4. **Exercise 4 (Programming `lm_sensors` `/etc/sensors3.conf` Formulas and Thresholds):** `compute in3 ((6.8/10)+1)*@, @/((6.8/10)+1)` scales raw `2.9762 V` by `1.68` to display `5.00 V` and inverts `set in3_min`/`in3_max` limits when `sensors -s` writes them to the `lm78-i2c-2d` chip.
5. **Exercise 5 (Configuring Multi-Vendor UPS Monitoring with `NUT` and `apcupsd`):** Configure `/etc/nut/{ups.conf,upsd.conf,upsd.users,upsmon.conf}` and query with `upsc ups1@localhost`; `upsmon slave` compute nodes shut down first to shed load while the `upsmon master` storage/management server shuts down last.
6. **Exercise 6 (Distinguishing `DCGM_FI_DEV_GPU_UTIL` from True Tensor Efficiency):** `99%` `GPU_UTIL` with `4%` `PIPE_TENSOR_ACTIVE`, `82%` `DRAM_ACTIVE`, and `260 W` power indicates a memory-bandwidth-bound kernel (or small batch size) leaving Tensor Cores idle; Open XDMoD + `SUPReMM` surfaces low-efficiency jobs per account.
