## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (`nvidia-fabricmanager` boot ordering):** If `kubelet` (`5.234s`) starts before `nvidia-fabricmanager` (`15.234s`), pods launch before the 4 NVSwitches are programmed, causing `NCCL` to fail P2P init or fall back to PCIe (`~800 GB/s -> ~90 GB/s`); prevent it by adding a drop-in with `[Unit]\nAfter=nvidia-fabricmanager.service\nWants=nvidia-fabricmanager.service` to `containerd.service` and `kubelet.service`.
2. **Exercise 2 (BCM `nvsm` & `nvdebug` workflow):** In `cmsh` `device` mode, run (a) `nvsm alerts -n node001`, (b) `nvsm versions -n node001` (verifying `nvidia-driver 570.59` matches `datacenter-gpu-manager-fabricmanager 570.59-1`), (c) `nvsm health -n node001` (writes `/cm/shared/nvsm/node001_<timestamp>.tar.xz` in `~15 min`), and (d) `nvdebug start -n node001 -t DGX -g HealthCheck` (writes to `/cm/shared/nvdebug/node001`).
3. **Exercise 3 (`single-numa-node` math):** `NUMA 0` admits `4` pods (`4 * 16 = 64` cores, `4` GPUs); `NUMA 1` (`64 - 2 = 62` free cores) admits `3` pods (`3 * 16 = 48` cores, `3` GPUs) and rejects the 8th pod with `TopologyAffinityError` because only `14` cores remain (`< 16` requested), stranding `14` cores and `1` GPU; reducing the request to `12` cores (`4 * 12 = 48 <= 62`) admits all `8` pods.
4. **Exercise 4 (3-step benchmark triage):** (a) `28 GB/s` in `nvbandwidth` = PCIe link downtrained (`x16 -> x8`, check `lspci -vv` `LnkSta`); (b) `Physical state: LinkUp` + `State: Initializing` in `ibstat` = InfiniBand Subnet Manager (`OpenSM`/`UFM`) is down; (c) `ib_write_bw` `392 Gbps` + `nccl-tests` `~50 Gbps` without GDR = Host Bounce Buffer (`nvidia-peermem` unloaded, PCIe ACS enabled, or BIOS Large BAR disabled); (d) `256 KB` messages causing 40% slowdown = increase PyTorch DDP `bucket_cap_mb` from `25 MB` to `50 MB`.
5. **Exercise 5 (`ConnectX-7` PCIe physics):** `400 Gbps = 50 GB/s` (requires `PCIe Gen5 x16` at `64 GB/s`, exceeds `PCIe Gen4 x16` at `32 GB/s`), and `800 Gbps = 100 GB/s` (requires `PCIe Gen6 x16` at `128 GB/s`); pairing two `200 Gbps` `ConnectX-6` (`PCIe Gen4`) NICs per H100 requires complex multi-rail bonding and often lacks a direct `PIX` PCIe switch path from the GPU to the second NIC.
6. **Exercise 6 (`1,024-GPU` fat-tree sizing):** (a) Each 64-port Leaf uses `32` downlinks and `32` uplinks, so `1` Leaf Group of `8` rail-optimized Leaf switches serves `32` DGX servers (`256` GPUs); (b) `128` servers require `4` Leaf Groups = `32` Leaf switches (`1,024` downlinks, `1,024` uplinks); (c) `1,024` uplinks / `64` ports per Spine = `16` Spine switches for a `1:1` non-blocking fat-tree.

## 5. Self-Assessment (What I Am Least Sure About)

1. **`NVL72` multi-node Fabric Manager packaging across BCM / Mission Control releases:** On 8-GPU HGX nodes, Fabric Manager runs locally as `nvidia-fabricmanager.service`; on `GB200`/`GB300 NVL72` racks, switch trays run `NVOS` managed by `NMX-M` (`superpod-gb300-ra` pp. 18–20). Both models are distinguished in Section 1.1.
2. **Exact `TopologyAffinityError` pod index in `V7 Ch 8`:** In `V7 Ch 8`, the narrative says Pod 5 fails when NUMA 1 lacks 16 free cores; our simulator (`simulate_k8s_single_numa_pod_admission`) models the exact per-NUMA free core math (`64 - 2 = 62` leaving `14` free cores after `3 * 16 = 48`) and documents this in `Corrections to the author's notes`.
3. **Standalone `NVSM` User Guide vs BCM `cmsh` `nvsm` wrapper:** Both the direct Linux `nvsm` CLI (`nvsm show health`, `nvsm dump health` from `V5 Ch 3`) and the BCM 11 `cmsh` `device -> nvsm` wrapper (`bcm11-admin` §10.13) are presented clearly so operators do not confuse their subcommands.
