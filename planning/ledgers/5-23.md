## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (The HPAv2 Algorithm, Metric APIs, and Behavior Tuning, `PK` Ch. 13):** `compute_hpa_desired_replicas()` computes $\lceil \text{current} \times (\text{currentMetric} / \text{targetMetric}) \rceil$ across all metrics (taking the max), ignores ratios within $\pm 10\%$ (`0.90..1.10`), caps scale-up steps via `scaleUp.policies` (`selectPolicy: Max`), and holds scale-down at the rolling-window maximum over `stabilizationWindowSeconds: 600`.
2. **Exercise 2 (Choosing Autoscaling Signals for LLM Inference and `prometheus-adapter`):** `DCGM_FI_DEV_GPU_UTIL` saturates near 100% even for 1 continuous-batching decode stream, so LLM inference scales on `vllm:num_requests_waiting` (queue depth), active concurrency, and `vllm:gpu_cache_usage_perc` exposed via `prometheus-adapter` or KEDA.
3. **Exercise 3 (Vertical Pod Autoscaler `VPA` and In-Place Pod Resize, `PK` Ch. 13):** VPA's `Recommender`, `Updater`, and `Admission Controller` compute and apply right-sized CPU/memory requests; `Off` mode (`Goldilocks`) provides safe recommendations without restarts, and VPA (`Auto`) must never share CPU/memory metrics with HPA.
4. **Exercise 4 (Event-Driven Autoscaling and Scale-to-Zero with `KEDA` vs. `Knative`/`KServe`):** KEDA's `keda-operator` handles `0 <-> 1` activation while its managed HPA handles `1 <-> N` (`ScaledObject` for Deployments, `ScaledJob` for long-running per-item GPU batch Jobs), whereas Knative/KServe adds an `activator` proxy to buffer HTTP requests in memory during `0 -> 1` cold starts.
5. **Exercise 5 (Node-Level Autoscaling: `Cluster Autoscaler` vs. `Karpenter` and Overprovisioning Pause Pods, `PK` Ch. 13):** `evaluate_ca_node_scale_down()` checks $<50\%$ utilization for 10m and flags `safe-to-evict=false`, unannotated `emptyDir`, `PDB=0`, and unmanaged pods; `simulate_overprovisioning_pause_pods()` shows negative-priority (`-10`) `pause` pods hold warm GPU nodes so burst inference pods schedule in `0s` while CA replenishes buffer nodes in the background.
6. **Exercise 6 (Atomic Multi-Node Gang Provisioning `ProvisioningRequest` + `Kueue` and Mitigating 70B Cold Starts):** `ProvisioningRequest` + Kueue `AdmissionCheck` guarantees all-or-nothing multi-node capacity before unsuspending training jobs, while pre-pulled images, local NVMe weight caches, `safetensors`/`tensorizer`, and KEDA `cron` pre-warming mitigate inference cold starts.
