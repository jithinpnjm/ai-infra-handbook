## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) On `N = 4` GPUs for the `7B` `FP32` model (`S_grad = 28.0 GB`), `DDP` Ring `AllReduce` moves **`42.0 GB` per GPU** (`2 * (3/4) * 28.0 GB`) whereas `nn.DataParallel` (`DP`) forces `GPU 0` to move **`168.0 GB`** (`2 * 3 * 28.0 GB`), a **`4.0x` higher traffic load on `GPU 0`**; (b) on `900.0 GB/s` `NVLink`, pure wire time is **`46.67 ms` (`~47 ms`)**, giving **`99.6%` theoretical scaling efficiency (`3.98x` speedup)** on the `12.3 s` baseline step, while the measured `12.9 s` (`31.2` vs `8.13 tokens/sec`) run achieves **`3.84x` (`~3.83x`) speedup** and **`95.9%` (`~95.7%`) scaling efficiency**.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) At `N = 8` GPUs on `900.0 GB/s` `NVLink`, Ring `AllReduce` moves **`49.0 GB` per GPU** (`2 * (7/8) * 28.0 GB`) in **`54.4 ms` (`~54 ms`)** across **`8` hops**; (b) at `N = 512` GPUs (`64` nodes) on `25.0 GB/s` `InfiniBand`, Ring `AllReduce` moves **`55.89 GB` (`~55.9 GB`) per GPU** in **`2.24 s` (`~2.2 s`)**, where `NCCL` switches from a **`512`-hop Ring** to a **Double Binary Tree / hierarchical algorithm (`ceil(log2(512)) = 9` hops)** to avoid `O(N)` per-hop latency and kernel-launch overhead.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) Under `bucket_cap_mb = 25.0 MB`, `DDP` packs parameters in reverse order (`layer 5 -> 0`) into **`3` buckets**: **Bucket 0** `("lm_head.weight", "layer4.weight")` = **`24.0 MB`**, **Bucket 1** `("layer3.weight", "layer2.weight")` = **`24.0 MB`**, and **Bucket 2** `("layer1.weight", "embed.weight")` = **`22.0 MB`**, placing `lm_head.weight` in Bucket 0 because its gradient finishes first during `loss.backward()`; (b) at `bucket_cap_mb = 50.0 MB`, `DDP` forms **`2` buckets** of **`48.0 MB`** and **`22.0 MB`**.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) With `4` buckets (`3.0 ms` compute, `2.5 ms` `AllReduce` each), sequential execution takes **`22.0 ms`** whereas two-stream overlapped execution finishes in **`14.5 ms`**, hiding **`7.5 ms` (`75.0%` overlap efficiency)** behind compute with only **`2.5 ms`** tail exposed (**`1.517x` speedup**); (b) overriding `DDP` to `1` giant bucket under `torch.compile` compiles **`1` subgraph (`0` graph breaks)** but delays `AllReduce` until all `12.0 ms` of backward compute finishes, lengthening total time to **`22.0 ms`** with **`0.0%` overlap efficiency** (`10.0 ms` exposed).
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) With `model.no_sync()`, `K = 4` accumulation cuts `AllReduce` calls from `4` to **`1`** (**`75.0%` saved**, **`168.0 GB -> 42.0 GB`/GPU**), and `K = 8` cuts `AllReduce` calls from `8` to **`1`** (**`87.5%` saved**, **`336.0 GB -> 42.0 GB`/GPU**); (b) setting `gradient_as_bucket_view=True` aliases `param.grad` into the `Reducer` buckets, reducing `7B` steady-state memory from **`140.0 GB` to `112.0 GB` (`28.0 GB` saved)** in `FP32` and from **`126.0 GB` to `112.0 GB` (`14.0 GB` saved)** in `BF16`/`FP32` mixed precision.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) For `D = 1003, N = 4`, `drop_last=True` yields **`250` samples/rank (`1,000` total, `3` dropped)** while `drop_last=False` yields **`251` samples/rank (`1,004` total, `1` padded)**; with `[2, 3]` uneven batches, `Rank 1` deadlocks at **step `3`** without `model.join()`, whereas `with model.join():` executes **`1` dummy `AllReduce`** on `Rank 0`; (b) the `24.0 ms` `MLPerf` step is **`43.8%` forward (`10.5 ms`), `37.5%` backward (`9.0 ms`) [`81.3%` compute], `16.7%` `AllReduce` (`4.0 ms`, `GOOD_OVERLAP < 20%`), and `2.1%` other (`0.5 ms`)**, while `Rank 0`'s `32.1 s` vs `12.2 s` step time is a **`+163.1%` skew (`> 10%` red flag)** caused by **`GPU 0` thermal throttling at `87 C` (`>= 80 C`)**.
