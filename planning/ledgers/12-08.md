## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) `16` VLs (`VL0..VL14` data, `VL15` reserved for unrouted `SMP`s), `1` credit = `64` B; (b) `65,536` B = `1,024` credits (`4,096` B MTU needs `64` credits): at `32,768` B occupied, `512` credits remain (`can_transmit = True`), whereas at `63,488` B occupied, `32` credits remain (`can_transmit = False`, stalled); (c) shared `VL0` inflates `p99` by `7.89x` (`1.8 -> 14.2 ms`), while separating onto `VL1` (`3.1 ms`) recovers `89.52%` of the regression.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Multiplying `4`-octet word deltas by `4` yields `21,474,836,480` B (`port 5`), `21,489,234,432` B (`port 6`), `1,073,741,824` B (`port 7`), and `1,081,654,272` B (`port 8`), a `20.01:1` max-to-min imbalance; (b) static `LFT` (`port 5`) and masked `SL 3` (`0xFFF7`) drive `port 5` to `580.0 Gbps` (`180.0 Gbps` excess), whereas `AR_group` (`0xFFFF`, `SL 0`) selects least-loaded `port 7` (`240.0 Gbps`, `380.0 Gbps` max uplink load, `0.0 Gbps` excess).
3. **Exercise 3:** Removing failed `port 6` leaves surviving `AR_group (5, 7, 8)`; hardware `SHIELD`/`FRN` reroutes in `10.0 µs` (`500,000` B in flight at `400G`) versus `500,000.0 µs` (`500 ms`) for an OpenSM `Trap 128` heavy sweep (`25,000,000,000` B in flight), a `50,000.0x` speedup.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Arrival stream `(100, 102, 103, 101, 104, 106, 105)` has `max_reorder_distance = 2` and delivers `(100..106)` in order with `0` `NAK`s when HCA OOO reordering (`window = 64`) is enabled versus `3` `PSN`-gap `NAK`s when disabled; (b) despite `p50` improving `3.1 -> 2.4 µs`, `p99` inflated `5.36x` (`7.2 -> 38.6 µs`) and `max` inflated `12.35x` (`9.1 -> 112.4 µs`), stalling synchronized barriers.
5. **Exercise 5:** (i) `25%` (`CCTI = 0`): `FECN=False, BECN=False, new_ccti=0, mult=1.0x, rate=400.0 Gbps`; (ii) `65%` (`0 -> 16`): `FECN=True, BECN=True, mult=1.5x, rate=266.67 Gbps`; (iii) `75%` (`16 -> 32`): `FECN=True, BECN=True, mult=2.0x, rate=200.0 Gbps`; (iv) `90%` (`120 -> 127` clamped): `FECN=True, BECN=True, mult=4.9688x, rate=80.5 Gbps`; (v) `20%` on timer expiry (`1 -> 0`): `FECN=False, BECN=False, mult=1.0x, rate=400.0 Gbps`.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) `port 3` (`PortXmitWait=998,432`, `0` errors) is `PURE_FABRIC_CONGESTION`, `port 4` (`SymbolErrorCounter=41`, `LinkDownedCounter=2`) is `PHYSICAL_LINK_FAULT`, and deltas `{1:340, 2:298, 3:51204, 4:47890, 5:312}` isolate root ports `(3, 4)`; (b) `port 12` accelerates `45.5x` (`295.33 -> 13,436.67` errors/day, active degradation) whereas `port 9` (`12,034 -> 12,034`) has `0` delta (historical scar tissue); `2:1` leaf oversubscription (`6,400 / 3,200 Gbps`) caps per-node bandwidth at `200.0 Gbps` and doubles a `2 GB` `AllReduce` step from `40.0 ms` to `80.0 ms` (`397.62` vs `201.18 Gbps` `ib_write_bw`).
