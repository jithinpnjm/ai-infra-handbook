## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Offset `10,486,272` maps to `chunk_index = 10`, `stripe_row = 1`, `target_slot = 2`, `TargetID = 2001`, and `intra_chunk_offset = 512` bytes; (b) `2` targets = `1.6 GB/s` (`312.5 s`), `8` targets = `6.4 GB/s` theoretical (`78.125 s`, or `5.0 GB/s` / `100.0 s` sustained at `78.125%` efficiency, saving **`234.375 s`** theoretical / **`212.5 s`** sustained per checkpoint), and `12` targets = `9.6 GB/s` (`52.0833 s`).
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Peak metadata rate is `256 x 800 = 204,800` ops/sec, requiring at least **`5` `MDS` nodes** (`5 x 50,000 = 250,000` ops/sec capacity) for raw files, whereas `100x` `WebDataset` sharding lowers demand to **`2,048` ops/sec** (easily served by `1` `MDS` at `< 5 ms`); (b) mean fill is **`58.0%`**, max spread is **`28.0%`** (`72.0% - 44.0%`, outside `±5%` band and `> 10%` threshold), and overloaded targets **`[2001, 2002]`** on `storage2` require `beegfs-ctl --migrate`.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) Raw NVMe is **`983.04 TB`**, `6+2` usable Tier 1 NVMe is **`737.28 TB` (`75.0%`)** vs **`327.68 TB` (`33.33%`)** under `3x` replication (a **`2.25x`** usable-capacity gain), and total unified namespace with `2,000 TB` Tier 2 S3 is **`2,737.28 TB`**; (b) `16+2` net erasure efficiency is **`88.89%`** (`16/18`), and pinning `4` of `128` host cores consumes **`3.125%`** CPU.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) VAST `146+4` wide stripe has **`2.67%` parity overhead** (`4/150`), **`97.33%` net efficiency** (`146/150`), **`1,460.0 TB`** physical usable `QLC` capacity, and **`2,920.0 TB`** effective logical capacity at `2.0x` data reduction; (b) `gdsio` `-x 0` achieves a **`+20.0%` throughput uplift** (`8.0 -> 9.6 GB/s`) and **`-20.0%` latency reduction** (`1.25 -> 1.00 ms`), while `H100` sees **`30%+` uplift** due to higher `200/400 Gb/s` NIC bandwidth and heavier host CPU bounce-buffer contention.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) DeepSeek's `68`-node `3FS` cluster has **`680` NVMe SSDs** (`10,880 TB` raw), delivers **`97.06 GB/s`** per storage node at `6.6 TB/s` read, reaches **`8.0 TB/s`** combined I/O (`6.6 + 1.4 TB/s` background), and outperforms Ceph (`1.1 TB/s`) by **`6.0x`** at `6.6 TB/s` and **`6.64x`** at `7.3 TB/s` peak; (b) random LLM reads thrash the OS page cache with near-zero hit rates, and standard user-space `FUSE` lacks kernel `nvidia-fs` `O_DIRECT` DMA integration required for `GDS`.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) Serial fetching of `2,000,000` objects at `150 ms` wastes **`300,000 s` (`83.33 hours`)**, whereas prefetching `20 GB` (`2 x 10 GB` shards) across `8` workers (`1.2 GB/s`) takes `16.67 s + 10.2 s =` **`26.87 s` (`~27 s`)** (`8.33 s + 10.2 s =` **`18.53 s`** with `16` workers at `2.4 GB/s`); (b) `16` S3 prefixes support **`88,000` `GET`/s** and **`56,000` `PUT`/s**, `8 x 1 GB` parallel download in `7.5 s` (`1,066.67 MB/s` agg, `133.33 MB/s`/worker) gives a **`6.61x` speedup** over `49.6 s` serial, Epoch 1 -> 2 reduces download wait by **`21.57x`** (`45.3 -> 2.1 min`), and Epoch 3 wait rises to `3.5 min` (`97.5%` hit rate) because cache fill (`88.0%`) exceeds `85.0%` and triggers LRU eviction.
