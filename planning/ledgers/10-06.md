| Book & section | Disposition |
|---|---|
| `ASP Ch 2` (pp. 20–42): Four hardware interconnect scales: (1) **`NV-HBI` (High-Bandwidth Interface)** `10 TB/s` on-package die-to-die link bonding the two reticle-limited dies (`104B` transistors + `96 GB` HBM3e each = `208B` transistors + `192 GB` raw / `180 GB` usable HBM3e + `126 MB` L2 cache) inside one Blackwell GPU; (2) **`NVLink-C2C` (Chip-to-Chip)** `~900 GB/s` (`450 GB/s` per direction) cache-coherent link between Grace CPU (`~480 GB` LPDDR5X @ `~500 GB/s`, `>100 MB` L3) and Hopper/Blackwell GPUs creating **Unified CPU-GPU Memory / Extended GPU Memory (`EGM`)**, contrasted against `PCIe Gen5 x16` (`64 GB/s`/dir) and `PCIe Gen6 x16` (`128 GB/s`/dir on Blackwell Ultra `B300`), plus why LPDDR5X over C2C has `~10x` lower bandwidth than HBM3e (`8 TB/s`) and requires explicit asynchronous prefetch/staging; (3) **`NVLink 5` & `NVSwitch`** (`18` links × `100 GB/s` = `1.8 TB/s` bidirectional per GPU, `1–2 μs` small-message latency vs `5–10+ μs` on IB, `18` NVSwitches across `9` switch trays in NVL72 = `130 TB/s` (`129.6 TB/s`) 1-hop crossbar); (4) **Multi-GPU memory consistency rule** (**only the CPU–GPU path over `NVLink-C2C` is hardware cache-coherent; GPU-to-GPU caches over NVLink are NOT globally hardware cache-coherent**, requiring `NCCL` or **`NVSHMEM`** PGAS synchronization); (5) **`NVLink SHARP` (`NVLS`)** in-switch reduction engines inside NVSwitch ASICs vs InfiniBand **`SHARP` (`CollNet`)**; (6) **GPUDirect RDMA** (`nvidia-peermem`) and **Co-Packaged Optics (`CPO`)** (`800 Gb/s` and `1.6 Tb/s` silicon photonics on Quantum-X800 / Spectrum-X800) | `used in sections 1, 2, 3, 4` and `gpu_interconnects.py` |
