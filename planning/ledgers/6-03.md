## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Amdahl's Law vs. Gustafson's Law):** On `n = 1,024` GPUs, `p = 0.99` gives Amdahl strong-scaling speedup `91.2x` (`8.9%` efficiency, ceiling `100x`) and Gustafson scaled speedup `1013.77x`; `p = 0.999` gives Amdahl speedup `506.2x` (`49.4%` efficiency, ceiling `1000x`) and Gustafson scaled speedup `1022.98x`.
2. **Exercise 2 (Karp-Flatt Overhead Diagnosis):** Using `karp_flatt()`, `e` rises from `0.083` (`n=2`) to `0.135` (`n=4`) to `0.196` (`n=8`), proving that parallel IPC/spawn overhead, memory-bandwidth contention, and P-core/E-core load imbalance are growing with worker count rather than a fixed serial section.
3. **Exercise 3 (Hockney Communication Model and Ring `AllReduce` Volume):** At `alpha = 2.0 us` and `beta = 50.0 GB/s`, `hockney_message_time_us()` gives a crossover size `m = alpha * beta = 100,000` bytes (`100 KB`, `4.0 us` total with `50%` latency share), and `ring_allreduce_volume_per_rank(1_000_000_000, 8)` gives `2 * (7/8) * 1 GB = 1.75 GB` sent per GPU.
4. **Exercise 4 (Parallel Programming Models: `MPI`, `OpenMP`, `OpenSHMEM`, `CUDA`, `OpenCL`, `OpenACC`):** The 7 MPI calls initialize MPI (`MPI_Init`), query size/rank/hostname (`MPI_Comm_size`, `MPI_Comm_rank`, `MPI_Get_processor_name`), broadcast `n` (`MPI_Bcast`), sum partial integrals (`MPI_Reduce`), and finalize (`MPI_Finalize`); `OpenSHMEM` (`oshc`, `oshfort`, `oshrun`, `SHMEM_SYMMETRIC_HEAP_SIZE`, `oshconfig`) uses explicit one-sided `PGAS` remote accesses unlike failed distributed `OpenMP` (`Intel Cluster OpenMP`, `ScaleMP vSMP`); and `CUDA` optimizes for NVIDIA GPUs while `OpenCL` restricts C99 for cross-vendor portability and `OpenACC` uses OpenMP-compatible directives.
5. **Exercise 5 (Numerical Libraries: `ScaLAPACK`, `PETSc`, `FFTW`, `Intel TBB`):** `PDGETRF` is parallel double-precision general LU factorization, `PSPOTRS` is parallel single-precision symmetric positive-definite factored solve, and `PZHEEVX` is parallel double-complex Hermitian expert eigensolver; `PBLAS` and `BLACS` sit above single-node `BLAS`/`LAPACK` and `MPI` in `SLmake.inc`; `PETSc` provides `-log_summary`, `-fp_trap`, `-trdump`, `-trmalloc`, and `-on_error_attach_debugger`; and `FFTW` v2 and v3 have incompatible APIs (while `MKL`/`ACML` include built-in FFTW wrappers).
6. **Exercise 6 (Parallel Debuggers and `PMPI` Tracers):** `TotalView` provides reverse debugging and `ThreadSpotter` while Arm `DDT` provides `ddt --offline` batch reports; and the `PMPI` wrapper interface intercepts `MPI_*` calls without source changes to record `CLOG`/`SLOG2` (`MPE`/`JumpShot`), `OTF`/`OTF2` (`Vampir`/`VampirTrace`), and `STF` (`Intel ITAC`) traces.
