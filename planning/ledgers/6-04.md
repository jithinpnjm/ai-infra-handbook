## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Open MPI 5-Level MCA Precedence and `HCOLL`/`UCX` Controls):** `resolve_openmpi_mca_param()` orders `/etc/openmpi-mca-params.conf` (1) < `$HOME/.openmpi/mca-params.conf` (2) < `--tune` files (3) < `OMPI_MCA_` env vars (4) < `-mca` CLI flags (5); `export OMPI_MCA_coll_hcoll_enable=0` disables `HCOLL` under `srun --mpi=pmix`, `-mca pml ucx -x UCX_NET_DEVICES=mlx5_0:1 -x HCOLL_MAIN_IB=mlx5_0:1 -x HCOLL_ENABLE_MCAST_ALL=1 -x HCOLL_ENABLE_SHARP=1` steers `UCX`/`HCOLL`, and `ucx_info -c -f` documents all `UCX` variables.
2. **Exercise 2 (`NCCL_SOCKET_IFNAME` and `NCCL_IB_HCA` Prefix Operators):** On `["lo", "docker0", "eth0", "eth1", "eth11", "bond0"]`, `match_nccl_ifname()` returns `["eth0", "eth1", "eth11"]` for `"eth"`, `["eth1"]` for `"=eth1"`, `["lo", "eth0", "eth1", "eth11", "bond0"]` for `"^docker"`, and `["eth1", "eth11", "bond0"]` for `"^docker,^=lo,^=eth0"`, while `NCCL_IB_GID_INDEX` should remain unset (`AUTO` in `2.21+`/`2.32u1+`).
3. **Exercise 3 (Eager vs. Rendezvous Deadlocks and MPI Thread Levels):** Small messages (`1 KB`) return immediately via internal eager bounce buffers whereas large messages (`10 MB`) block in `MPI_Send` until a matching `MPI_Recv` is posted under rendezvous; fix with `MPI_Sendrecv`, ordered send/recv, or `MPI_Isend`/`MPI_Irecv`, and use `MPI_THREAD_FUNNELED` when only the main thread calls MPI.
4. **Exercise 4 (Algorithmic vs. Bus Bandwidth and GPUDirect RDMA):** `compute_allreduce_algbw_and_busbw(1_000_000_000, 0.005, 8)` gives `algbw = 200.0 GB/s` and `busbw = 200.0 * (14/8) = 350.0 GB/s`; missing `nvidia_peermem`, enabled PCIe `ACS` (`SYS` vs `PIX`), or PCIe Gen 4 downtraining (`~31.5 GB/s` vs Gen 5 `~63 GB/s` for a `50 GB/s` 400G NIC) throttles GPUDirect RDMA.
5. **Exercise 5 (Launching at Scale and NUMA Binding):** `srun --mpi=pmix --kill-on-bad-exit=1` wires ranks via `slurmstepd` PMIx without SSH and aborts cleanly on single-rank failure; `mpirun` requires `-x` env export and `--bind-to none` for multi-threaded dataloaders; and `mpirun --report-bindings`, `numactl --show`, and `taskset -cp <PID>` verify socket/core affinity.
6. **Exercise 6 (The 4-Layer Hang & Fabric Debugging Playbook):** Check (1) launch count, `ulimit -l` (`memlock unlimited`), and exit codes `137`/`139`/`143`; (2) `srun --mpi=list` and `NCCL_SOCKET_IFNAME`; (3) `NCCL_DEBUG_SUBSYS=INIT,GRAPH,ENV,NET`, `NCCL_RUN_DIAGNOSTICS=1`, `ncclras` (`localhost:28028`), and `NVRM: Xid 79`/`119`; and (4) `ibstat`/`perfquery -x`/`ibdiagnet` or Cumulus RoCE `DSCP 26` (prio `3` PFC) and `DSCP 48` (prio `6` CNP).
