### B. *AI Data Center Network Design and Technologies* Chapters 2 & 3 (`aidc-ch2`, `aidc-ch3`)
| Item | Disposition |
|---|---|
| `aidc-ch2`: RoCEv2 UDP destination port `4791` encapsulating the InfiniBand Base Transport Header (`BTH`); low entropy and elephant flows (simultaneous and sequential flowlets); proactive load balancing hierarchy: Static Load Balancing (`SLB`, 5-tuple hash) -> Dynamic Load Balancing (`DLB`, local link/queue utilization) -> Global Load Balancing (`GLB`, Next-to-Next-Hop `NNH` downstream awareness) -> Selective DLB (`sDLB`, BTH opcode matching for elephant-flow packet spraying); reactive congestion control via **DCQCN** (`ECN` bits `11` marked by congested switch -> destination returns Congestion Notification Packet `CNP` with IP ECN `01` -> source NIC throttles rate, backed up by link-level `PFC` pause frames) | `used in section 3`, `Commands and configurations that matter`, `How it fails`, and `superpod_networks.py` |
| `aidc-ch3`: Rail-Optimized Design (`ROD`, 1:1 GPU-to-rail-leaf, 1-hop intra-rail vs 2-hop inter-rail via spine) vs Rail-Only Design (no inter-rail spine connections; cross-rail traffic uses internal NVSwitch) vs Rail-Unified Design (`RUD`, all 8 NICs of a server to 1–2 local leaves; simpler DAC cabling but single-leaf failure isolates entire server); Leaf Switch Physical Placement comparison (`Table 3-1`): **Top-of-Rack (`ToR`, max cable span 8 racks)** vs **Middle-of-Row (`MoR`, max cable span 6 racks, offloads switch power/cooling from server racks)** vs **End-of-Row (`EoR`, max cable span 10 racks)**; 3-stage Clos max scale (`2,048 GPUs` with `64 × 400G` switches) vs 5-stage Clos (`1,024-GPU` blocks/bricks with Super-Spines at `1:1` to `7:1` oversubscription) vs Scheduled Fabric (`VOQ` cell spraying, `64/128/256-byte` cells) and Dragonfly | `used in section 2` and `superpod_networks.py` |
