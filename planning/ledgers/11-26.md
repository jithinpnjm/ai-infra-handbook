## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) **vGPU** (`Virtual device assigned to a guest VM`, billed by `Assigned vGPU/VM-hour`), (b) **MIG** (`Supported hardware GPU instance`, billed by `Allocated profile-hour`), (c) **Time-slicing** (`Software-advertised logical replica`, billed by `Access-hour or queue class`), (d) **CUDA MPS** (`Shared single CUDA context in EXCLUSIVE_PROCESS mode`, billed by `Access-hour`), and (e) **Dedicated GPU** (`Whole physical GPU device`, billed by `Reserved GPU-hour`).
2. **Exercise 2:** `nvidia-smi -i 0 --query-gpu=mig.mode.current` = `Enabled` (`nvidia-smi mig -lgi`) indicates **MIG**; active `nvidia-smi vgpu` instances indicate **vGPU**; `1` non-MIG physical GPU with `4` or `8` allocatable GPUs and multiple PIDs in `nvidia-smi --query-processes` sharing the same pod `GPU-...` UUID indicates **Time-slicing**; and `1` physical GPU with `1` allocatable GPU and `1` PID indicates **Dedicated GPU**.
3. **Exercise 3:** The `5` layers are `Physical inventory -> Configured inventory -> Allocatable inventory -> Service capacity -> Sellable capacity`; a `48%` fleet utilization average can have `0` sellable `3g.40gb` capacity when free slices sit in `1g.10gb` pools, nodes are draining for maintenance, or remaining headroom is held to protect `p99` latency SLOs.
4. **Exercise 4:** Each node provides `5 × (1 - 0.20) = 4.0` effective sellable allocations, requiring `N = ceil((70 + 6) / 4.0) = 19` nodes; the `5` named reserves in `H = 0.20` are `Node-failure reserve`, `Maintenance reserve`, `Rollout reserve`, `Demand-variance reserve`, and `Fragmentation reserve`.
5. **Exercise 5:** Billing by sampled `DCGM_FI_DEV_GPU_UTIL` `%` fails to recover costs when a tenant reserves `3g.40gb` (`50%` of the GPU) and sits at `0%` compute utilization while blocking others; bill `Interactive sandbox` by `Access-hour`, `Protected inference` by `Allocated profile-hour`, `Large job` by `Reserved GPU-hour`, and `Regulated VM` by `Assigned vGPU/VM-hour`.
6. **Exercise 6:** On an `80 GB` time-sliced GPU, a workload can burst to `14 GB` using idle neighbor VRAM, which immediately OOMs on a hard `10 GB` (`1g.10gb`) MIG partition; Lab 03 holds image, artifact, input, precision, concurrency, and node constant across `whole`, `mig`, and `timeslice` trials (`SHARING_RUNTIME_PROBE_COMPLETE` & `SHARING_NEIGHBOR_PROBE_COMPLETE`).
