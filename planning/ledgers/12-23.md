## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Worst-case downlink demand is `16 x 800 = 12,800 Gb/s`; normal uplink capacity is `8 x 400 = 3,200 Gb/s` (`4.00:1`), and `N-1` (`7` uplinks) is `2,800 Gb/s` (`4.57:1`); with `50%` rack locality (`6,400 Gb/s` cross-cut), ratios drop to `2.00:1` normal (`400 Gb/s`/node) and `2.29:1` `N-1`; (b) `16 x 400 = 6,400 Gb/s` requires a `32`-port leaf (`16` down + `16` up) for `1:1` (`2 GB` `AllReduce` in `40.0 ms` at `50 GB/s`), whereas a `24`-port leaf (`8` uplinks = `3,200 Gb/s`, `2:1`) halves node bandwidth to `25 GB/s` (`80.0 ms`) and losing `1` of `2` spines (`4` uplinks = `1,600 Gb/s`, `4:1`, `12.5 GB/s`) quadruples it to `160.0 ms`.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) 2-tier `1:1` (`64`-port, `5%` spares): `1,024` GPUs needs `32`L + `16`S = `48` active (`51` w/ spares) switches, `2,048` active (`2,151` w/ spares) cables, and `4,096` active (`4,301` w/ spares) optics; `2,048` GPUs needs `64`L + `32`S = `96` active (`101` w/ spares) switches, `4,096` active (`4,301` w/ spares) cables, and `8,192` active (`8,602` w/ spares) optics; (b) 3-tier `4,096` GPUs at `1:1` needs `128`L + `128`S + `64`SS = `320` active (`336` w/ spares) switches, `12,288` active (`12,903` w/ spares) cables, and `24,576` active (`25,805` w/ spares) optics (at `2:1` super-spine: `32`SS, `288`/`303` switches, `10,240`/`10,752` cables, `20,480`/`21,504` optics); `8,192` GPUs `1:1` needs `256`L + `256`S + `128`SS = `640` active (`672` w/ spares) switches, `24,576` active (`25,805` w/ spares) cables, and `49,152` active (`51,610` w/ spares) optics.
