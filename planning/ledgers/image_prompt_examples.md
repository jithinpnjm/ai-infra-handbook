concept: Each process has its own page table, one MMU translates every access in hardware, an unmapped access raises a page fault.

Bottom strip with three small icons: Isolation, Sharing, Protection. ## Lessons from generation (what makes Gemini behave)
- Always end with TEXT RULES and the exact label list. Every label appears once unless the prompt says otherwise.
- 10 to 16 labels works. Over about 18 the model starts duplicating or misspelling.
- Describe layout concretely (left to right, two lanes, strip along the bottom). Describe what must NOT touch (for example "the orange arrow does not touch the file") when a wrong picture would mislead.
- Do not reuse the same words in the scene text and the label list in a way that invites a second copy.
- Titles of 2 to 6 words. Numbered steps use the chapter's own wording.
