| The **Five Network Segments across Four Physical Fabrics** (`superpod-gb300-ra` p. 18): Segments = (1) NVLink 5, (2) Compute Network, (3) Storage Network, (4) In-Band Management Network, (5) Out-of-Band Management Network; Physical Fabrics = (1) Multi-Node NVLink (`MN-NVL`, managed by `NMX-M`), (2) Compute InfiniBand Fabric (`Quantum-2 QM9700` 64x400G NDR or `Quantum-X800 Q3400-RD` 144x800G XDR, managed by `UFM`), (3) Storage and In-Band Ethernet Fabric (`Spectrum-4 SN5600D` 64x800G / 128x400G), (4) Out-of-Band Ethernet Fabric (`SN2201` 48x1G RJ45) | `used in section 3` and `superpod_concepts.py` (detailed port/cable maps in `9.9`) |
| Exact multi-SU switch & cable scaling tables: (a) `superpod-b200-dc` p. 19 (1 to 4 SUs: leaves `8, 16, 24, 32`; spines `4, 8, 16, 16`; compute+UFM cables `252, 508, 764, 1,020`; spine-leaf cables `256, 512, 768, 1,024`); (b) `superpod-gb300-ra` Table 3 p. 21 & Table 4 p. 23 (2, 4, 8, 16 SUs = `1,152, 2,304, 4,608, 9,216` GPUs; IB leaves `8/SU` = `16, 32, 64, 128`; IB spines `8, 18, 36, 72`; Ethernet spines `4, 8, 12, 24`; Ethernet compute leaves `8, 16, 32, 64` at 1:3 blocking; storage leaves `2, 2, 4, 8` non-blocking; management leaves + OOB spines `2+2`) | `used in section 3` and `superpod_concepts.py` |
