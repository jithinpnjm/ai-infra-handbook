## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Overlay Encapsulation vs. Native Routing, `PK` Ch. 5 & `NK` Ch. 4–5):** VXLAN encapsulates inner Pod frames inside outer Node-to-Node UDP `4789` packets (50B overhead), Geneve (`UDP 6081`) adds extensible TLV metadata, and IPIP (`proto 4`, 20B) encapsulates L3-only; `select_calico_cross_subnet_mode()` routes intra-subnet traffic natively and encapsulates only across L3 router boundaries.
2. **Exercise 2 (Routed BGP Designs with Calico):** Calico combines `Felix` (kernel routes/policy), `BIRD` (BGP), and `Typha` (watch fan-out) with `/26` `IPPool` block aggregation; `compute_bgp_session_counts()` shows 500 nodes require 124,750 sessions in full-mesh iBGP versus 1,494 with 3 Route Reflectors or 1,000 with dual-ToR eBGP peering.
3. **Exercise 3 (Cilium's eBPF Datapath: Security Identities, BPF Maps, and `netkit`):** `compare_policy_scaling_ip_vs_identity()` shows label-derived numeric Security Identities compress $1,000 \times 500 = 500,000$ IP-pair combinations into 2 `O(1)` BPF map entries unaffected by Pod IP churn, while `bpf.hostRouting=true` and `netkit` bypass host `iptables` and softirq overhead.
4. **Exercise 4 (Cilium `kubeProxyReplacement`, `DSR`, Maglev, and BGP Control Plane):** Socket-LB translates `ClusterIP` at `connect()`, `loadBalancer.mode=dsr` lets backend nodes reply directly to external clients without triangle-routing through the ingress node, and `loadBalancer.algorithm=maglev` provides consistent hashing across ECMP nodes.
5. **Exercise 5 (Selecting and Hardening the Primary CNI on Multi-Homed GPU Clusters):** `select_primary_cni_node_interface()` pins Calico (`IP_AUTODETECTION_METHOD=interface=eth0`) and Cilium (`--set devices=eth0`) to the management NIC so RDMA interfaces (`ens1f*`, `ib*`) remain untouched for Multus/SR-IOV/NCCL.
6. **Exercise 6 (Benchmarking, Observability `Hubble`/`Tetragon`, and Safe CNI Migration):** Benchmark throughput, `TCP_RR` latency, and CPU/Gbps with `tx-udp_tnl-segmentation` verified; monitor flows/drops with `hubble observe --verdict DROPPED`; and migrate CNIs via blue-green clusters or rolling per-node dual-overlay drains.
