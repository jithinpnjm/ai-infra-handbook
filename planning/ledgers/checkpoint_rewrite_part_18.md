## Unresolved

- 07 #16 (boundary day): the chapter says an object can be deleted once its age exceeds the retention period. 30 January is 90 days after 1 November, so the first day the age strictly exceeds 90 days is 31 January. The new answer keeps 30 January as the old answer did and tells the reader to test the boundary. The owner should confirm which day the rule means before the answer is used as a fact.
- 07 #20 and 04 #19: the ordered steps are judgement, not stated in the chapter body. The old answer for 07 #20 also named a source address, which is not in the chapter body, so the new answer drops it. The same applies to the phrase 'signers must watch' in old 07 #19, which is also dropped.
- 02 #17 and 07 #16: derived numbers (270, 30 January) were already in the old answers. They are arithmetic on chapter numbers (300 nodes and 10 percent; 1 November and 90 days), not new facts.
