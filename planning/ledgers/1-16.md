## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Syslog Facilities, Severities `0`..`7`, and `rsyslog` Selectors):** (a) The 8 severities from `0` (highest) to `7` (lowest) are `emerg` (`0`), `alert` (`1`), `crit` (`2`), `err` (`3`), `warning` (`4`), `notice` (`5`), `info` (`6`), and `debug` (`7`); `mail.err` matches `err` (`3`), `crit` (`2`), `alert` (`1`), and `emerg` (`0`), whereas `local3.=info` matches only `info` (`6`) and `auth,authpriv.none` excludes `auth`/`authpriv`; (b) `@@logs.example.com:514` uses TCP (reliable, supports TLS and disk-assisted queues `queue.type="LinkedList"`), whereas `@` uses unacknowledged UDP.
2. **Exercise 2 (`logrotate` Open-Inode Behavior, `SIGHUP`, and `copytruncate`):** When `logrotate` renames `app.log` to `app.log.1` and creates a new `app.log`, a daemon holding an open file descriptor continues writing to the old inode (`app.log.1_old_inode`) until `postrotate` sends `SIGHUP` to make the daemon reopen `app.log` (or `copytruncate` copies `app.log` to `app.log.1` and truncates `app.log` in place, at the risk of losing lines written during the copy window).
3. **Exercise 3 (`chrony` `makestep 1.0 3`, Slew vs. Step, and Munge/Kerberos `300 s` Skew):** (a) With `makestep 1.0 3`, an initial `90 s` offset on poll update `1` (`<= 3`) is stepped immediately (`step_clock_immediately`), whereas a `2.5 s` offset on update `10` (`> 3`) is slewed gradually (`slew_clock_gradually`) so running training jobs and timers never see the clock jump backward; (b) a `400 s` clock skew between login and compute nodes exceeds the `300 s` (`5`-minute) Munge and Kerberos TTL, causing Slurm jobs to fail with `Invalid credential` / `Credential expired`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Cluster NTP Hierarchy, `local stratum 10 orphan`, and `Reach 377`):** Compute nodes point only at the cluster head/management node (`server head.cluster iburst`), while the head node syncs with external upstream servers and sets `allow 10.0.0.0/16` and `local stratum 10 orphan` so the cluster stays internally synchronized even if external internet connectivity drops; in `chronyc sources`, `Reach 377` (octal `0o377 = 11111111` binary) means the last `8` consecutive polls succeeded (`Reach 0` means 0 responses, e.g., UDP port `123` blocked).
5. **Exercise 5 (Cron Syntax, the Day-of-Month OR Day-of-Week Trap, and Safe Fleet Wrappers):** (a) In `0 3 15 * 1`, because both day-of-month (`15`) and day-of-week (`1` = Monday) are restricted (not `*`), POSIX `cron` runs the job whenever **either** condition is true (the 15th of the month **or** any Monday); (b) wrap fleet cron jobs with `flock -n /run/lock/job.lock`, `timeout 120`, a randomized jitter (`sleep $((RANDOM % 300))`), explicit `PATH=`, and `\%` escaping inside `date +\%F`.
6. **Exercise 6 (`cron` vs. `systemd` Timers and `at` / `systemd-run` One-Offs):** `systemd` timers run each job in its own `cgroup` with `journalctl -u` logging, `After=` dependency ordering, `RandomizedDelaySec=`, and `Persistent=true` catch-up (like `anacron`); for a one-off job in 30 minutes, `sudo systemd-run --on-active=30m --unit=late-drain /usr/local/bin/drain-node.sh` creates a transient timer and service unit tracked by `systemd`.
