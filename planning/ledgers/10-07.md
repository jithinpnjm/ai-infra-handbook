## 6. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Gaming Card Defense):** Four technical showstoppers (no GPUDirect RDMA forces CPU bounce buffer, no NVLink strangles TP, lack of ECC risks NaN divergence from cosmic-ray bit flips, active fans disrupt linear front-to-back chassis airflow) plus legal clause 1.3 of GeForce Driver EULA ("No Datacenter Deployment").
2. **Exercise 2 (LLM Inference Sizing Math):** Weight memory = 26.0 GB; KV cache per user = 40 layers * 40 heads * 2 bytes * 4096 tokens * 2 (K+V) = 26,843,545 B ≈ 0.0268 GB/user; 32 users = 0.859 GB; total VRAM with 1.2x overhead = (26.0 + 0.86) * 1.2 = 32.23 GB, which fits easily on a single 48 GB L40S card.
3. **Exercise 3 (PCIe vs. SXM Fine-Tuning TCO):** An 85%+ communication tax means 10 engineers ($50,000/week) waiting 5 times longer (e.g. 5 weeks instead of 1 week, costing an extra $200,000 per training run) wipes out the $900,000 savings in 4.5 training cycles (or 18 weeks of delay).
4. **Exercise 4 (The H200 Concurrency Advantage):** Upgrading from 8x H100 (640 GB) to 8x H200 (1,128 GB) adds 488 GB of usable VRAM; at 1.34 GB per user session, the server supports 488 / 1.34 ≈ 364 additional concurrent user sessions before running out of memory.
5. **Exercise 5 (The Missing NVLink Trap):** Pipeline parallelism exchanges only boundary activations between sequential stages over PCIe (low bandwidth demand), whereas Tensor Parallelism requires an `AllReduce` on every layer across all 4 GPUs; because L40S lacks NVLink, TP saturates the 64 GB/s PCIe bus.
6. **Exercise 6 (Custom Scorecard Design):** GH200 satisfies G1 (datacenter compliant), G2 (576 GB unified memory > 400 GB table), G3 (1 kW node < 3 kW ceiling), G4 (ECC/BMC), and G5 (900 GB/s NVLink-C2C handles EGM query bandwidth), winning over 4x H100 (only 320 GB VRAM, fails G2) and 8x L40S (fails G5 due to lack of NVLink/C2C memory pooling).

## 7. Self-Assessment (What I Am Least Sure About)

1. **Optical reticle limit:** The exact physical optical reticle limit is conventionally cited as ~858 mm² for standard DUV/EUV scanners; vendor sources vary between 850 mm² and 858 mm² depending on mask edge margins.
2. **L40S pricing and availability during 2023 shortages:** The economic trade-off of L40S as a stop-gap during H100 shortages is documented in Volume 04 Chapter 5, but exact enterprise street prices fluctuated widely during that window.
3. **Blackwell B300 memory capacity:** Quoted as 288 GB HBM3e in *AI Systems Performance Engineering* Chapter 2 (p. 38) for Blackwell Ultra, while early announcements also referenced 216 GB configurations. Both represent Blackwell Ultra expansions.
