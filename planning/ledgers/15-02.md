## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) For $h = 4,096$ in FP16, prefill arithmetic intensity is **`62.06 FLOPS/B`** ($s = 64$, memory-bound), **`409.60 FLOPS/B`** ($s = 512$, just below the **`419 FLOPS/B`** `L40S` ridge point), and **`1,365.33 FLOPS/B`** ($s = 4,096$, compute-bound), whereas single-request decode ($s = 1$) is **`1.00 FLOPS/B`**; (b) a `7B` model at `100 tokens/s` moves **`1,400 GB/s` (`70.0%` MBU)** in FP16 (`2` B/param) and **`700 GB/s` (`35.0%` MBU)** in 8-bit (`1` B/param) on a `2,000 GB/s` `A100`.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) The `B = 32` request-level batch has **`2,310` valid tokens** and **`64,000` padded tokens**, yielding a **`96.39%` Padding Waste Ratio** and trapping the `31` short requests for **`1,990` extra decode iterations**; (b) with `45` active decode streams and $C = 512$, each iteration processes **`557` tokens** (`<= 2,048` budget) and completes the `12,288`-token prompt across **`24` iteration steps**.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) On an 80-layer 70B model ($H_q = 64, D_{\text{head}} = 128, P = 2$), per-token KV Cache is **`2,621,440 B` (`2.5 MiB`)** under MHA (`10.0 GiB` at `4K`, `312.5 GiB` at `128K`), **`327,680 B` (`320 KiB`, `8x` smaller)** under GQA (`1.25 GiB` at `4K`, `39.06 GiB` at `128K`), and **`40,960 B` (`40 KiB`, `64x` smaller)** under MQA; (b) 61-layer DeepSeek MLA caches **`576` elements/layer** ($512 + 64$), equivalent to **`2.25` GQA groups**, consuming **`70,272 B/token` (`68.625 KiB`) in BF16** and **`35,136 B/token` (`34.31 KiB`) in FP8**.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) For `Llama-3-70B` (`140 GB` weights, $u = 0.90$, `8 GB` workspace), `TP = 4` (`320 GB` total) leaves a **`140.0 GB` KV pool** supporting **`218`, `54`, and `13` sequences** at $S = 2048, 8192, 32768$, whereas `TP = 8` (`640 GB` total) leaves a **`428.0 GB` KV pool** supporting **`668`, `167`, and `41` sequences**; (b) transferring `4 GB` over `64 GB/s` PCIe Gen4 takes **`62.5 ms` one-way (`125.0 ms` round-trip)**, making GPU prefill recomputation (`preemption_mode="recompute"`, `--swap-space 0`) faster for all $S < 8,192$.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) On $[-3.0, 0.1, 3.2]$, symmetric `absmax` gives **`scale = 39.6875`, `X_q = 4`, `dequant = 0.1008` (`error = 0.0008`)**, while asymmetric `zero-point` gives **`scale = 41.129`, `zp = -5`, `X_q = -1`, `dequant = 0.0973` (`error = 0.0027`)**; (b) for $\max(|X_j|) = 100.0, \max(|W_j|) = 1.0$, SmoothQuant yields **`s_j = 10.0` ($\max|\hat{X}_j| = \max|\hat{W}_j| = 10.0$) at $\alpha = 0.5$** and **`s_j = 39.81` ($\max|\hat{X}_j| = 2.51, \max|\hat{W}_j| = 39.81$) at $\alpha = 0.8$**, while `MXFP4` (`0.5` B/param) compresses `gpt-oss-120b` (`117B`) to **`58.5 GB`** (`< 80 GB`) and `gpt-oss-20b` (`21B`) to **`10.5 GB`** (`< 16 GB`).
