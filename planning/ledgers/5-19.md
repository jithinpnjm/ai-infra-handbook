## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Comparing Kubernetes Deployment Models and Cluster Topologies, `PK` Ch. 1–2 & `KUR` Ch. 3):** Weigh Managed Kubernetes, GPU-Cloud Managed K8s, Self-Managed `kubeadm`/`Kubespray`, Packaged Distributions, `Cluster API` + `Metal3`, and `NVIDIA BCM` across control, hardware integration, and operational toil; separate production inference clusters from large shared training clusters to isolate SLOs while maximizing batch GPU pooling.
2. **Exercise 2 (Colocated vs. Dedicated `etcd` Thresholds and Node Pool Sizing Ratios, `PK` Ch. 2):** `analyze_gpu_fleet_and_etcd_sizing()` recommends considering dedicated `etcd` above `50` workers and requiring dedicated `etcd` above `200` workers (with `kube-apiserver` connecting directly to `etcd` members without a load balancer), while characteristic-based (`M5` `1:4`, `R5` `1:8`, `C5` `1:2`, GPU) and role-based (`system`, `edge`) pools prevent resource stranding.
3. **Exercise 3 (The 9-State GPU Node Lifecycle, Join Validation Gates, and PDB-Aware `kubectl drain`):** `evaluate_node_validation_and_drain()` holds new/repaired nodes behind a `validating` taint until `dcgmi diag`, intra-node `nccl`, inter-node `ib_write_bw`, jumbo MTU, and `fio` storage gates pass, and enforces PDB `disruptionsAllowed`, `--ignore-daemonsets`, and `--delete-emptydir-data` during `kubectl drain`.
4. **Exercise 4 (GPU Capacity Management: Allocation vs. Utilization Efficiency, Idle Reclamation, and Headroom):** `analyze_gpu_fleet_and_etcd_sizing()` distinguishes `requested / allocatable` from `busy / allocatable` to identify idle-allocated GPUs (0% DCGM utilization) and tracks `largest_free_block` and N+k rack-level spare headroom.
5. **Exercise 5 (The Add-On Compatibility Matrix and Drift Detection):** Lock and test the 8-layer version matrix (Kernel, NVIDIA Driver, MOFED, `containerd`/Toolkit, Kubernetes, CNI/CSI, GPU/Network Operator, CUDA base images) in GitOps rings, verified continuously by `Sonobuoy`, `kube-bench`, and `kubescape`.
6. **Exercise 6 (Cluster API, Pivoted Self-Contained Autoscaling `PK` Ch. 2, and On-Call SLOs/Runbooks):** `Cluster API` (`KubeadmControlPlane`, `MachineDeployment`, `MachineHealthCheck`) automates rolling node replacement and remediation, pivoting CAPI resources into the workload cluster (`clusterctl move`) removes external management-cluster dependencies for `Cluster Autoscaler`, and `Xid 79` runbooks automate cordon -> forensics -> checkpoint/drain -> cold BMC power cycle -> re-validation/RMA.
