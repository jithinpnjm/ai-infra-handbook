## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Why Per-Packet Round Robin Fails for TCP, Flow Hashing, and Hash Polarization):** (a) Sending consecutive packets of a single TCP flow alternating across parallel links (`balance-rr`) causes out-of-order arrival whenever queue depths differ, triggering duplicate ACKs and false TCP fast-retransmit rate cuts; (b) `select_bond_member_by_hash()` shows that `layer2` (MAC-only) hashing pins all 16 routed flows through a single gateway MAC onto 1 link, whereas `layer3+4` (5-tuple) spreads them across all 4 links; **hash polarization** occurs when leaf and spine switches use the exact same hash function and seed, leaving subset paths idle.
2. **Exercise 2 (LACP `802.1AX` / `802.3ad` Mechanics, Timers, and `/proc/net/bonding/bond0` Diagnostics):** LACP exchanges LACPDUs (`01:80:C2:00:00:02`, EtherType `0x8809`) where at least one side must be `active` (`evaluate_lacp_negotiation()`) and `lacp_rate=fast` sends hellos every `1 s` (`3 s` timeout vs `30 s`/`90 s` slow); in `/proc/net/bonding/bond0` (`audit_proc_bonding_status()`), a Partner MAC of `00:00:00:00:00:00` means the switch is not speaking LACP, and different `Aggregator ID`s across slaves mean the cables are plugged into uncoordinated switches.
3. **Exercise 3 (Linux Bonding Modes `0`–`6`, `xmit_hash_policy`, and Why Compute Rails Are Never Bonded):** Use Mode `1` (`active-backup`) when dual-homing to two independent switches without MLAG and Mode `4` (`802.3ad` LACP with `xmit_hash_policy=layer3+4` and `miimon=100`) when connecting to one switch or an MLAG pair; never bond GPU back-end compute rails (`ens1f0..ens8f0`) because `NCCL` requires independent rail-optimized NICs per GPU.
4. **Exercise 4 (Multi-Chassis Link Aggregation `MLAG`/`vPC` vs. `EVPN Multihoming` vs. L3 Routed Hosts):** Proprietary `MLAG`/`vPC` pairs synchronize state over a peer link and keepalive link to appear as one LACP partner (risking split-brain, orphan-port blackholes, and upgrade lockstep), whereas `EVPN multihoming` (`ESI-LAG`, `RFC 7432`) uses standards-based BGP EVPN without a peer link, and L3 routed uplinks (BGP/ECMP to the host or leaf) eliminate L2 multi-chassis state completely.
5. **Exercise 5 (Routed `ECMP`, Modulo-N vs. Resilient Hashing, Elephant Flows, Flowlets, and Adaptive Routing):** Using `compare_modulo_vs_resilient_remap_pct(4)`: when 1 of 4 ECMP paths fails, plain `hash mod N` (`mod 4` -> `mod 3`) remaps **`75.0%`** of all active flows, whereas **resilient (consistent) hashing** remaps only the **`25.0%`** of flows that were on the failed link; to split colliding **elephant flows**, AI fabrics use multiple QPs with varied RoCEv2 UDP source ports, **flowlet switching**, or **adaptive routing / packet spraying** (`InfiniBand AR`, `NVIDIA Spectrum-X`).
6. **Exercise 6 (HPC/AI Cluster Node Roles, Five Network Types, and Interconnect Topologies, `SCL` Chapter 4):** Separate **compute nodes** (lean, homogeneous), **control/management nodes** (HA pair), **login/access nodes** (strictly resource-limited), and **service nodes** (storage, build, pre/post-processing with `256 GB+` RAM—never running an NFS server on a compute node), wired across 5 networks (compute `1–3 µs` IB/RoCE, control Ethernet, OOB BMC, storage, external) using fat-tree, torus, or dragonfly topologies.
