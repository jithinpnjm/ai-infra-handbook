# Claim Ledger: 7.4 Redfish authentication, sessions and security

## DMTF Redfish Specification (DSP0266 v1.21.0) Section 13 Claims
- **TLS & Certificates (§13.1, §13.2):** Services shall support TLS 1.2 or later (RFC 7525) and only offer IANA "Recommended" cipher suites (`AES-GCM`); certificates shall be X.509 v3 (RFC 5280) and replaceable via `CertificateService.ReplaceCertificate` (and `GenerateCSR`). Sensitive properties like `Password` shall be `null` in all responses (§13.2).
- **Authentication & Sessions (§13.3):** Services shall support both HTTP Basic (over HTTPS, controllable via `HTTPBasicAuth`) and Redfish sessions (`POST /redfish/v1/SessionService/Sessions` returning `201 Created`, `X-Auth-Token`, `Location`, and `Session` body). Sessions use a sliding idle timeout (`SessionTimeout`) and terminate via `DELETE` on the `Location` URI (`204 No Content`) without aborting independent SSE or `UpdateService` streams (§13.3.4.3–13.3.4.4). Mutual TLS client certificate auth (§13.3.5) verifies against `AccountService.MultiFactorAuth.ClientCertificate` and OCSP, controlled by `RespondToUnauthenticatedClients`.
- **Authorization, Roles, Privilege Registry & OAuth 2.0 (§13.4):** Required standard roles (Table 41) are `Administrator` (`Login`, `ConfigureManager`, `ConfigureUsers`, `ConfigureComponents`, `ConfigureSelf`), `Operator` (`Login`, `ConfigureComponents`, `ConfigureSelf`), and `ReadOnly` (`Login`, `ConfigureSelf`), plus optional roles (`SystemAdministrator`, `SystemOperator`, `StorageAdministrator`, `StorageBackupOperator`, Table 42). Privilege Registry overrides evaluate in precedence order `PropertyOverrides` $\rightarrow$ `ResourceURIOverrides` $\rightarrow$ `SubordinateOverrides` (Table 43). OAuth 2.0 JWTs (§13.4.4) forbid `alg: "none"`, require `iss`, `sub`, `aud`, `scope`, and use `Redfish.Role.<RoleId>` / `Redfish.Privilege.<PrivilegeId>`.
- **Account Service, Lockout, Atomic Password Change & TOTP (§13.5):** `AccountLockoutThreshold` and `AccountLockoutDuration` lock accounts after failed attempts; `RequireChangePasswordAction: true` mandates `ManagerAccount.ChangePassword` with `SessionAccountPassword` (§13.5.3); `PasswordChangeRequired: true` restricts sessions to `403` except reading/updating own account or logging out (§13.5.4); TOTP MFA (§13.5.5) uses `GenerateSecretKeyRequired`, `GenerateSecretKey`, and `VerifyTimeBasedOneTimePassword`.
