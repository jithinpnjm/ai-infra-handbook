## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Threat Modeling, `STRIDE`, Biometrics vs. Rotatable Secrets, and Defense in Depth, `CN` Chapter 21):** (a) Apply `STRIDE` (*Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of privilege*) across the trust boundaries of an AI cluster; (b) fingerprints and biometrics are convenient local device factors but are **not secret** (left on surfaces) and **cannot be rotated after a database breach** (`CN` Ch. 21), so remote authentication must rely on rotatable cryptographic keys/certificates and hardware MFA (`FIDO2`) across overlapping Defense-in-Depth layers.
2. **Exercise 2 (`AAA` Protocols `RADIUS` vs. `TACACS+`, `802.1X`, and Layer-2 Switch Security):** **RADIUS** (`UDP 1812/1813`) combines authN/authZ for network access (`802.1X`) and encrypts only the password attribute, whereas **TACACS+** (`TCP 49`) encrypts the entire packet body and provides per-command authorization/accounting for switch/router administration; at Layer 2, combine **DHCP snooping**, **Dynamic ARP Inspection (`DAI`)**, **RA Guard**, **BPDU Guard**, and **MACsec (`802.1AE`)**.
3. **Exercise 3 (DoS/DDoS Classes, BGP Traffic Diversion `NO_PEER` / `NO_ADVERTISE` / `RTBH`, and Control-Plane Policing `CoPP`):** Mitigate volumetric floods (`DNS`/`NTP` amplification) with **BCP 38 / uRPF** anti-spoofing, anycast scrubbing, **RTBH (`65535:666`)**, or selective BGP community diversion (`NO_PEER` / `NO_ADVERTISE` to steer traffic away from saturated peering links, `CN` Ch. 21); mitigate state-exhaustion SYN floods with **`tcp_syncookies=1`**; mitigate GPU inference API exhaustion with authN and token-bucket quotas; and protect router CPUs with **CoPP**, **GTSM (`TTL 255`)**, and `TCP-AO`/`HMAC-SHA`.
4. **Exercise 4 (`SSH` Cryptography, Host-Key Verification, `ProxyJump` vs. Agent Forwarding, `sshd_config` Hardening, and `SSH` Certificates):** Using `audit_sshd_config()` and `simulate_fail2ban_window()`: enforce `PermitRootLogin no`, `PasswordAuthentication no`, `AllowAgentForwarding no` (using **`ProxyJump -J`** through a bastion so private keys never touch intermediate hosts), `MaxAuthTries 3`, and short-lived **SSH CA certificates** (`TrustedUserCAKeys` and `@cert-authority`) to eliminate TOFU and manual `authorized_keys` sprawl.
5. **Exercise 5 (Auditing Attack Surface with `nmap` / `ss -tulpn` and Zero-Trust Microsegmentation with Kubernetes `NetworkPolicy`):** Compare internal `ss -tulpn` with external `nmap -p- -sV` scans to catch exposed internal services (`Docker 2375`, `etcd 2379`, `Redis 6379`, `Ray 8265`, `Jupyter 8888`, `kubelet 10250`, `BMC 623`), and enforce Zero Trust (`NIST SP 800-207`) in Kubernetes with a namespace **default-deny Ingress/Egress `NetworkPolicy`** plus explicit label-based allow rules (`evaluate_k8s_ingress_network_policy()`, remembering to allow UDP/TCP `53` to `kube-dns`).
6. **Exercise 6 (Securing AI-Specific Surfaces: Out-of-Band `BMC`s, InfiniBand `P_Key`s, Supply Chain, and Boyd's `OODA` Loop):** Isolate BMCs (`IPMI 623` / Redfish `443`) on an air-gapped OOB VLAN; segment shared InfiniBand fabrics using 16-bit **Partition Keys (`P_Key`s)** enforced in HCA hardware (`evaluate_ib_pkey_communication()`, where bit 15 is full `1` vs limited `0` membership and bits `0..14` are the partition ID); sign container images and model weights; and run security operations through Colonel John Boyd's **OODA loop** (*Observe, Orient, Decide, Act*).
