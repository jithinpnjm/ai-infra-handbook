# Claim Ledger: 7.2 Redfish concepts: service root, resources, collections and OData

## DMTF Redfish Specification (DSP0266 v1.21.0) & Resource Guide (DSP2046) Claims
- **Well-Known URIs & Unauthenticated Access (§6.7 Table 4, §13.3.2):** `/redfish`, `/redfish/v1/`, `/redfish/v1/odata`, and `/redfish/v1/$metadata` are required well-known URIs (plus optional `/redfish/v1/openapi.yaml` in v1.6+) and allow unauthenticated `GET` requests (`200 OK`), while subordinate resources like `/redfish/v1/Systems` require authentication (`401 Unauthorized`). Verified by `code/part07/crawl_concepts_lab.py` (`3/3` PASS).
- **Resource & Collection Properties (§9.2.1, §9.5, §9.6):** Every resource requires `@odata.id`, `@odata.type`, `Id`, and `Name`. Every collection requires `@odata.id`, `@odata.type`, `Name`, `Members`, and `Members@odata.count` (with `Members@odata.nextLink` for pagination). `$top` and `$skip` do not change `Members@odata.count`, while `$filter` does (§9.6.10).
- **Naming, Annotations, Settings & OEM (§9.7–9.10):** PascalCase naming with unit suffixes (`Mbps`, `MHz`, `MB`, `Count`); annotations (`[<PropertyName>]@<Namespace>.<TermName>`) restricted to `odata`, `Redfish`, and `Message` namespaces (`@Redfish.AllowableValues`, `@Redfish.AllowablePattern`, `@Redfish.AllowableNumbers`, `@Redfish.ActionInfo`, `@Redfish.WriteableProperties`, `@Redfish.Settings`, `@Message.ExtendedInfo`, `@Redfish.Deprecated`); active resources link via `@Redfish.Settings` to `/Settings` or `/SD` for pending future state (§9.10); OEM extensions use ICANN domain, `EID_<num>`, or reserved `OpenBMC` identifiers under `Oem` (§9.8).
- **SSDP Discovery (§12.4):** Multicast on UDP port `1900` (`239.255.255.250` / `ff02::c`) with search target `urn:dmtf-org:service:redfish-rest:1`.
