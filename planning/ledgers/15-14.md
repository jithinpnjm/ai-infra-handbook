## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Dividing by `71.24 s` gives **`RPS = 14.04 req/s`**, **`Input TPS = 3,383.52 tok/s`**, **`Output GTPS = 2,658.76 tok/s`**, **`Total TPS = 6,042.27 tok/s`**, mean residency **`W = 0.14218 + (189.41 - 1) * 0.01642 = 3.2359 s`**, and Little's Law in-flight concurrency **`L = 14.04 * 3.2359 = 45.43` requests**; (b) for `mu = 25.0 RPS`, open-loop queueing yields **`(W_queue = 60.0 ms, W = 100.0 ms)` at `15.0 RPS` (`rho = 0.60`), `(W_queue = 360.0 ms, W = 400.0 ms)` at `22.5 RPS` (`rho = 0.90`), and `(W_queue = 1,960.0 ms, W = 2,000.0 ms)` at `24.5 RPS` (`rho = 0.98`)**, whereas a closed-loop test at `N = 4` self-throttles its arrival rate **5.0x from `20.0 RPS` (`4 / 0.20 s`) down to `4.0 RPS` (`4 / 1.00 s`)**.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Under `P90 TTFT <= 400 ms` and `P90 TPOT <= 40 ms`, colocated Goodput is **`min(3.0, 1.6) = 1.60 RPS/GPU`**, `2P1D` cluster Goodput is **`min(2 * 5.6, 10.0) = 10.0 RPS`** across `3` GPUs, and disaggregated Goodput per GPU is **`10.0 / 3 = 3.33 RPS/GPU` (`~3.3 RPS/GPU`, a `2.08x` per-GPU speedup)**; (b) the Pareto-optimal Goodput point under `P95 TTFT <= 300 ms` and `P95 ITL <= 25 ms` is **`C = 32` (`1,340.0 tok/s`, `245.0 ms` TTFT, `22.8 ms` ITL)**, and at `C = 128` (`2,150.0 tok/s`), **`2,150.0 - 1,340.0 = 810.0 tok/s`** of raw throughput exceeds the SLO ceiling (giving `0.0 tok/s` strict Goodput).
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) On the L40S, 4-bit AWQ expands KV cache capacity **`2.65x` (`191,056 / 72,064` tokens)**, Prefix Repetition increases FP16 throughput **`2.37x` (`1,123.13 / 474.38 tok/s`)**, AWQ increases ShareGPT throughput **`2.70x` (`1,280.0 / 474.0 tok/s`, reported `2.7x`)**, and AWQ reduces mean TTFT by **`42.78%` (`~42%`, from `103.61 ms` to `59.29 ms`)**; (b) `6.5x` on `8` GPUs equals **`81.25%` scaling efficiency** (`6.5 / 8`), `52.0%` on `64` GPUs leaves **`30.72` (`~31`) idle GPUs** (`64 - 33.28`), and Amdahl's Law with `s = 0.10` caps maximum speedup at **`1 / 0.10 = 10.0x`**.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Moving from `64` to `4,096` prompt tokens changes Request Rate by **`-86.22%` (`-86%`, `45.0 -> 6.2 req/s`)**, P95 TTFT by **`+12,584.21%` (`+12,584%`, `38.0 -> 4,820.0 ms`)**, P95 ITL by **`+75.31%` (`+75%`, `16.2 -> 28.4 ms`)**, and VRAM by **`+86.67%` (`+86%`, `42.0 -> 78.4 GB`)**, which is remediated by **`--enable-chunked-prefill --max-num-batched-tokens 2048 --max-num-seqs 128`** (`290.0 ms` P95 TTFT, `16.62x` faster); (b) in Lab 04, `ProcessPoolExecutor` boosts throughput **`7.73x` (`104.17 / 13.47 RPS`)** and reduces P99 pipeline latency **`31.48x` (`1,410.20 / 44.80 ms`)**.
