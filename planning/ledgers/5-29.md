## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (`CRD` Anatomy, Structural Schemas, Pruning, and `x-kubernetes-validations` CEL Rules):** `evaluate_crd_schema_and_cel()` prunes undeclared fields (`preserveUnknownFields: false`), applies schema `default`s, and enforces CEL cross-field rules (`CordonAndDrain` requiring `maxUnavailableNodes`) and immutability (`self == oldSelf`) in-process without webhooks.
2. **Exercise 2 (CRD Subresources `/status` and `/scale`, Multi-Version Serving, and Storage Version Migration):** `/status` separates RBAC and updates `.status` without incrementing `metadata.generation`, `/scale` wires CRs into HPA/`kubectl scale`, and `convert_crd_via_hub_and_migrate_storage()` converts `v1alpha1` objects to the `v1beta1` hub storage version and updates `status.storedVersions`.
3. **Exercise 3 (The Operator Pattern, the 5 Capability Levels, and `ownerReferences` vs. `finalizers`):** `reconcile_cr_with_finalizer_and_status()` uses `ownerReferences` for in-cluster child garbage collection and `finalizers` (`platform.example.com/cleanup`) for external cleanup before deletion; CRs must be deleted before uninstalling the operator to avoid stuck `Terminating` objects.
4. **Exercise 4 (Programming Kubernetes from Python `kubernetes` Client and `kopf`, `KUR` Ch. 18):** Use `config.load_kube_config()` or `config.load_incluster_config()`, `CoreV1Api`/`AppsV1Api`, `watch.Watch().stream()`, `stream(v1.connect_get_namespaced_pod_exec, ...)`, and `portforward(v1.connect_get_namespaced_pod_portforward, ...)`.
5. **Exercise 5 (Packaging, Distributing, and Upgrading Operators):** Because Helm's `crds/` directory is ignored on `helm upgrade`, upgrade CRDs explicitly via `kubectl apply --server-side` (avoiding the 256 KiB `last-applied-configuration` limit) or a dedicated `-crds` chart/OLM bundle before upgrading the operator deployment.
6. **Exercise 6 (Mapping the AI-Platform Operator Ecosystem):** Maps `GPU Operator` (`ClusterPolicy`), `Network Operator` (`NicClusterPolicy`), `Kueue` (`ClusterQueue`/`Workload`), `JobSet`, `Kubeflow Training Operator` (`PyTorchJob`), `KubeRay` (`RayCluster`), and `KServe` (`InferenceService`); already-running workload pods continue executing if an operator pod crashes.
