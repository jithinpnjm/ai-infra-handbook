## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (`controller-runtime` Architecture: `Manager`, Shared Cache, Caching `Client`, and Leader Election, `KPG` Ch. 10):** `Get`/`List` read from the shared Informer cache while `Create`/`Update`/`Patch`/`Delete`/`Status().Patch` write directly to `kube-apiserver`; `LeaderElection` ensures only one replica reconciles while standby replicas keep caches warm.
2. **Exercise 2 (Defining APIs with `kubebuilder`/`controller-gen` Markers and Hub-and-Spoke Conversion, `KPG` Ch. 9 & 13):** `controller-gen` reads `+kubebuilder:*` markers to generate `zz_generated.deepcopy.go`, CRD schemas, and RBAC; `convert_myresource_spoke_to_hub_and_back()` demonstrates Hub (`func (*MyResource) Hub() {}`) and Spoke (`ConvertTo`/`ConvertFrom`) multi-version conversion.
3. **Exercise 3 (Writing an Idempotent `Reconcile` Loop and Avoiding `/status` Self-Trigger Hot Loops, `KPG` Ch. 10 & 11):** Follow `Observe -> Decide -> Act -> Report`, use `client.IgnoreNotFound(err)` on the primary CR, and use `set_status_condition_and_check_noop()` (`meta.SetStatusCondition` + skipping no-op status writes + `GenerationChangedPredicate`) to prevent status-update hot loops.
4. **Exercise 4 (Controller Builder Watches `For`/`Owns`/`Watches`, `Predicates`, and Cache `FieldIndexer`, `KPG` Ch. 10):** `evaluate_watch_predicates()` filters out status-only updates on primary CRs and heartbeat-only updates on watched `Node`s, while `mgr.GetFieldIndexer().IndexField` enables `O(1)` `client.MatchingFields{"spec.nodeName": node}` queries.
5. **Exercise 5 (Finalizers, Foreign-Object Provenance Annotations, RBAC Markers, and Webhooks, `PK` Ch. 8):** Add finalizers before side effects, stamp `platform.example.com/cordoned-by: <policy>` on shared `Node`s before cordoning, scope `+kubebuilder:rbac` markers to least privilege, and check `req.DryRun` before external side effects in webhooks.
6. **Exercise 6 (Three-Layer Operator Testing `Pure Functions -> envtest -> Kind/GKE` and Production Hardening, `KPG` Ch. 12):** Pure functions test decision logic in microseconds, `envtest` tests real `kube-apiserver` + `etcd` CRD/CEL/patch/finalizer semantics (without kubelet/GC/RBAC restrictions), and `kind`/GKE tests RBAC, leader failover, and live node drains with disruption budgets and kill switches.
