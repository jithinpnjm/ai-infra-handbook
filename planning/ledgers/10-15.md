## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Unfused vs `fusedL2Norm`):** (a) `1 FLOP / 12 B = 0.0833 FLOPs/byte`; (b) `36 B` per element across 3 passes and `4 FLOPs / 36 B = 0.1111 FLOPs/byte`; (c) `fusedL2Norm` moves `12 B` per element (`3.0x` HBM traffic reduction), achieving `3 / 12 = 0.25 FLOPs/byte` (conservative) or `4 / 12 = 0.3333 FLOPs/byte` (full, `3.0x` higher intensity).
2. **Exercise 2 (Thread Block Cluster `TMA` multicast):** A `2 × 2` (`4`-CTA) cluster multicasts one HBM tile load into the `DSMEM` of all `4` SMs (`4x` traffic reduction); CUDA's portable cluster limit is `8` CTAs, and `cudaFuncAttributeNonPortableClusterSizeAllowed` unlocks `16`-CTA clusters (`16x` reuse) on Blackwell.
3. **Exercise 3 (`256 KB` `TMEM` & `stage_ab_tiles` `SMEM` sizing):** (a) `256 × 512 × 2 B = 262,144 B` (`256.0 KiB`, `100%` of `TMEM`), (b) `256 × 256 × 4 B = 262,144 B` (`256.0 KiB`, `100%` of `TMEM`), (c) `256 × 256 × 0.5 B = 32,768 B` (`32.0 KiB`, `12.5%` of `TMEM`), (d) `512 × 512 × 2 B = 524,288 B` (`512.0 KiB`, exceeds `256 KiB` `TMEM`); `stage_ab_tiles` uses `4 × 1,024 × 4 B = 16,384 B` (`16.0 KiB`) of dynamic `SMEM`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Precision, 2:4 sparsity & `NestedTensor`):** (a) `10,000 / 80 = 125.0x` (`~2` orders of magnitude) and `B300` `15 PFLOPS` is `+50.0%` over `B200` `10 PFLOPS`; (b) `INT8` (`1 B`) cuts `FP32` (`4 B`) weight traffic by `75.0%` and `DP4A` executes `4` INT8 MACs/instruction; (c) `NestedTensor` processes `1,920` real tokens instead of `4 × 1,024 = 4,096` padded tokens, saving `2,176 / 4,096 = 53.125%` (`53.1%`) of padding traffic.
5. **Exercise 5 (`CUTLASS` vs hand-tuned MMA Table 9-1):** Both achieve `98%` Tensor Core utilization (`~60` vs `~52` regs/thread, `~4 KB` vs `~2 KB` SMEM/CTA), with `CUTLASS` saving weeks of assembly tuning (`96%–98%` efficiency); `TMA` moves tiles `HBM → SMEM` asynchronously and `tcgen05.mma` reads `SMEM → TMEM` (`256 KB`/SM) without occupying general-purpose registers.
6. **Exercise 6 (Inline PTX Table 9-2 & DeepSeek `DeepEP`):** `ld.global.nc.l1::no_allocate.l2::256b` issues a non-coherent load (`nc`), prevents L1 cache line allocation (`l1::no_allocate`) so MoE streaming does not evict compute warps' hot L1 data, and prefetches `256 B` into L2 (`l2::256b`); in Table 9-2, inline PTX (`mad.wide.u32` + `ld.global.cg` + `cp.async.bulk.prefetch.L2.global`) cuts memory stalls `35% → 20%`, raises IPC `1.5 → 1.6`, and speeds up runtime `1.07 → 1.00 ms` (`1.07x`), while `DISABLE_AGGRESSIVE_PTX_INSTRS=1` guards against undocumented PTX breaking across `ptxas`/GPU generations.
