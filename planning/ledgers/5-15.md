## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (`NetworkPolicy` Isolation Semantics, Additive Union Rules, and the Single-Dash AND vs. OR YAML Trap, `NK` Ch. 4–5):** Pods are non-isolated until selected for a direction, after which only the additive union of matching rules is allowed; `evaluate_peer_list()` proves that putting `namespaceSelector` and `podSelector` in one `from` list item ANDs them, whereas an accidental second `-` dash ORs them and exposes the service to every pod in the peer namespace.
2. **Exercise 2 (Building a Layered Multi-Tenant Namespace Baseline):** Combine `default-deny-all` (`podSelector: {}, policyTypes: [Ingress, Egress]`), `allow-dns` (`UDP/TCP 53` to `kube-dns` and `169.254.20.10/32`), `allow-same-namespace`, and app-specific rules (`allow-gateway-to-model`).
3. **Exercise 3 (Kernel Enforcement: `conntrack`, Calico `iptables` + `ipset`, and Cilium `eBPF` Identity Maps):** `conntrack` (`ESTABLISHED,RELATED`) makes policies stateful so replies and pre-existing connections pass until flushed; Calico compiles selectors into `cali40s:*` kernel `ipset`s while Cilium uses `cilium_policy_<id>` eBPF maps keyed by numeric Security Identity.
4. **Exercise 4 (Extended Policy Models: `GlobalNetworkPolicy`, `CiliumNetworkPolicy`, and `AdminNetworkPolicy`):** `evaluate_policy_hierarchy()` enforces `AdminNetworkPolicy` (`Allow`/`Deny`/`Pass`) before namespace `NetworkPolicy` and `BaselineAdminNetworkPolicy` after; `GlobalNetworkPolicy` blocks `169.254.169.254/32` and `CiliumNetworkPolicy` enables `toFQDNs` via the per-node DNS proxy.
5. **Exercise 5 (The 12 `NetworkPolicy` Traps on GPU Clusters):** `NetworkPolicy` does not cover `hostNetwork: true` pods or secondary Multus RDMA interfaces (`net1..net8`, which require fabric VLAN/VRF or InfiniBand `P_Key` isolation), and default-deny must allow control-plane IPs to reach admission webhook pods.
6. **Exercise 6 (Testing, Auditing, and Debugging Policies):** Use `cilium policy trace`, `hubble observe --verdict DROPPED` (or `--verdict AUDIT` in `policyAuditMode`), `iptables -L cali-tw-<iface> -v`, and CI connectivity matrices (`cyclonus`).
