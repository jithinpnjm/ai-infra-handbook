## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Testing Slurm-to-PyTorch Rendezvous Glue Without a Cluster):** `expand_hostlist("gpu[01-03,07],cpu1,x[9-10]")` returns `['gpu01', 'gpu02', 'gpu03', 'gpu07', 'cpu1', 'x9', 'x10']`, and `dist_env()` maps `SLURM_JOB_ID=4242` and `gpu[01-03,07]` to `MASTER_ADDR=gpu01` and `MASTER_PORT=14242`.
2. **Exercise 2 (Comparing `srun`-per-GPU vs `torchrun`-per-Node Launch Scripts):** Pattern A (`--ntasks-per-node=8 --gpus-per-node=8`, `srun --kill-on-bad-exit=1 --cpu-bind=cores --gpu-bind=closest python train.py`) gives Slurm per-GPU cgroup/affinity/accounting control, while Pattern B (`--ntasks-per-node=1 --cpus-per-task=96`, `srun torchrun --nproc-per-node=8 ...`) gives `torchrun` elastic worker management.
3. **Exercise 3 (DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 Slurm Launch and the `127.0.0.1` Trap):** `deepspeed.init_distributed()` requires `LOCAL_RANK=$SLURM_LOCALID` exported in the `srun` subshell; setting `MASTER_ADDR=127.0.0.1` causes each node to form an isolated 1-node process group that never synchronizes gradients across nodes.
4. **Exercise 4 (Megatron-LM 8B Checkpoint Footprint and `.distcp` Conversion):** An `8.03B` parameter GPT model takes `8.03B * 2 B = 16.06 GB` (`bf16` weights) + `8.03B * 8 B = 64.24 GB` (`fp32` Adam states) = `80.30 GB` theoretical and `4 * 27G = 108.0 GB` across `.distcp` shards; `convert_megatron_checkpoint.py` strips optimizer state and exports a standalone PyTorch `model.pt` for inference.
5. **Exercise 5 (Quantifying `gloo` vs `nccl` Bandwidth and Communicator Init Overhead):** A `400 MB` all-reduce takes `200.0 ms` over `2 GB/s` TCP (`gloo`) vs `4.0 ms` over `100 GB/s` IB (`nccl`), and moving `dist.init_process_group("nccl")` (`48.0 ms`) outside the training step loop reduces step communication overhead from `48.5 ms` to `0.5 ms` (`>98%` savings).
6. **Exercise 6 (Multi-Node `NCCL` Diagnostic Benchmarking):** `nccl_diag_metrics()` computes Ring AllReduce bus bandwidth as `(size_mb * 2 * 10) / elapsed` (`100.0 GB/s`) and one-way point-to-point latency over 100 ping-pongs as `(elapsed / 200) * 1e6` (`20.0 us`).
