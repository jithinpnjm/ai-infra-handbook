## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Across `8` H100 SXM GPUs (`8 * 989 = 7,912 TFLOPS` peak), `3,200 tok/s` achieves **`1,344.0 TFLOPS` (`16.99%` / `~17%` MFU)** and `9,000 tok/s` achieves **`3,780.0 TFLOPS` (`47.78%` / `~48%` MFU, a `2.8125x` gain)**; (b) with full activation recomputation (`8N` vs `6N`), `HFU = (8/6) * 47.78% = 63.70%` (**`5,040.0 TFLOPS`**), and completing `100,000` tokens in `10 s` (`10,000 tok/s`) against `8 * 1,500 = 12,000 tok/s` peak yields **`83.33%` (`83.3%`) Goodput efficiency**.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) At `52%` scaling efficiency, `64` GPUs deliver **`33.28` (`~33`) effective GPUs** and waste **`30.72` (`~31`) GPUs**, losing **`10,321.92` GPU-hours** over `336 hours` (**`$41,287.68` at `$4/hr` to `$51,609.60` at `$5/hr`**); (b) scaling from `155.0 ms` (`1` GPU) to `33.0 ms` (`16` GPUs, `55%` comm) yields a **`4.697x` speedup (`29.36%` scaling efficiency)** decomposed into **`14.85 ms` compute and `18.15 ms` exposed AllReduce**, while Amdahl's Law with `s = 0.10` caps maximum speedup at **`10.0x`** (`8.77x` on `64` GPUs).
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) In Table 13-2 (`138.00 ms` across `320` calls), GEMMs take **`78.50 ms` (`56.88%`)**, MoE routing takes **`30.80 ms` (`22.32%`)**, and the other 6 ops take **`28.70 ms` (`20.80%`)**, which matches `nsys nvtx_gpu_proj_sum` (`60.5 + 58.3 + 19.2 = 138.0 ms`) where CUPTI projects all `64` asynchronous per-expert `aten::add_` kernels onto `1` host `optimizer_step` range (`19.2 ms`); (b) in the `24.0 ms` MLPerf step, compute is **`19.5 ms` (`81.3%`)** and AllReduce is **`4.0 ms` (`16.7%` = `1/6`)**, while `ncu` Roofline shows the baseline kernel is memory-bound (`50%` FLOPS / `70%` HBM BW) and the optimized kernel is compute-bound (`85%` FLOPS / `40%` HBM BW).
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) `torch.compile(mode="max-autotune")` saves **`75.0 ms` (`30.24%` reduction, `1.43x` speedup)** from `248.0 ms` to `173.0 ms`, and capturing CUDA Graphs in **`[8, 4, 2, 1]` decreasing order** allocates the maximum buffer on batch `8` first so smaller batches reuse it; (b) `512:2` creates `256 MB` sub-buckets (`[512, 768, 1024]`) rounding `600 MB` to `768 MB`, whereas `expandable_segments:True` keeps reserved VRAM at **`600 MB` (saving `168 MB` / `21.88%`)**, `SimpleFSDP` achieves **`46.08 GB` (`-28%`) and `16,900 tok/s` (`+69%`)**, and `AsyncTP` reduces `16.0 ms` by `3.4 ms` (`85%` of `4.0 ms`) to **`12.6 ms` (`21.25%` reduction)**.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) Moving `init_process_group` (`48.0 ms`) outside the loop cuts step time from `48.5 ms` to **`0.5 ms` (`98.97%` reduction, `97.0x` speedup)**, and on `28.0 GB` FP32 gradients an `8`-GPU Ring moves **`49.0 GB` in `54.4 ms` (`8` hops)** on `900 GB/s` NVLink while a `512`-GPU Ring moves **`55.89 GB` in `2.24 s`** on `25 GB/s` IB (`512` Ring hops vs **`9` Tree hops**); (b) the straggler is **`node-14-straggler` (`0 ms` NCCL wait) / HTA Rank `7` (`21.0 ms` compute, `0.0 ms` idle)** while Rank `0` has `2.0 ms` idle, diagnosed via `dmesg | grep -i "AER"` and remediated via `kubectl cordon node-14-straggler`.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) In Meta's 54-day Llama 3 405B study (`419` stops), `Faulty GPU` (`148`) + `HBM3` (`72`) = **`220` stops (`47.3%`)** and all six `GPU` categories sum to **`268` stops (`64.0%`)**; (b) thermal drift from `45.0%` to `28.0%` MFU is a **`37.78%` relative compute drop**, a stopped `nvidia-fabricmanager` drops throughput by **`67.37%` (`950 -> 310 tok/s`)** and `busbw` by **`20x` (`400 -> 20 GB/s`)**, and the PyTorch HUD CI gate **passes `[9400, 9450, 9800]`** (only 2 consecutive `>= 5%` drops) and **fails `[9400, 9450, 9480]`** (3 consecutive `>= 5%` drops).
