## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** `inf-embed-a` (`0.5`), `nb-alice` (`0.25`), and `nb-bob` (`0.25`) pack onto `GPU 0` (`1.0` fraction, `80,000 MiB <= 81,920 MiB`) backed by **`1` integer reservation pod**, `train-full-1` (`1.0`) lands on `GPU 1`, and **`2` whole GPUs (`GPU 2` and `GPU 3`) remain 100% free**.
2. **Exercise 2:** Although `0.25 + 0.25 = 0.50 <= 1.0` in compute fraction, their combined memory claim is `50,000 + 50,000 = 100,000 MiB > 81,920 MiB`, so KAI Scheduler rejects placing the second pod on the same GPU to prevent a CUDA OOM.
3. **Exercise 3:** `1` replica: `(25%, 12 GB, 8 ms)`; `2` replicas: `(40%, 24 GB, 25 ms)`; `4` replicas: `(55%, 48 GB, 120 ms, 32 GB >= 20 GB headroom)`; `8` replicas: `(65%, 96 GB > 80 GB OOM, 500 ms)` -> maximum safe setting is **`replicas: 4`**.
4. **Exercise 4:** `1.3B` LLM Training (`12.4 GB`) -> **`2g.20gb`** (`2/7` slices, `20 GB`, `283` TFLOPS); Batch CNN (`4 GB`) -> **`1g.10gb`** (`1/7` slice, `10 GB`, `141` TFLOPS); Online Embedding (`< 1 GB`) -> **`1g.10gb`** (`1/7` slice, `10 GB`, `141` TFLOPS); total used = **`4/7` slices and `40/80 GB`**, leaving **`3/7` slices and `40 GB` free**.
5. **Exercise 5:** Assign **`1 × 3g.40gb`** (`3/7` slices, `40 GB` for the `35 GB` tenant) and **`2 × 2g.20gb`** (`2/7` slices, `20 GB` each for the two `15 GB` tenants), consuming `3 + 2 + 2 = 7/7` slices and `40 + 20 + 20 = 80/80 GB`.
6. **Exercise 6:** Run `nvidia-smi -i 0 --query-gpu=mig.mode.current --format=csv,noheader` + `nvidia-smi mig -lgi` (checks MIG), `nvidia-smi vgpu` / `systemctl status nvidia-vgpud` (checks vGPU), and `nvidia-smi --query-processes=gpu_uuid,pid,process_name,gpu_memory_usage --format=csv` (checks multiple container PIDs time-slicing a single GPU UUID).
