## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Why More Bandwidth Does Not Eliminate QoS, Classification/Marking, Trust Boundaries, and the Unmarked Internet, `CN` Chapter 8):** (a) Buying bigger links does not eliminate microsecond fan-in/incast bursts, serialization jitter (`9000` B = `0.72 ms` delaying a `64` B = `0.05 ms` packet by `14x` on `100 Mb/s`), or downstream bottlenecks; (b) `encode_decode_ip_tos_byte()` packs `6`-bit **DSCP** (`26` for RoCE data, `48` for CNPs, `46` for EF) and `2`-bit **ECN** (`10b` = `ECT(0)`, `11b` = `CE`) into the 8-bit IP ToS/Traffic Class byte (`0x6a` -> `0x6b`), which must be validated at **trust boundaries** and copied across tunnel headers (while the public Internet ignores/rewrites DSCP).
2. **Exercise 2 (Policing vs. Shaping, `LLQ` vs. `CBWFQ`/`ETS`, and Active Queue Management `WRED` / `CoDel`):** A **policer** immediately drops/re-marks excess above a token bucket (no delay, causes TCP sawtooth), while a **shaper** buffers and smooths bursts (adds delay); **Strict Priority (`LLQ`)** serves latency-critical packets first (and must be policed to prevent starvation), while **`CBWFQ` / `DRR` / `ETS` (`802.1Qaz`)** guarantees weighted bandwidth shares; **WRED** drops/marks probabilistically between `Kmin` and `Kmax`, and **CoDel (`RFC 8290`)** cures bufferbloat by dropping/marking when minimum packet sojourn delay exceeds `5 ms` over a `100 ms` window.
3. **Exercise 3 (Clos Congestion Points and End-to-End `ECN` + `CNP` in RoCEv2, `ADC` Chapter 7):** Even in a `1:1` Clos, congestion occurs at leaf downlinks (**incast**), local leaf switching, leaf-to-spine uplinks (hash collisions), spine-to-leaf downlinks, and super-spine links; in RoCEv2 (`UDP 4791`), the sender sets `ECT(0)` (`10`), a congested switch sets **`CE` (`11`)** when queue depth crosses `Kmin..Kmax`, the receiving NIC generates a high-priority **Congestion Notification Packet (`CNP`, BTH opcode `0x81` = `129`)** back to the sender, and the sending NIC throttles that specific Queue Pair (`QP`).
4. **Exercise 4 (`PFC` `802.1Qbb`, `XON`/`XOFF`, Headroom Math, and `PFC` Storms / Deadlocks / Watchdog):** **PFC** (`EtherType 0x8808`, opcode `0x0101`, MAC `01:80:C2:00:00:01`) pauses a single priority (`0..7`) for up to `65535` quanta of `512` bit-times when ingress buffer occupancy crosses **`XOFF`** (resuming at **`XON`**), requiring **headroom buffer** above `XOFF` (`compute_pfc_headroom_bytes()`, which increases by `> 200 KB` on a `500 m` `400G` fiber vs `10 m`) to absorb in-flight bytes; because PFC pauses the entire priority class, it can cause **Head-of-Line blocking**, **PFC pause storms** (mitigated by the **PFC watchdog** after `100–200 ms`), and **PFC deadlocks** on cyclic paths.
5. **Exercise 5 (`DCQCN` Threshold Ordering `XON < ECN < XOFF`, `SFC` `802.1Qdw`, and `CSIG`):** Using `evaluate_dcqcn_queue_action()`, **DCQCN** orders queue thresholds strictly as **`XON < ECN (Kmin / Kmax) < XOFF`** so flow-granular **ECN/CNP** rate reduction resolves congestion first and class-wide **PFC** fires only as a last-resort safety net before buffer drop; **Source Flow Control (`SFC`, `802.1Qdw`)** trims congested packets and signals the source leaf/NIC directly (avoiding ECN's full receiver RTT and PFC's class-wide HOL blocking), while **CSIG** embeds multi-bit bottleneck bandwidth/delay telemetry in-band.
6. **Exercise 6 (Configuring and Troubleshooting a Lossless RoCEv2 Fabric with `mlnx_qos`, `ethtool -S`, and SONiC):** Trust DSCP end-to-end, map RoCE data (`DSCP 26`) to lossless **priority `3`** (`mlnx_qos -i eth2 --trust dscp --pfc 0,0,0,1,0,0,0,0`) with ECN (`Kmin < Kmax < XOFF`) and PFC watchdog, map **CNPs (`DSCP 48`)** to strict-priority **priority `6`**, verify **zero drops** on the lossless queue, and use the Section 11 decision tree (`drops` -> `PFC pauses` -> `ECN marks/CNPs` -> `physical errors` -> `NCCL/host stragglers`) to isolate slow training jobs.
