## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Cost order is `LOC` (`0`) $<$ `NVL` (`10`) $<$ `C2C` (`15`) $<$ `PIX` (`30`) $<$ `PXB` (`50`) $<$ `PHB` (`80`) $<$ `SYS` (`200`); (b) a `500 MB` (`0.5 GB`) transfer takes `0.56 ms` on `NV4` (`900 GB/s`) versus `17.86 ms` (`~18 ms`) on `SYS` (`28 GB/s`), a `32.14x` slowdown; (c) topology-aware overlapped execution raises SM busy from `60%` to `90%` and cuts iteration time from `100.0 ms` to `70.0 ms` (`30.0%` reduction).
2. **Exercise 2:** `(GPU 0, mlx5_0)` is `PIX` (same PCIe switch `0000:01:00.0`), `(GPU 1, mlx5_0)` is `PXB` (same root `0000:00:00.0`, different switch `0000:02:00.0`), `(GPU 0, mlx5_1)` is `SYS` (crosses `NUMA 0 -> NUMA 1`), and `(GPU 2, mlx5_1)` is `PIX` (`0000:81:00.0`); at `NCCL_NET_GDR_LEVEL=PIX` only `(GPU 0, mlx5_0)` and `(GPU 2, mlx5_1)` get `GPUDirect RDMA`, whereas `NCCL_NET_GDR_LEVEL=PXB` also enables `(GPU 1, mlx5_0)`.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) `16` channels consume `16` SM blocks (`12.12%` of `132` H100 SMs) and $16 \times 2 \times 4\text{ MiB} = 128.0\text{ MiB}$ of HBM; (b) `MIN=4, MAX=8` clamps to `8` channels (`6.06%` of SMs) and $8 \times 2 \times 8\text{ MiB} = 128.0\text{ MiB}$; (c) zero-copy `ncclCommRegister` cuts channels from `16` to `4` (saving `12` SM blocks, `3.03%` of SMs) and reduces channel buffer memory to $4 \times 2 \times 4\text{ MiB} = 32.0\text{ MiB}$.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Intra-node `NV18` selects `P2P` (`P2P/IPC`) when `NCCL_P2P_DISABLE=0` and `SHM` (`SHM/direct`, host staging) when `NCCL_P2P_DISABLE=1`; (b) inter-tray `NVL72` with `NCCL_MNNVL_ENABLE=1` + `NCCL_NVLS_ENABLE=1` selects `NVLS` (`mnnvl_active=True`); (c) inter-node `PIX` selects `NET_IB` with `NET/IB/GDRDMA` (`gpudirect_rdma_active=True`) when `nvidia_peermem` is loaded and host-staged `NET/IB` (`gpudirect_rdma_active=False`) when unloaded; (d) Quantum IB with `NCCL_COLLNET_ENABLE=1` selects `COLLNET` (`CollNet/SHARP`) when `NCCL_SHARP_DISABLE=0` and `NET_IB` when `NCCL_SHARP_DISABLE=1`; (e) `NCCL_IB_DISABLE=1` selects `NET_SOCKET` (`NET/Socket`).
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) `NCCL_SOCKET_IFNAME` matches `["eth0", "eth0.100"]` for `"eth0"`, `["eth0"]` for `"=eth0"`, `["ib0", "ib1"]` for `"^docker,lo,eth"`, and `["eth0.100", "eth1", "ib0", "ib1"]` for `"^=lo,docker0,eth0"`; (b) `NCCL_IB_HCA` matches `["mlx5_0:1", "mlx5_1:1", "mlx5_2:1", "mlx5_3:1"]` for `"^mlx5_bond"` and `["mlx5_0:1"]` for `"=mlx5_0:1"`; (c) `(2, 2) = 4` sockets (valid `<= 64`), `(8, 8) = 64` sockets (valid `<= 64`), `(16, 8) = 128` sockets (invalid `> 64`), with `2x 800 Gbps = 1.6 Tbps` and `4x 800 Gbps = 3.2 Tbps`.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) `Pods 1–4` consume all `64` cores and `4` GPUs on `NUMA 0` (`[0` cores, `0` GPUs`]`), while on `NUMA 1` (`64 - 2 = 62` free cores) `3` pods consume `48` cores leaving `[0, 14]` free cores and `[0, 1]` free GPUs, so the 8th pod (`16` cores requested $> 14$ free) fails with `TopologyAffinityError`; (b) reducing CPU requests to `12` cores/pod (`4 * 12 = 48 <= 62`, max `15` cores/pod) admits all `8` pods, and `NCCL_IGNORE_CPU_AFFINITY=1` prevents `NCCL` proxy threads from collapsing onto a narrow inherited core mask.
