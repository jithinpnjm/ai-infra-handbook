## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (The Complete Kubernetes-to-Linux-Kernel Master Map):** Maps Pods to the 8 namespaces, requests/limits to Cgroup v2 (`cpu.weight`, `cpu.max`, `memory.max`, `pids.max`), exclusive CPUs to `cpuset.cpus`, volumes/images to `overlayfs` + bind mounts + `tmpfs`, GPUs/RDMA to `devtmpfs` + `BPF_PROG_TYPE_CGROUP_DEVICE`, `securityContext` to capabilities + `PR_SET_NO_NEW_PRIVS` + `seccomp-bpf` + LSMs, and Services/NetworkPolicy to `veth` + `netfilter`/`conntrack`/`IPVS`/`eBPF`.
2. **Exercise 2 (The Eight Linux Namespaces and Pod User Namespaces `hostUsers: false`):** `net`, `ipc`, `uts`, and `cgroup` are shared across the Pod while `mnt` and `pid` are isolated per container; `check_kernel_version_features()` verifies idmapped mounts (5.12+) and idmapped `overlayfs` (5.19+) required for `hostUsers: false`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Cgroup v2 Unified Hierarchy, Multi-Threaded CFS CPU Throttling, and `memory.current` vs. `working_set`):** `simulate_cgroup_v2_cpu_and_memory()` shows 8 threads on a 2-CPU limit burn their 200ms quota in 25ms and stall for 75ms, while `working_set = memory.current - inactive_file` determines kubelet eviction and `/dev/shm` `tmpfs` counts against `memory.max`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Kernel Security Primitives and `overlayfs` / Page-Cache Writeback Stalls):** `drop: ["ALL"]`, `PR_SET_NO_NEW_PRIVS`, and `seccomp-bpf` shrink the shared-kernel syscall surface; modifying files in `lowerdir` triggers whole-file `overlayfs` copy-up; and setting `vm.dirty_background_bytes = 256Mi` / `vm.dirty_bytes = 1Gi` prevents multi-hundred-GB dirty page flush stalls on 1–2 TiB RAM nodes.
5. **Exercise 5 (Kernel Networking and the GPU/RDMA Kernel Stack):** `nvidia.ko`, `nvidia_uvm.ko`, `nvidia_peermem.ko` (GPUDirect RDMA), `nvidia_fs.ko` (GPUDirect Storage), and `mlx5_core`/`mlx5_ib` pair with `intel_iommu=on iommu=pt` and `CAP_IPC_LOCK` for line-rate DMA.
6. **Exercise 6 (Auditing Boot Parameters, `sysctl` Baselines, and Kernel Observability):** `audit_gpu_node_kernel_sysctls()` verifies `protectKernelDefaults` (`vm.overcommit_memory=1`, `kernel.panic=10`, `kernel.panic_on_oops=1`) plus `ip_forward=1`, `bridge-nf-call-iptables=1`, `numa_balancing=0`, and `rp_filter=0/2`, while `PSI` (`/proc/pressure/*`) and `Inspektor Gadget` (`kubectl gadget`) expose kernel stalls per Pod.
