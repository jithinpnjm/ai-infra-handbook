## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Slurm Daemons, Ports, and Hierarchical RPC Fan-Out):** `slurmctld` (`6817`) manages cluster/job state and scheduling, `slurmd` (`6818`) manages each compute node and forks `slurmstepd` per job step, `slurmdbd` (`6819`) mediates MariaDB accounting, `slurmrestd` exposes HTTP/JSON, `sackd` serves configless auth on login nodes, and `compute_rpc_tree_depth(1024, 50)` shows `slurmctld` reaches `1,024` nodes in `2` tree hops with at most `50` direct RPCs.
2. **Exercise 2 (Munge Authentication, Clock Skew, and UID Consistency):** `validate_munge_credential()` rejects a mismatched `/etc/munge/munge.key` with `Invalid credential`, a sender timestamp > 5 minutes in the future with `Rewound credential`, and a timestamp > 5 minutes in the past with `Expired credential`, while numeric UIDs/GIDs must match on all nodes because Munge encodes numeric IDs.
3. **Exercise 3 (Dedicated Test Partition Sizing and Compute-Node SSH Lockdown):** `check_test_partition_policy()` enforces a `10–15` minute walltime cap and `3–5` queued tasks per user to guarantee `1–2` hour test turnaround (`SCL` Ch. 13), while `pam_slurm_adopt` (or prolog/epilog `pam_listfile`) blocks direct SSH to compute nodes unless the user owns an active job on that node.
4. **Exercise 4 (Controller High Availability, `StateSaveLocation`, and Split-Brain Recovery):** Active/passive `SlurmctldHost` controllers share `StateSaveLocation` (`scontrol takeover` triggers planned failover); `-R` recovers full state including partitions, `-i` ignores corrupt state records, and `-c` wipes all running/queued jobs; recovering from split-brain requires stopping all but one controller, restoring `StateSaveLocation`, starting with default/`-R`, and verifying `squeue`/`sinfo`.
5. **Exercise 5 (Accounting Architecture `slurmdbd.conf`, `sacctmgr` Hierarchy, and Database Protection):** `slurmdbd` decouples SQL latency from `slurmctld`'s scheduling loop; `sacctmgr` builds nested associations (`root -> genai -> llm-pretrain -> jsmith`); and `PurgeJobAfter`, `ArchiveJobs=yes`, and `MaxQueryTimeRange` prevent unbounded DB growth and lock-inducing `sacct` queries.
6. **Exercise 6 (Hardware Discovery `slurmd -C`, `cgroup.conf` v2, and Configless Mode):** Running `slurmd -C` prints the exact hardware topology so `slurm.conf` never exceeds physical RAM/cores (`invalid` / `Low RealMemory` drain); `ConstrainCores=yes`, `ConstrainRAMSpace=yes`, and `ConstrainDevices=yes` isolate CPUs, RAM, and GPUs in cgroups; and `SlurmctldParameters=enable_configless` (`slurmd --conf-server`) serves configs from `slurmctld`.
