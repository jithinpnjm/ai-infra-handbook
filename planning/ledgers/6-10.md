## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Verifying `gpu_env_check.py` and `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` Remapping):** `gpu_env_check_lab.py` verifies that `ConstrainDevices=yes` fences the job's cgroup to `/dev/nvidia3,/dev/nvidia7` and numbers them `0` and `1` relative to the allocation so `--gpus-per-task=1` assigns `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0` to Task 0 and `1` to Task 1.
2. **Exercise 2 (Writing `slurm.conf` and `gres.conf` for Mixed H100 + MIG Nodes):** Declare `GresTypes=gpu`, `AccountingStorageTRES=gres/gpu,gres/gpu:h100,gres/gpu:a100,gres/gpu:a100_3g.20gb`, `NodeName=gpu[01-16] Gres=gpu:h100:8`, `NodeName=mig[01-04] Gres=gpu:a100:1,gpu:a100_3g.20gb:2`, and `AutoDetect=nvml` in `gres.conf`, and verify with `slurmd -G`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Writing a Portable Multi-Node PyTorch Batch Script):** Specify `--nodes=2`, `--ntasks-per-node=4`, `--gres=gpu:4` (or `--gpus-per-node=4`), `--cpus-per-task=8`, `--mem=128G`, `--time=04:00:00`, and `--exclusive` so every node gets a symmetric 4-GPU rank layout and ample host RAM for `DataLoader` workers.
4. **Exercise 4 (NUMA Socket Affinity `Cores=` and NVLink Topology `Links=`):** `--gres-flags=enforce-binding` restricts CPU allocation to the socket listed in `Cores=` (`0-31` or `32-63`) for local PCIe/NUMA bandwidth, `--gres-flags=disable-binding` allows cross-socket CPUs when more cores are needed, and `Links=-1,18,18,18,0,0,0,0` guides `select/cons_tres` to allocate NVLink-connected GPU sets.
5. **Exercise 5 (Automated Multi-Node GPU Straggler and OOM Detection):** `parse_nvidia_smi_csv_and_detect_anomalies()` parses `nvidia-smi --query-gpu=utilization.gpu,memory.used,memory.total` CSV lines and flags GPU 1 (`32%` utilization, `78.0 / 80.0 GiB` VRAM) for both `< 50%` utilization and `> 95%` memory usage.
6. **Exercise 6 (Profiling `ncclAllReduce` vs Compute Bottlenecks on Slurm):** Wrap 5 training steps in `torch.profiler.profile(activities=[ProfilerActivity.CPU, ProfilerActivity.CUDA], ...)`, export `trace.json` only on `dist.get_rank() == 0`, inspect `cuda_time_total` for `ncclAllReduce` dominance, and use `NCCL_DEBUG=INFO`, `NCCL_DEBUG_SUBSYS=ALL`, and `TORCH_DISTRIBUTED_DEBUG=DETAIL` for transport diagnostics.
