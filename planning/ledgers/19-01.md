- Temperature alert thresholds differ across V16 ch. 2 (above 80 °C), V24 ch. 4 (above 85 °C in two minutes), and V20 ch. 6 (per-model thresholds). Chapter does not commit to a number; it says "sustained temperature above the model's limit".
- V24 ch. 06 is internally inconsistent about which MIG mix it assigns (walkthrough: 1×2g.20gb + 2×1g.10gb; summary: 3g.40gb + 2g.20gb + 1g.10gb). Chapter uses only the profile list, not the example allocation.
