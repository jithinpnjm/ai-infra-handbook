## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Planning Multi-Hop Slurm Upgrades):** `slurm_upgrade_path.py` computes `22.05 -> 23.11 -> 25.05 -> 26.05` because releases prior to `24.11` accept live upgrades from the previous 2 major releases (9-month cycle) while `24.11+` accepts the previous 3 major releases (6-month cycle); out-of-window upgrades fail immediately on daemon startup.
2. **Exercise 2 (Enforcing the Mandatory Daemon Upgrade Sequence and Rollback Backup Plan):** Upgrade `slurmdbd -> slurmctld -> slurmd -> clients/pyxis` after taking a stopped/consistent `StateSaveLocation` snapshot and `mysqldump --single-transaction`, because once a newer daemon starts and migrates state/DB schemas, reverting requires restoring both backups.
3. **Exercise 3 (Designing `slurmctld` and `slurmdbd` High Availability):** Configure `SlurmctldHost=ctl1` and `SlurmctldHost=ctl2` sharing `StateSaveLocation` (`SlurmctldTimeout=120`) and `DbdBackupHost` backed by Galera/replicated MySQL; running `slurmstepd` jobs survive both `slurmctld` failover and `slurmdbd` outages.
4. **Exercise 4 (Calculating Hierarchical `slurmd` Fan-Out `TreeWidth`):** `tree_fanout_nodes(16, 3)` computes $16 + 16^2 + 16^3 = 4,368$ nodes in 3 hops for `TreeWidth=16` (and $32 + 32^2 = 1,056$ in 2 hops / $33,824$ in 3 hops for `TreeWidth=32`).
5. **Exercise 5 (Tuning Kernel `sysctl` and `SchedulerParameters` for High-Throughput Clusters):** Raise `fs.file-max >= 388067`, `net.core.somaxconn >= 4096`, and `munged --num-threads=10`, lower `MinJobAge`, and set `SchedulerParameters=defer,max_rpc_cnt=128,default_queue_depth=100` to batch scheduling cycles.
6. **Exercise 6 (Deploying Configless Slurm with `sackd` and DNS `SRV` Records):** Enable `SlurmctldParameters=enable_configless` and publish `_slurmctld._tcp` DNS `SRV` records pointing to `ctl1:6817` and `ctl2:6817` so `slurmd` and login-node `sackd` fetch and cache configs automatically.
