## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Tables 13-2 & 13-3 reconciliation):** (a) GEMMs (`aten::matmul` `43.00 ms` + `aten::linear` `35.50 ms`) = `78.50 ms` (`56.9%` of `138.00 ms`), and routing (`dispatch` `18.70 ms` + `combine` `12.10 ms`) = `30.80 ms` (`22.3%`); (b) `64` experts each run `1` forward + `1` backward `matmul` (`128` calls) and `1` optimizer `add_` (`64` calls); (c) `nsys` uses `CUPTI` to project all `64` asynchronous `aten::add_` kernels launched between the host's single `optimizer_step` push/pop pair onto `1` NVTX instance (`19.2 ms`).
2. **Exercise 2 (Table 13-4 Roofline & Grace `perf`):** Baseline (`50%` FLOPS, `70%` mem BW, `60%` occ) stalls on HBM transfers (`Memory bound`), whereas Optimized (`85%` FLOPS, `40%` mem BW, `80%` occ) is `Compute bound`; Grace IPC is `1.58` vs `8.0` max IPC, and the `5` `perf report` hotspots are mitigated by `torch.compile` (`45.0%` `py::forward`), kernel fusion/custom GEMM (`20.5%` `aten::matmul`), `num_workers=4`/GPU + `persistent_workers=True` (`10.2%` `dataloader_iter_next`), `25 MB` `DDP` bucket tuning/overlap/compression (`8.7%` `ncclAllReduce`), and `pin_memory=True` + `prefetch_factor=4` or `8` + contiguous `WebDataset`/`Arrow`/`Parquet` shards (`5.3%` `read`).
3. **Exercise 3 (`torch.compile` speedup & Regional Compilation):** `248.0 - 173.0 = 75.0 ms` saved (`30.24%` reduction, `1.43x` speedup); `MoE` has hundreds of small/medium GEMMs and routing kernels to fuse whereas a single-GEMM dense model is already Tensor-Core bound (`< 10%`); `@torch.compiler.nested_compile_region` compiles `TransformerBlock` once and `TORCHINDUCTOR_CACHE_DIR` / `save_cache_artifacts()` persists compiled binaries across nodes.
4. **Exercise 4 (`PYTORCH_ALLOC_CONF` & FireworksAI shared pool):** (a) In `[512 MB, 1,024 MB]`, `512:2` creates `2` sub-buckets of `256 MB` (`[512, 768, 1024]`), rounding `600 MB` up to `768 MB`; (b) `expandable_segments:True,max_split_size_mb:128,garbage_collection_threshold:0.8` lowers reserved VRAM from `768 MB` to `600 MB` (`168 MB` = `21.88%` saved); (c) capturing in `[8, 4, 2, 1]` decreasing order allocates the max buffer on batch `8` first so batch sizes `4, 2, 1` reuse it with zero extra VRAM.
5. **Exercise 5 (`SimpleFSDP`, `AsyncTP`, `SuperOffload`, `MemPool`, `Symmetric Memory`):** (a) `SimpleFSDP` cuts `64.0 GB` by `28%` to `46.08 GB` and boosts `10,000 tok/s` by `69%` to `16,900 tok/s`; (b) `AsyncTP` reduces `16.0 ms` sequential (`12.0 + 4.0 ms`) by `3.4 ms` (`85%` of `4.0 ms`) to `12.6 ms` (`21.25%` reduction); (c) `SuperOffload` overlaps CPU `STV` + `GraceAdam` (`32` vector / `16` predicate `SVE` regs) over `NVLink-C2C`, `ncclMemAlloc` `MemPool` enables zero-copy `NVSwitch`/`SHARP` collectives over `1.8 TB/s` NVLink 5, and `PyTorch Symmetric Memory` enables one-sided `PGAS` `all_to_all` inside `CUDA Graphs`.
6. **Exercise 6 (`HTA`, Table 13-6 `MLPerf`, & `PyTorch HUD` CI gate):** (a) Rank `7` is the compute straggler (`21.0 ms` compute) and Rank `0` has the largest idle gap (`24.0 - (18.0 + 4.0) = 2.0 ms`); (b) Table 13-6 (`24.0 ms` total) has `forward = 10.5 ms` (`43.8%`), `backward = 9.0 ms` (`37.5%`, total compute = `19.5 ms` = `81.3%`), `allreduce = 4.0 ms` (`16.7%` = `1/6`), and `other = 0.5 ms` (`2.1%`); (c) `[9,400, 9,450, 9,800]` passes (only `2` consecutive `>= 5%` drops), while `[9,400, 9,450, 9,480]` fails (`3` consecutive runs `>= 5%` below `10,000 tok/s`).
