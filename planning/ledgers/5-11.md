## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Kubernetes DNS Record Types and `dnsPolicy` Modes, `KUR` Ch. 7 & `NK` Ch. 4–5):** `ClusterIP` resolves to 1 VIP, Headless resolves to all Ready Pod IPs, `StatefulSet` pods resolve at `pod-0.svc.ns.svc.cluster.local`, named ports resolve via `SRV`, and `ExternalName` returns a `CNAME`; `ClusterFirst` is the default, `Default` inherits node DNS, `ClusterFirstWithHostNet` gives `hostNetwork: true` pods cluster DNS, and `None` uses `dnsConfig`.
2. **Exercise 2 (The `ndots:5` Query Amplification Problem and Mitigations, `NK` Ch. 5 & `PK` Ch. 6):** `expand_dns_search_queries()` shows `api.huggingface.co` (2 dots < `ndots: 5`) triggers 8 queries (6 `NXDOMAIN`s across 3 search domains + 2 final answers), whereas a trailing dot (`api.huggingface.co.`) or `ndots: "2"` takes only 2 queries while preserving 1-dot `vllm.serving` search resolution.
3. **Exercise 3 (CoreDNS `Corefile` Architecture and Split-Horizon / Rewrites, `NK` Ch. 4):** CoreDNS plugin execution order is compiled into the binary; `detect_coredns_forwarding_loop()` catches `127.0.0.53` self-forwarding loops, stub server blocks (`corp.example.com:53`) forward internal zones, and `rewrite`/`hosts` (with `fallthrough`) alias names in-cluster.
4. **Exercise 4 (Scaling CoreDNS, `NodeLocal DNSCache` `169.254.20.10`, and the 5-Second UDP `conntrack` Race, `PK` Ch. 6):** Parallel `A`/`AAAA` UDP queries from one socket race `conntrack` DNAT insertion; `NodeLocal DNSCache` (`169.254.20.10` with `NOTRACK` iptables rules and TCP upstream forwarding) eliminates the race, while `cluster-proportional-autoscaler` scales CoreDNS replicas with node/core count.
5. **Exercise 5 (DNS-Based Rendezvous for Distributed Training and Negative Caching):** `resolve_headless_service_records()` shows `publishNotReadyAddresses: true` is mandatory when `StatefulSet`/Job workers must resolve `trainer-0.trainer` before readiness probes pass, and lowering `cache denial` TTL + retrying `getaddrinfo` avoids 30-second negative-cache startup stalls.
6. **Exercise 6 (Systematic 10-Step Cluster DNS Debugging):** Test `@<coredns-pod-ip>` vs `@10.96.0.10` (`kube-proxy`) vs `@169.254.20.10` (`NodeLocal`) vs `@<upstream>` with `dig`, verify `UDP` and `TCP` port `53` in `NetworkPolicy`, and inspect `coredns_dns_responses_total{rcode}`.
