## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Kaplan scales dataset tokens slower than parameters ($D_{\text{opt}} \propto C^{0.27}, N_{\text{opt}} \propto C^{0.73}$) whereas Chinchilla scales them equally ($D_{\text{opt}} \propto C^{0.50}, N_{\text{opt}} \propto C^{0.50}$), and Llama 3's combined math+code share is **`42.0%` in pre-training (`25% + 17%`), `36.08%` in SFT (`21.19% + 14.89%`), and `12.82%` in preference alignment (`5.89% + 6.93%`)**; (b) the curation funnel progresses **`10,000 -> 9,000 -> 6,000 -> 9,000 -> 11,000` final examples**, and `$10,000` at `$2.00`/example funds **`5,000` annotations**.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) For `b = 16` bands and `r = 8` rows (`M = 128`), $P(\text{candidate}) = 1 - (1 - J^8)^{16}$ equals **`0.000041` (`0.004%`) at `J = 0.20`, `0.060687` (`6.07%`) at `J = 0.50`, and `0.947102` (`94.71%`) at `J = 0.80`**; (b) repeating `0.1%` of data `100` times causes severe memorization that halves an `800M` model's effective capacity to **`400M`**, and benchmark decontamination is automated by appending evaluation sets to the deduplication index and removing colliding samples strictly from the training split.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) `NeMo Curator` cleans, deduplicates, and tokenizes sharded `JSONL`/`Parquet` files offline into contiguous memory-mappable **`.bin` token files and `.idx` byte-offset index files** (with $N$ pre-shuffled epoch copies) to eliminate runtime CPU tokenization and shuffling stalls; (b) reducing I/O wait from `30%` to `5%` yields a **`6.0x` (`30 / 5`) step speedup**, and `num_workers=8` with `prefetch_factor=4` and `batch_size=32` queues **`32` batches (`1,024` samples)** in pinned host RAM.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Llama 3.1 synthesized **`2.7 million` coding examples** via problem generation, CoT solution generation, parser/linter/AI-unit-test verification (where **`20%` of initially failing solutions self-corrected** via execution feedback), cross-language translation, and documentation back-translation; (b) under `Nemotron-4`'s swapped-order judge protocol, **`pair-1` (`(A, B)`) and `pair-3` (`(B, A)`) are retained** as consistent winners while **`pair-2` (`(A, A)`) and `pair-4` (`(B, B)`) are rejected** for position bias.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) `ChatML` wraps turns in `<|im_start|>{role}\n...\n<|im_end|>`, `Llama 3` uses `<|begin_of_text|><|start_header_id|>{role}<|end_header_id|>\n\n...<|eot_id|>`, and masking `4` prompt tokens before `[20, 21, 22]` gives `labels = [-100, -100, -100, -100, 20, 21, 22]`; (b) across `12` samples (`3,000` tokens) at `max_seq_length = 1,024`, packing cuts allocated tokens from **`12,288` (`24.41%` util) to `3,072` across `3` bins (`97.66%` util, `4.0x` batch reduction)**, sets `cu_seqlens = [0, 250, 550, 750, 1000]` on Bin 0, and cuts attention ops from `1,000,000` to `255,000` (**`3.92x` reduction**).
