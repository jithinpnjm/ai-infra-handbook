## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (`100 MB` Fetch & Lab 03 Decision Math):** (a) Traditional CPU-bounce copy takes `33.33 ms` (`33.43 ms` total with `0.10 ms` storage latency, `2` `PCIe` hops) vs `16.67 ms` (`16.77 ms` total, `1` `PCIe` hop) for `GDS` direct `DMA` (`2.0x` copy speedup); (b) the `5` trials (`0.265 s`, `0.250 s`, `0.248 s`, `0.247 s`, `0.246 s`) average **`0.251 s`** (**`398.0 MB/s`**), so `960.0 MB/s` `GDS` yields a **`+141.2%`** gain (`> 20.0%` gate -> **`DEPLOY GDS`**).
2. **Exercise 2 (`cuFile` 7-Step Lifecycle & Path Resolution):** (a) `gds-p2p-dma` (`nvidia-fs` stats increment, `__memcpy_avx2` `False`); (b) `gds-root-complex-degraded` (`nvidia-fs` stats increment, `__memcpy_avx2` `False`, degraded by `PHB`); (c) `compat-posix-fallback` (`nvidia-fs` stats `False`, `__memcpy_avx2` `True`); (d) `compat-posix-fallback` (`FUSE` cannot do kernel `O_DIRECT` `GDS`); (e) `error-io-rejected` (`allow_compat_mode=False` fails loudly when `nvidia-fs.ko` is absent).
3. **Exercise 3 (`4 KiB` `O_DIRECT` & `8 KiB` Lustre Alignment):** `4,100 % 4,096 = 4`, `1,000,000 % 4,096 = 576`, and `0x7F0000100100 % 4,096 = 256` all fail `4 KiB` alignment; rounding `4,100` down gives **`4,096` bytes** and rounding `4,100 + 1,000,000 = 1,004,100` up to `1,007,616` gives an aligned read size of **`1,003,520` bytes**; on `8 KiB` (`8,192 B`) Lustre, a `4,096 B` offset (`8,192 B` size) realigns down to offset **`0`** and size **`16,384` bytes**.
4. **Exercise 4 (`gdsio` Table 5-1 & CPU Efficiency Math):** (a) Switching from `-x 2` (`8.0 GB/s`, `1.25 ms`) to `-x 0` (`9.6 GB/s`, `1.00 ms`) yields **`+20.0%`** throughput and **`-20.0%`** latency; (b) CPU utilization drops by **`60.0` percentage points** (`85.0% -> 25.0%`); (c) CPU efficiency rises from **`0.0941 GB/s` per `%` CPU** (`8.0 / 85`) to **`0.3840 GB/s` per `%` CPU** (`9.6 / 25`), a **`4.08x`** improvement.
5. **Exercise 5 (`/etc/cufile.json` Linter):** `33,554,432 KiB = 32.0 GiB`; the three defects are `allow_compat_mode: true` (masks silent POSIX fallback), `use_poll_mode: true` with `poll_mode_max_size_kb: 4` (ineffective on `1,024 KiB` I/Os), and `posix_unaligned_kb: true` (enables hidden bounce buffers); fix by setting `allow_compat_mode: false`, `use_poll_mode: false`, `posix_unaligned_kb: false`, `nvtx: true`, and `cufile_stats: 3`.
6. **Exercise 6 (Blackwell `800 GB/s` Decompression & DeepSeek `3FS`):** (a) Uncompressed `4.0 GB` read at `50.0 GB/s` takes **`80.0 ms`** vs **`32.0 ms`** compressed (`1.6 GB`) + **`5.0 ms`** hardware unpack at `800.0 GB/s` = **`37.0 ms`** total (**`108.11 GB/s`** effective, **`2.16x`** speedup); (b) DeepSeek's `68`-node `3FS` cluster has **`680` drives** (**`10,880 TB`** raw capacity) and delivers **`6.6 TB/s` + `1.4 TB/s` = `8.0 TB/s`** combined (`7.3 TB/s` peak), which is **`6.0x`** faster than `Ceph` (`1.1 TB/s`).
