## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Observability Methodologies `RED`/`USE` and the Three Core Kubernetes Exporters):** `RED` (Rate, Errors, Duration) measures request-driven services while `USE` (Utilization, Saturation, Errors) measures hardware resources; `cAdvisor` reports live container cgroup usage, `kube-state-metrics` reports declarative API object specs/statuses, and `node-exporter` reports host OS/hardware telemetry.
2. **Exercise 2 (Prometheus Operator CRDs, `relabelings` vs. `metricRelabelings`, and the `release` Label Trap, `PK` Ch. 9):** `ServiceMonitor` selects `Service`s (requiring `release: kps` unless `serviceMonitorSelectorNilUsesHelmValues: false`), `relabelings` transforms discovery metadata before scraping, and `metricRelabelings` drops high-cardinality or unused series after scraping before TSDB ingestion.
3. **Exercise 3 (SLO-Driven Alerting `Multi-Window, Multi-Burn-Rate`, `Alertmanager` Inhibition, and `Watchdog`, `PK` Ch. 9):** `evaluate_slo_multiwindow_burn_rate()` checks that both the long window (`1h` / `6h`) and short window (`5m` / `30m`) exceed `14.4 * (1 - SLO)` or `6.0 * (1 - SLO)` so alerts fire only on real budget burn and clear within 5 minutes of recovery; `Watchdog` fires continuously to an external dead-man's-switch receiver to prove the alerting pipeline is alive.
4. **Exercise 4 (GPU Fleet Telemetry `DCGM` Standard vs. Profiling Metrics and Diagnosing Slow Training Jobs):** `DCGM_FI_DEV_GPU_UTIL` can report 100% while SMs spin in a NCCL wait loop, whereas `DCGM_FI_PROF_PIPE_TENSOR_ACTIVE` measures actual Tensor Core math pipeline duty cycle; per-node heatmaps of SM clock, temperature, PCIe width, and RDMA throughput isolate the straggler node.
5. **Exercise 5 (Logging `Fluent Bit` -> `Loki`, Kubernetes `Events` `1h` TTL, Audit Logs, and `OpenTelemetry` Tracing, `PK` Ch. 9):** Keep Loki stream labels low-cardinality (`namespace`, `app`, `container`), export Kubernetes `Events` before their 1-hour TTL expires, log `secrets` at `Metadata` audit level, and use OTel `Exemplars` (`trace_id`) and Tail Sampling to link p99 latency spikes to slow traces.
6. **Exercise 6 (Controlling Prometheus Cardinality, Scaling Long-Term Storage, and Showback Models, `PK` Ch. 9):** `estimate_prometheus_series_cardinality()` shows how a 50,000-value `user_id` label multiplies 10,000 series to 500,000,000 series; `Thanos` / `Mimir` deduplicate HA Prometheus replicas over S3 storage; and `compute_showback_requests_vs_consumption()` contrasts Requests-based showback with Consumption-based (`max(request, usage)`) showback.
