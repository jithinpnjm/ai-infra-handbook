## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** `RoCEv1` (`0x8915`) has `74 B` non-payload overhead (`18 B L2 + 40 B GRH + 12 B BTH + 4 B ICRC`, `98.23%` efficiency at `4096 B`), `RoCEv2-IPv4` (`UDP 4791`) has `62 B` (`18 B L2 + 20 B IPv4 + 8 B UDP + 12 B BTH + 4 B ICRC`, saving `12 B` by replacing `40 B GRH` with `28 B IPv4+UDP`, raising payload efficiency from `94.29%` at `1024 B` to `98.51%` at `4096 B`), and `RoCEv2-IPv6` has `82 B` (`98.04%` at `4096 B`).
2. **Exercise 2:** `ipv4_to_rocev2_gid("10.20.4.15")` returns `"0000:0000:0000:0000:0000:ffff:0a14:040f"`; `ib_write_bw -d mlx5_0 -x 2` failed with `Failed to modify QP to RTR` because `-x 2` selected `eth0.100` on one host while the peer's `INDEX 2` mapped to a different VLAN or `v1` (`fe80::`) entry.
3. **Exercise 3:** `DSCP 26` with `ECN = 10` (`ECT(0)`) is `0x6a` (`106`) and with `ECN = 11` (`CE`) is `0x6b` (`107`); the receiver's `CNP` uses `IB BTH` opcode `129` (`0x81`) and `ECN = 01`; at `40 KB` (`<= 64 KB` `XON`) the queue sends `PFC XON` (`timer = 0`), at `500 KB` (`150..1500 KB`) it marks `ECN = 11` without `PFC`, and at `3200 KB` (`>= 3000 KB` `XOFF`) it sends `PFC XOFF` (`timer = 65535`).
4. **Exercise 4:** The `6` Clos congestion points are `Local leaf south-facing`, `Leaf-to-spine`, `Spine-to-leaf`, `Leaf-to-server egress`, `Spine-to-super-spine`, and `Super-spine-to-spine`; spine load balancing never engages on local leaf switching (`Point 1`) and cannot split a single destination NIC link (`Point 4`); the `PFC Watchdog` executes `Detection`, `Mitigation` (dropping queued/new packets and pause frames so `SpineA` does not pause `LeafA`/`LeafB`), and `Restoration`.
5. **Exercise 5:** At `400 Gbps` and `20.48 us` RTT (`BDP = 1,024,000 B = 250` packets of `4096 B`), `Go-Back-N` discards and retransmits all `250` in-flight packets for `1` drop vs `1` packet under `Selective Repeat` (`250x` amplification); with `1` straggler at `1 s` among `10` `1 ms` tasks, serial `JCT = 1.010 s` and parallel `JCT = 1.000 s` (`999 ms` barrier wait).
6. **Exercise 6:** `SFC` (`IEEE 802.1Qdw`) signals the source directly from the congested switch in `2.0 us` vs `14.0 us` (`6.0 + 8.0 us`) for `ECN + CNP` (`7.0x` speedup), trimming the payload and reversing IP `src`/`dst` for flow-level throttling without class `HoL` blocking; `CSIG` (`draft-ravi-ippm-csig-01`) inserts a Layer 2.5 tag carrying `3` signal types (`min(ABW/C)`, `min(ABW)`, `max(PD)`) and `4` locator fields (`Capacity`, `Stage`, `Device ID`, `Link ID uplink/downlink`).
