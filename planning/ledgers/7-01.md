# Claim Ledger: 7.1 BMCs, IPMI and out-of-band management

## DMTF Redfish Specification (DSP0266 v1.21.0) Claims
- **BMC Definition (§3.1.1):** An embedded device or service—typically an independent microprocessor or system on a chip (SoC) with its own firmware—that performs out-of-band monitoring and management.
- **Design Tenets & Console Scope (§5.1–5.5):** Separation of protocol and data model; single Service Root `/redfish/v1/` with `@odata.id` hypermedia discovery; *"interoperable does not mean identical"* (§5.4); KVM and serial console (§5.5) are exposed as discovery metadata rather than carried over REST.
- **Security & Transport (§13.1, §13.3):** HTTPS over TCP (`443`) with TLS 1.2 or later required; services must support both HTTP Basic (over HTTPS) and Redfish session login (`POST /redfish/v1/SessionService/Sessions` returning `X-Auth-Token`).

## `SCL` Chapter 10 (Remote Management) & `BCM 11 Admin Manual` Claims
- **OpenIPMI & `ipmitool` (`SCL` Ch. 10):** Local in-band KCS access uses `ipmi_msghandler`, `ipmi_si`, and `ipmi_devintf` (`/dev/ipmi0`). Remote access uses `-I lanplus` (IPMI 2.0 RMCP+ over UDP port `623`) vs legacy `-I lan` (IPMI 1.5), and `-f pass_file` instead of `-P`. User Slot `3` on Channel `1` uses privilege level `4` (Administrator). `SOL` (`ipmitool sol activate`) uses `~.` to disconnect (`~~.` over one SSH hop) and requires `ttyS1` (`115200n8`) + `agetty`. `conman` logs consoles and pairs with `powerman` for power control.
- **BCM 11 BMC Network & Credential Auditing (`bcm11-admin` §3.7):** Default BMC subnet is `10.148.0.0/16` (`bmcnet`); `addinterface -n node001..node150 bmc ipmi0 bmcnet 10.148.0.1` creates `150` BMC interfaces; `credentialsoverview --inverse` uses per-session salted SHA-256 fingerprints (`code/part07/cred_audit.py` and `oob_cred_lab.py` 3/3 pass).

## DGX H100/HGX & `nvidia-devops` (`volume-05/ch3` & `volume-10/01`) Claims
- **Dual-Tray `8U` DGX H100 Anatomy (`10.2 kW` max, `6 x 3.3 kW` PSUs):** Host Motherboard Tray (`2` CPUs, `2 TB` DDR5 RAM, ASPEED `AST2600` primary BMC on `5V_STBY`/`12V_STBY`, `2x` BlueField-3 DPUs), HGX GPU Baseboard Tray (`8x` SXM5 GPUs at `700 W` TDP, `4x` NVSwitches at `900 GB/s` per GPU across `18` NVLink lanes, and Satellite Management Controller), and PCIe Gen5 (`32 GT/s`) I/O Tray (`8x` ConnectX-7 `400G` HCAs).
- **`nvsm` & Out-of-Band Firmware/BMC Recovery:** `nvsm show health`, `nvsm show gpus`, and `nvsm dump health` provide in-band diagnostics; `ipmitool mc reset cold` resets a hung BMC (`100%` fan fail-safe) over local KCS; `60`-second PDU power drain resets standby rails.
