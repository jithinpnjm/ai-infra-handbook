## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Serial vs Parallel Tail-Latency & JCT Math):** (a) For $n = 10$ (`1 ms` normal, `1,000 ms` `taskX`), serial JCT is `1.010 s` (`1,010.0 ms` book formula `1 s + 10 * 1 ms`) and `1,009.0 ms` (`1.009 s` strict sum); (b) in parallel execution, the `9` fast GPUs wait `999.0 ms` for a `1,000.0 ms` (`1.0 s`) JCT (`1,000.0x` slowdown); (c) `2.0 ms` compute vs `50.0 ms` sync yields a `4.0%` compute-to-sync ratio (`3.85%` of the `52.0 ms` step).
2. **Exercise 2 (Compute-Communication Overlap & `DP` vs `DDP`):** (a) `10.0 ms + 12.0 ms = 22.0 ms` serial vs $\max(10.0, 12.0) = 12.0\text{ ms}$ ideal full overlap (`45.45%` reduction); (b) `50%` overlap on `40.0 ms` compute + `60.0 ms` comm cuts step time from `100.0 ms` to `70.0 ms` (`30.0%` faster) and lifts SM busy from `60%` to `90%`; (c) `DDP` (`30.00 ms`) is `33.33%` faster (`1.5x`) than `nn.DataParallel` (`45.00 ms`).
3. **Exercise 3 (Ring, Tree, and `SHARP` / `NVLS` Math):** (a) For $B = 100\text{ MB}, n = 8$: chunk $B/n = 12.5\text{ MB}$, Ring `ReduceScatter` = `87.5 MB` (`7` steps), Ring `AllReduce` = `175.0 MB` (`14` steps); (b) `SHARP`/`NVLS` `ReduceScatter` receives $B/n = 12.5\text{ MB}$ per GPU, a `1/(n-1) = 1/7` volume reduction vs Ring; (c) at $n = 1,024$, Ring `AllReduce` takes `2,046` steps (`O(N)`) vs `20` steps (`O(log N)`) for binary Tree, and `SHARP` reduces endpoint `ReduceScatter` volume by `1/1,023`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Multi-Node Pitfalls & Llama 3 405B Interruptions):** (a) On `400.0 MB` over `800 Gb/s` IB, `Gloo` achieves `2.0 GB/s` (`200.00 ms`) vs `NCCL` `100.0 GB/s` (`4.00 ms`, `50.0x` speedup); (b) moving `48.0 ms` `init_process_group` outside the loop cuts step time from `48.5 ms` to `0.5 ms` (`98.97%` reduction); (c) `16 * 8 = 128` violates the `64`-connection socket ceiling; (d) Meta's `54`-day run had `419` interruptions across `18` categories (`148` Faulty GPU = `30.1%`, and `35 + 7 + 7 = 49` network/NIC/watchdog events).
5. **Exercise 5 (RoCEv2 Headers, Load-Balancing Pyramid & `DCQCN`):** Static 5-tuple `SLB` fails because RoCEv2 pins all packets to `UDP` port `4791` with inner `QP`/`PSN` hidden in `IB BTH`; the 4-tier pyramid progresses from `SLB` (5-tuple) -> `DLB` (local link/buffer load) -> `GLB` (`NNH` spine path quality) -> `sDLB` (`BTH` Read/Write opcode elephant detection + packet spraying), backed by `DCQCN` (`ECN = 11` switch marking -> destination `CNP` with `IP ECN = 01` -> source NIC per-flow rate cut + hop-by-hop `PFC` pause quanta).
6. **Exercise 6 (`1,024`-GPU Non-Blocking Fat-Tree Sizing):** On `64`-port switches (`32` downlinks / `32` uplinks per leaf), `1` Leaf Group of `8` rail-aligned Leaves serves `32` servers (`256` GPUs), so `128` servers (`1,024` GPUs) require `4` Leaf Groups = `32` Leaf switches (`1,024` downlinks, `1,024` uplinks) and `1,024 / 64 = 16` Spine switches (`1:1` non-blocking).
