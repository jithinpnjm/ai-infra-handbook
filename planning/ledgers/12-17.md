## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) On `n = 8` GPUs (`512 MiB` = `536,870,912 B` in `9,871.4 µs`), `algbw = 54.39 GB/s`; `AllReduce` has factor `1.75` (`busbw = 95.18 GB/s`), `ReduceScatter`/`AllGather`/`AllToAll` each have factor `0.875` (`busbw = 47.59 GB/s`), and `Broadcast` has factor `1.0` (`busbw = 54.39 GB/s`); (b) for `1 GiB` (`1,073,741,824 B`), single-node `8`-GPU H100 (`2,480.0 µs`, factor `1.75`) achieves `algbw = 432.96 GB/s` and `busbw = 757.68 GB/s`, while `64`-GPU multi-node NDR400 (`5,850.0 µs`, factor `1.96875`) achieves `algbw = 183.55 GB/s` and `busbw = 361.36 GB/s`.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Tier 1 intra-node PCIe link downtrained (`x16` $\rightarrow$ `x8` at `28.0 GB/s` vs `50–60 GB/s`); (b) Tier 2a physical InfiniBand/RoCE link downtrained (`4x` $\rightarrow$ `2x` lanes at `195.0 Gbps` vs `390.0 Gbps`); (c) Tier 2b GPUDirect RDMA bounce-buffer fallback (`nvidia_peermem` missing, PCIe `ACS` enabled, or `SYS` cross-socket locality); (d) Tier 4 framework bucket fragmentation (`256 KB` `AllReduce` payloads; increase DDP `bucket_cap_mb` from `25 MB` to `50 MB`).
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) Binary-search bisection tests `[0..31]` (pass) $\rightarrow$ `[32..47]` (fail) $\rightarrow$ `[32..39]` (pass) $\rightarrow$ `[40..43]` (fail) $\rightarrow$ `[40..41]` (fail) $\rightarrow$ `[40, ref]` (pass) $\implies$ `dgx-41` isolated in $\lceil \log_2 64 \rceil = 6$ split steps (`7` total runs vs `64` linear runs, saving `58` runs); (b) test each rail via `NCCL_IB_HCA==mlx5_<k>:1` (`k = 0..7`) with `all_reduce_perf` and `ib_write_bw -d mlx5_<k> --use_cuda=<k>`.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) `8`-GPU speedup is `6.5x`, scaling efficiency is `6.5 / 8 = 81.25%`, and predicted `16`-GPU speedup is `16 * 0.8125 = 13.0x`; (b) at `52%` efficiency on `64` GPUs, effective compute is `33.28` (`~33`) GPUs and wasted compute is `30.72` (`~31`) GPUs, wasting `10,321.92` GPU-hours and `$46,448.64` (`~$50,000`) over `336` hours at `$4.50`/GPU-hr; (c) `155 ms / 33 ms = 4.70x` speedup (`29.36%` efficiency) with `18.15 ms` communication (`55%`) and `14.85 ms` compute (`45%`), and Amdahl's Law with `10%` serial work caps speedup at `8.77x` on `64` GPUs and `10.0x` on $\infty$ GPUs.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) Post-collective timing measures the barrier completion time (`1,250.5 ms` on all ranks); pre-collective timing shows a `1250.5 / 124.8 = 10.02x` imbalance where GPU `2` is idle (`5%` SM, `40 W`), proving a software/dataloader starvation issue rather than GPU hardware degradation; (b) `70B` H100 `MFU` rises from `1,344 / 7,912 = 16.99%` (`~17%` at `3,200` tok/s) to `3,780 / 7,912 = 47.78%` (`~48%` at `9,000` tok/s); (c) moving `48.0 ms` `init_process_group`/`destroy_process_group` out of the loop cuts step time from `48.5 ms` to `0.5 ms`, a `98.97%` reduction.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) At `16` tokens, `decode_ms = 150.0 ms` and `e2e_latency = 195.0 ms` (`TTFT` share `23.08%`); at `201` tokens, `decode_ms = 2,000.0 ms` and `e2e_latency = 2,045.0 ms` (`TTFT` share `2.20%`, `decode` share `97.80%`); (b) System A passes (`P99 = 110.0 ms <= 200.0 ms`), whereas System B's `5%` outlier tail at `500.0 ms` pushes `P99` to `500.0 ms` (`2.5x` the `200.0 ms` SLA) despite its `80.0 ms` average.
