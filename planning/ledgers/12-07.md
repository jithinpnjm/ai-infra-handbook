## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) `sm-b` (`GUID 0x0002c903004a1b20`, `priority 14`) wins `SMINFO_MASTER` (`state 3`) over `sm-a` (`GUID 0x08c0eb0300f1a2c3`, `priority 14`) via the lowest-`GUID` tie-breaker (`0x0002c903004a1b20 < 0x08c0eb0300f1a2c3`), while `sm-a` and `sm-c` (`priority 8`) enter `SMINFO_STANDBY` (`state 2`); (b) raising `sm-c` to `priority 15` makes `sm-c` win `SMINFO_MASTER` outright on priority (`15 > 14`).
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Logical states advance `1: Down -> 2: Initialize (VL15 SMP only, Base LID 0) -> 3: Armed (LID assigned, VL15 only) -> 4: Active (VL0..VL14 user RDMA enabled after LFT programming)`; (b) `hop_count = 3`, `Initial Path = (0, 1, 7, 14)`, `Return Path = (0, 18, 4, 2)`, forward `hop_pointer` sequence `(1, 2, 3)` (`D = 0`), and reverse `hop_pointer` sequence `(3, 2, 1)` (`D = 1`).
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) Unicast `LID`s span `1..0xBFFF` (`2^15 + 2^14 - 1 = 49,151`), `Base LID = 16, LMC = 2` yields `4` `LID`s `(16, 17, 18, 19)`, and `Base LID = 64, LMC = 3` yields `8` `LID`s `(64..71)`; (b) multiplying by `4` bytes/word gives `21,474,836,480` B (port `5`), `21,489,234,432` B (port `6`), `1,073,741,824` B (port `7`), and `1,081,654,272` B (port `8`), a `20.01:1` imbalance with hot ports `(5, 6)` and idle ports `(7, 8)`.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) `6,400 Gbps / 3,200 Gbps = 2:1` oversubscription gives `200 Gbps` (`25 GB/s`) per node and `2 GB / 25 GB/s = 80.0 ms` per `AllReduce` step vs `400 Gbps` (`50 GB/s`) and `40.0 ms` at `1:1`; (b) losing `1` of `2` spines drops active uplinks from `8` to `4` (`1,600 Gbps`), doubling oversubscription to `4:1` (`100 Gbps` = `12.5 GB/s` per node, `160.0 ms` per `2 GB` step).
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) `1` row (`256` GPUs) = `32` servers, `8` leaves, `4` spines; `2` rows (`512` GPUs) = `64` servers, `16` leaves, `8` spines; `8` rows (`2,048` GPUs) = `256` servers, `64` leaves, and `32` fixed `64 x 400G` spines (or `8` chassis `256 x 400G` spines); (b) with `32` `64 x 400G` super-spines (`2,048` ports), `2` blocks (`2,048` GPUs) = `1:1`, `4` blocks (`4,096` GPUs) = `2:1`, `8` blocks (`8,192` GPUs) = `4:1`, and `14` blocks (`14,336` GPUs, `448` leaves, `448` spines) = `7:1`.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) Losing `1` leaf in `ROD` isolates `0` servers (`32` servers lose `1/8` capacity), whereas losing `1` leaf in `RUD` completely isolates `4` servers (`32` GPUs), and `Multi-Planar` (`800G -> 4 x 200G` across `FP1..FP4`) limits a full plane failure to `25%` bandwidth loss; (b) `eBGP` unnumbered (`RFC 5549`, shared Spine ASN `65001`), `BGP DPF` session coloring, `RIFT` (`RFC 9692`), `IS-IS` `Flex Algo` (`128..130`), and `SRv6` `16`-bit `uSID` provide loop-free multipathing and combine with `EVPN-VXLAN` `MAC-VRF`/`RT5` + `MIG` `VLAN 1..7` for tenant isolation.
