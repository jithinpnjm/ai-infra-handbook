## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) On `2x H100 80GB` GPUs in throughput mode, NIM selects **`tensorrt_llm-h100-fp8-tp2-throughput` (`tensorrt_llm` pre-built engine)**; on `1x L40S 48GB` without a pre-built L40S TensorRT-LLM engine, it selects **`vllm-generic-bf16-tp1-lora` (`vllm` fallback, enforcing `TensorRT-LLM > vLLM > SGLang`)**; and on `1x A100 40GB` with `NIM_MODEL_PROFILE=tensorrt_llm-h100-fp8-tp2-throughput`, startup **fails immediately with `override_hardware_mismatch`**; (b) `14 + 2 + 4 + 2 = 22.0 GB` required GPU memory (`18.0 GB` free margin on a `40.0 GB` A100), and `ceil(100.0 / 50.0) = 2` replicas (`2` GPUs).
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Downloading `140,000 MB` at `116.67 MB/s` takes `1,200.0 s` (`20.0 min`) plus `5.0 s` engine init = **`1,205.0 seconds` (`20.08 minutes`)**, compared to **`2.0 seconds`** for a `20.0 MB` Nginx image; (b) reading `140.0 GB` from the NVMe `ReadWriteMany` PVC at `3.5 GB/s` takes `40.0 s` plus `5.0 s` init = **`45.0 seconds`**, delivering a **`26.78x` startup speedup**.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) Hosting `10` dedicated fine-tuned 8B pods requires **`10 * 18.0 GB = 180.0 GB` of GPU VRAM across `10` GPUs**; (b) consolidating all `10` adapters (`64.0 MB` each) into a single Multi-LoRA NIM pod with `NIM_MAX_GPU_LORAS=8` (`8 * 64.0 MB = 0.512 GB` GPU LoRA pool + `16.0 GB` base weights + `2.0 GB` runtime) consumes **`18.512 GB` on `1` GPU**, saving **`161.488 GB` or `89.72%` of GPU VRAM**.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) The six NeMo Guardrails stages sum to `45 + 15 + 30 + 100 + 50 + 10 = 250.0 ms` (`205.0 ms` added overhead, **`5.56x` latency multiplier** over the `45.0 ms` base model), while `NemoEvaluator` in Lab 03 recorded an **`18.7%` perplexity reduction (`18.7 -> 15.2`), `+12.9%` ROUGE-1 gain (`0.31 -> 0.35`), and `+16.7%` BLEU gain (`0.24 -> 0.28`)**; (b) against the `180.0 ms` baseline, `195.0 ms` (`+8.3% <= 200.0 ms`) triggers **`PROMOTE_TO_PRODUCTION`**, `252.0 ms` (`+40.0% >= 250.0 ms`) triggers **`PAUSE_CANARY_WARNING`**, and `305.0 ms` (`+69.4% >= 300.0 ms`) triggers **`AUTOMATIC_ROLLBACK` (`helm rollback`)**.
