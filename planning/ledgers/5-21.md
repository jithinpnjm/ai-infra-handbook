## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (The Kubernetes PKI Hierarchy, Certificate Renewal, and the CSR API, `PK` Ch. 10):** `kubeadm` separates Cluster CA, `etcd` CA, and `front-proxy` CA (`10y`) plus `sa.key`/`sa.pub` (`1y` leaf certs renewed by `kubeadm certs renew all`); `kubelet` client CSRs auto-approve whereas `kubelet-serving` CSRs require SAN validation (`kubelet-csr-approver`); and client certificates have no CRL/OCSP revocation.
2. **Exercise 2 (Identity Federation: OIDC, `kube2iam`/`kiam` vs. `IRSA`, and `SPIFFE`/`SPIRE`, `PK` Ch. 10):** `kube2iam` and `kiam` (`spec.nodeName` bypass) rely on node metadata proxying, whereas `IRSA` uses projected OIDC JWTs with `sts:AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity`; `match_spire_workload_entry()` verifies `spire-agent` workload attestation against `k8s:ns`, `k8s:sa`, `k8s:pod-label`, and `k8s:container-image` selectors.
3. **Exercise 3 (Admission Control Pipeline: `NamespaceLifecycle`, `controller-runtime` `PatchResponseFromRaw`, and `ValidatingAdmissionPolicy`, `PK` Ch. 8):** `NamespaceLifecycle` blocks creations in `Terminating` namespaces, `controller-runtime` `PatchResponseFromRaw` auto-generates `JSONPatch` diffs from mutated Go structs, and `ValidatingAdmissionPolicy` (`evaluate_vap_and_gatekeeper_policies()`) runs CEL validations in-process with zero webhook latency.
4. **Exercise 4 (Pod Security Standards, `amicontained`, `RuntimeClass`, and `kube-bench`, `KUR` Ch. 19):** `evaluate_pod_security_standard()` enforces `privileged`, `baseline` (no `hostNetwork`/`hostPID`/`hostIPC`/`hostPath`/`privileged`), and `restricted` (`runAsNonRoot`, `allowPrivilegeEscalation: false`, `drop: ["ALL"]`, `seccompProfile`); `kubectl label --dry-run=server --overwrite ns --all pod-security.kubernetes.io/enforce=baseline` audits live pods without enforcing.
5. **Exercise 5 (Policy Engines: `Kyverno` vs. `OPA Gatekeeper`, `KUR` Ch. 20 & `PK` Ch. 8):** Kyverno validates, mutates, generates default `NetworkPolicy`/quotas per namespace, and verifies Cosign image signatures (`mutateDigest: true`), while OPA Gatekeeper uses Rego `ConstraintTemplate`s, `Constraint`s (`enforcementAction: dryrun|warn|deny`), `Config` (`data.inventory` sync), and `Assign` mutations.
6. **Exercise 6 (Designing a 15-Point Multi-Tenant GPU Cluster Guardrail Set and Troubleshooting):** Layer PSA, VAP/Kyverno/Gatekeeper, and RBAC to block `spec.nodeName`, enforce queue/priority labels, cap GPUs, and verify signed registries; fix webhook `caBundle` drift via `cert-manager` `cainjector`, approve `kubelet-serving` CSRs, and keep old `sa.pub` during `sa.key` rotation.
