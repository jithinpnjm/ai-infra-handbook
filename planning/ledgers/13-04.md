## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) `260 KiB` file: **`260 KiB` (`266,240` bytes) on `MDT` (`DoM`)**, **`0` bytes on `OST`s (`0` active `OST`s)**; (b) `512 MiB` shard: **`1 MiB` on `MDT` (`DoM`)**, **`511 MiB` on `OST`s** (`63 MiB` in Component 1 on `1` `OST` + `448 MiB` in Component 2 across **`4` active `OST`s**); (c) `10 GiB` checkpoint: **`1 MiB` on `MDT` (`DoM`)**, **`10,239 MiB` on `OST`s** across **all `24` active `OST`s** (`6 GiB` in Component 3 striped across `24` `OST`s).
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) `open` rate is `950,000 / 60 = 15,833.33 opens/sec`, `getattr` rate is `1,900,000 / 60 = 31,666.67 ops/sec` (`47,500` ops/sec combined, or `63,333` ops/sec including `950,000` closes), and average `open` latency is **`45.2 ms`** (`9.04x` above the `5.0 ms` healthy threshold -> `METADATA_SATURATED`); (b) repackaging `10,000,000` files into `100` shards (`100,000` files/shard) reduces `open()` calls by **`100,000x`** (`10,000,000 -> 100`), taking **`0.0005 seconds` (`0.5 ms`)** across `4` `MDT`s (`200,000` ops/sec).
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) Under **DNE Phase 1** (`lfs mkdir -i 0`), a single directory resides on **`1` active `MDT`** (`50,000` ops/sec capacity), so opening `200,000` files takes **`4.0 seconds`**; (b) under **DNE Phase 2** (`lfs setdirstripe -c 4`), filenames hash across all **`4` active `MDT`s** (`200,000` ops/sec aggregate capacity), reducing open time to **`1.0 second`** (`4x` faster).
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Untuned (`8` RPCs, `256` pages = **`1.0 MiB` RPC**, **`8.0 MiB` in flight** per `OST`): at `4,000 us` RTT, single-`OST` BDP is **`2,097.15 MB/s`**, capping `8` `OST`s at **`16,777.22 MB/s` (`~16.78 GB/s`)**; (b) Tuned (`64` RPCs, `1024` pages = **`4.0 MiB` RPC**, **`256.0 MiB` in flight** per `OST`): single-`OST` BDP rises to `67,108.86 MB/s`, saturating all `8` NVMe `OST`s at `8 * 6,000 =` **`48,000.0 MB/s` (`48.0 GB/s`)**.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) Single `OST` (`stripe_count = 1` at `800 MB/s`): **`0.8 GB/s`**, **`625.0 seconds`**; (b) Unaligned `N-to-1` shared file (`12` `OST`s at `1,000 MB/s`, `25%` lock efficiency): **`3.0 GB/s`**, **`166.67 seconds`**; (c) Sharded `N-to-N` (`PyTorch DCP`): **`12.0 GB/s`** and **`41.67 seconds`** across `24` `500 MB/s` `OST`s (`4x` faster than unaligned `N-to-1`, `15x` faster than single-`OST`), and **`560.0 GB/s`** / **`10.0 seconds`** for a `5,600 GB` checkpoint on the `4`-SU SuperPOD Enhanced tier.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) With `N_threads = 120 / 6 = 20`, baseline per-`OST` RAM is `400 + 600 + 1.5 * 20 =` **`1,030.0 MB`** and recommended `OSS` RAM is `2 + 1 * 6 =` **`8.0 GB`**; (b) fill skew is `98% - 60% =` **`38.0%`** (remediate with `lfs find /lustre/dataset --ost ai_fs-OST0000 -type f -print0 | lfs migrate -0 -c 6`); (c) `gdsio` `-x 0` (`9.6 GB/s`, `1.00 ms`) vs `-x 2` (`8.0 GB/s`, `1.25 ms`) yields a **`+20.0%` throughput gain** and a **`-20.0%` latency reduction**.
