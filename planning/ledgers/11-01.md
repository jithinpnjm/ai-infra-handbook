## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Two-Path Agreement Model across 4 nodes):** `gpu-node-07` passes both `Bound?` and `CudaInit?`; `gpu-node-11` fails `Bound?` at Layer 4 (`nvidia.com/gpu` absent from `status.capacity`, plugin `CrashLoopBackOff` `14` restarts); `gpu-node-22` fails at Layer 2 (`Ready` in K8s, but empty `lsmod` and Secure Boot `error -1`); `gpu-node-30` passes `Bound?` (`Allocatable: 4`) but fails `CudaInit?` at Layer 3 (`CreateContainerError: /dev/nvidia0: no such device or address`).
2. **Exercise 2 (32-GPU Pool Fragmentation):** (a) `8 - (4 + 4) = 0` free GPUs in the `general` pool; (b) `24` of `32` cluster GPUs (`75.0%`) are idle (`25.0%` used); (c) Job C stays `Pending` because the `24` idle GPUs are locked behind `training` (`16`) and `distributed-inference` (`8`) taints that an unconstrained request does not tolerate.
3. **Exercise 3 (Whole-GPU waste, `70B` quantization & `GB` vs `GiB`):** (a) `24.0 - 1.8 = 22.2 GB` (`92.5%` / `~92%`) unused on one `L4` GPU, while `5` pods across `10` time-sliced replicas use `5 * 1.8 = 9.0 GB <= 24.0 GB`; (b) `70B` requires `280.0 GB` in `FP32` (`4 B`/param) vs `35.0 GB` in `4-bit` (`0.5 B`/param); (c) `40 GB ≈ 37.25 GiB` and `80 GB ≈ 74.5 GiB`.
4. **Exercise 4 (Combinatorial Qualification Matrix):** `3 * 2 * 4 = 24` combinations across 3 layers and `24 * 2 = 48` combinations across 4 layers; collapsing to `2` approved Node Profiles × `4` image families requires only `2 * 4 = 8` combinations (`6.0x` reduction).
5. **Exercise 5 (GPU Operator Operands & Ownership Models):** The 6 operand families are Driver, Container Toolkit, Device Plugin, NFD/GFD, MIG Manager, and Validators/DCGM; ownership is Host-Managed (`driver.enabled=false, toolkit.enabled=false`), Operator-Managed (`driver.enabled=true, toolkit.enabled=true`), or Hybrid (`driver.enabled=false, toolkit.enabled=true`), governed by the single-reconciler rule (one team/controller per layer).
6. **Exercise 6 (5-Gate Node Acceptance Suite):** Gate 1 (Hardware/Driver: `lspci`, `lsmod`, `nvidia-smi`), Gate 2 (Runtime Injection: minimal `nvidia/cuda` container prints `CUDA_CHECK_OK`), Gate 3 (K8s Resource: `Allocatable > 0` + GFD labels), Gate 4 (Workload Image: PyTorch/vLLM smoke test), and Gate 5 (Operations: `dcgm-exporter` `up == 1`); running both Gate 2 and Gate 4 separates a platform runtime bug (Gate 2 fails) from an application image `sm_` build bug (Gate 2 passes, Gate 4 fails with `no kernel image is available`).
