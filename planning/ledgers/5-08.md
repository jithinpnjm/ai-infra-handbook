## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (The End-to-End 20-Step Pod Lifecycle):** Traces `POST Pod` -> `kube-scheduler` `Binding` (`PodScheduled`) -> `kubelet` `syncPod` -> `DevicePlugin.Allocate` -> `CSI NodeStageVolume/NodePublishVolume` -> `CRI RunPodSandbox` -> `CNI ADD` -> `CRI PullImage` -> `CreateContainer`/`StartContainer` (`Initialized`, `ContainersReady`, `Ready`) -> `DELETE` (`preStop`, `SIGTERM`, `SIGKILL`, `CNI DEL`, `NodeUnpublishVolume`).
2. **Exercise 2 (Inside `kubelet`'s `syncPod` Loop, `TopologyAffinityError`, and CSI Staging/Publishing):** PLEG relists container states every 1s to feed `syncPod`; `kubelet` local admission can reject a scheduled Pod with `TopologyAffinityError` if GPUs, NICs, and CPUs cannot align on one NUMA node; and CSI stages volumes globally via `NodeStageVolume` before bind-mounting per Pod via `NodePublishVolume`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Sandbox Creation `RunPodSandbox`, CNI `ADD` / IPAM JSON, and `/proc/<pid>/ns` Inodes, `NK` Ch. 3–4 & `PK` Ch. 5):** `verify_pod_namespace_inodes()` confirms `net`, `ipc`, `uts`, and `cgroup` match the `pause` container while `mnt` and `pid` are isolated; `CNI_COMMAND=ADD` wires the `veth` pair and returns the IPAM JSON result, and `spec.readinessGates` gates `Ready=True` on external load-balancer target health.
4. **Exercise 4 (GPU and RDMA Attachment: `Allocate` RPC, CDI, and Cgroup v2 eBPF Device Filters):** `verify_gpu_cgroup_device_isolation()` verifies that a container receives only its allocated `/dev/nvidia*` character devices in its mount namespace plus a matching cgroup v2 eBPF device allow-list filter, while `/var/lib/kubelet/pod-resources/kubelet.sock` maps GPUs to Pods for `dcgm-exporter`.
5. **Exercise 5 (Measuring and Accelerating Pod Startup Latency on 64-Node GPU Jobs):** `compute_pod_startup_intervals()` separates scheduling, initialization, container start, readiness, and `kubelet_pod_start_sli_duration_seconds` (excluding image pull); startup is accelerated via P2P/lazy image pulling, `serializeImagePulls: false`, pre-mounted shared filesystems, and `fsGroupChangePolicy: OnRootMismatch`.
6. **Exercise 6 (Pod Termination, `D`-State Uninterruptible Sleep on Hung Mounts, and Debugging by Stage):** A process stuck in kernel `D` (`TASK_UNINTERRUPTIBLE`) state on a hung NFS/Lustre mount cannot handle `SIGKILL`, keeping the Pod in `Terminating` until the storage mount recovers or the node reboots.
