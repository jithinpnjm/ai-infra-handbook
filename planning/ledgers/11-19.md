## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** MIG, Time-Slicing, MPS, and vGPU are the underlying hardware/driver sharing primitives exposed by the NVIDIA GPU Operator and Device Plugin; the open-source KAI Scheduler is the cluster-local `Queue`/`PodGroup`/`BindRequest` scheduling engine; and the commercial Run:ai Platform sits above Kubernetes to provide multi-cluster `Department -> Project` governance, SSO, self-service `Workspace`/`Training`/`Inference` portals, and chargeback analytics.
2. **Exercise 2:** The 7 layers are `Identity`, `Admission`, `Kubernetes`, `Data`, `GPU`, `Host`, and `Observability` across the `Workload`, `Node`, `Management`, and `Observability` planes; verify isolation via `nvidia-smi --query-processes=pid,process_name,gpu_memory_usage --format=csv` inside Team A's MIG pod and `curl http://prometheus:9090/api/v1/query?query=nvidia_smi_memory_used_mib | grep -i "team_a"` using Team B's credentials.
3. **Exercise 3:** Professor A has a `-100` GPU-hour deficit (`400` Month 2 effective quota), Professor B has a `+150` GPU-hour surplus (`400` Month 2 effective quota), combined Month 1 utilization is `700 / 750 = 93%`, the `400/500` `2`-GPU job scores `-(400-500)/500 + 0.1 = +0.3`, and the `300/250` `8`-GPU job scores `-(300-250)/250 + 0.0 = -0.2`.
4. **Exercise 4:** `0 s` `SIGTERM` -> `5–10 s` checkpoint save (`30 s` `SIGKILL` deadline) -> `45 s` new job starts -> `90 s` old job resumes (`~2 min` wall-clock loss); Local NVMe recovers in `< 5 min` (`0` CapEx), Shared NFS (`1 TB`/team, every `8h`) adds `1–2%` async overhead and survives node failure, and Nightly Tape survives catastrophic NFS failure.
5. **Exercise 5:** Hardware CapEx = `$2.4M` (`200 × $12K`), Total CapEx = `$2.7M` (`+$100K` net `+$200K` NFS), Power OpEx = `$105,120/yr` (`≈ $105K/yr` for `80 kW` at `$0.15/kWh`), Total Annual OpEx = `≈ $0.9M/yr`, Delivered GPU-hours at `60%` = `1,051,200`, Cost = `≈ $0.86/GPU-hr`, and `1,000`-GPU `5x` scale = `$13.5M` CapEx and `≈ $4.5M/yr` OpEx.
6. **Exercise 6:** `L1` Redfish APIs -> BMCs (`128` nodes), `L2` BCM PXE-boots unified OS/driver/`MLNX_OFED` image (`1,024` GPUs), `L3` InfiniBand NDR fat-tree (`768` GPUs) + RoCEv2 Spectrum-X with SR-IOV (`256` GPUs), `L4` Lustre + GPUDirect Storage (`GDS`), `L5` Slurm (`75%` = `768` GPUs for Foundation Model) + Kubernetes with Run:ai and MIG (`25%` = `256` GPUs for `10` research teams), and `L6` `DCGM-Exporter` -> Prometheus/Grafana auto-cordoning on double-bit ECC.
