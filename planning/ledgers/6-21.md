## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Verifying Crash-Safe Checkpoint Commits and Bit-Rot Detection):** `ckpt_atomic.py` ignores uncommitted `.tmp` directories (`step-200.tmp`), discovers `step-300` even when `latest` is stale (`step-100`), and falls back from `step-400` to `step-300` when `MANIFEST.json` SHA-256 verification catches a 4-byte corruption in `shard-2.bin`.
2. **Exercise 2 (Resharding Distributed Checkpoints Across World Sizes):** `dcp_reshard.py` writes `__0_0.distcp..__3_0.distcp` plus `.metadata` for an $8 \times 3$ `DTensor` across 4 ranks (`(2, 3)` each), allowing `dcp.load()` on 2 ranks (`(4, 3)` each) or 1 rank (`(8, 3)`) to read only the required tensor slices without rewriting the checkpoint files.
3. **Exercise 3 (Calculating Optimal Checkpoint Intervals with the Young/Daly Approximation):** $T_{\text{opt}} = \sqrt{2 \cdot C \cdot \text{MTBF}}$ yields $\sqrt{2 \times 1 \times 360} \approx 27\text{ minutes}$ for a 1-minute synchronous checkpoint and $\sqrt{2 \times (5/60) \times 360} \approx 7.7\text{ minutes}$ when async NVMe staging lowers GPU stall $C$ to 5 seconds.
4. **Exercise 4 (Designing 2D Block-Decomposed MPI-IO and Parallel HDF5 Collective Writes):** `MPI_Type_create_subarray` builds `filetype` (global tile on disk) and `memtype` (interior `ny` $\times$ `nx` subarray excluding `ng` ghost cells) for `MPI_File_write_all`, matching HDF5's `H5Pset_fapl_mpio`, `H5Sselect_hyperslab`, and `H5Pset_dxpl_mpio(xfer_plist, H5FD_MPIO_COLLECTIVE)`.
5. **Exercise 5 (Isolating PyTorch `DataLoader` vs H2D DMA Copy Bottlenecks and GPU Decoding):** Benchmark `num_workers=0` to measure pure Python dataset latency, overlap H2D DMA on a dedicated `torch.cuda.Stream()` with `pin_memory=True` and `non_blocking=True`, and use NVIDIA `DALI` / Blackwell Decompression Engine (`800 GB/s`) to drop CPU usage from `800%` to `200%`.
6. **Exercise 6 (Comparing DeepSeek `3FS` and GPUDirect Storage `GDS` for Random-Read AI Workloads):** `GDS` (`cuFileRead` + `nvidia-fs.ko`) bypasses the host page cache (`8.0 -> 9.6 GB/s`, `85% -> 25%` CPU), while DeepSeek's `3FS` pairs `FoundationDB` metadata and `CRAQ` NVMe storage chains with an RDMA/`io_uring` `USRBIO` client tailored for massive random reads.
