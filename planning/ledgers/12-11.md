## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) `51.2 Tbps` (`512 x 100G PAM4`): `64x800G` (`8` lanes), `128x400G` (`4` lanes), `256x200G` (`2` lanes), `512x100G` (`1` lane); (b) `64.0 MB / 64` = `1.0 MB` sliced cap (`7.0 MB` dropped of `8.0 MB` burst) vs `16.0 MB` dynamic shared cap ($\alpha = 25\%$, `0.0 MB` dropped); (c) `2, 4, 8, 16` SUs = `1,152, 2,304, 4,608, 9,216` GPUs, `4, 8, 12, 24` spines, `8, 16, 32, 64` compute leafs (`24x400G`, `1:3`), `2, 2, 4, 8` storage leafs (`72x400G`, `1:1`), `64, 128, 256, 1024 x 400GbE` (`25.6..409.6 Tbps`), Standard `90/45` (`1` SU) & `360/180 GB/s` (`4` SUs) vs Enhanced `280/140` (`1` SU) & `1,120/560 GB/s` (`4` SUs).
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) Downlink = `12,800 Gbps`, normal uplink = `3,200 Gbps` (`4.0:1` oversubscription), `N-1` uplink = `2,800 Gbps` (`4.57:1` oversubscription); (b) Draining `1` of `2` spines halves `nexthop-group 40` (`2 -> 1`), concentrating cross-rack traffic onto half the uplinks and raising `tx_ecn_marked_prio3` `5.87x` (`15,000 -> 88,120`).
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) DPU SKU defaults to `DPU_MODE_ECPF` (Arm owns `eSwitch`, host isolated) vs SuperNIC SKU `NIC_MODE_SUPERNIC`; `swp14 up` + failed `openvswitch-policy-load.service` + `enp1s0f0np0 DOWN` is `LAYER2_DPU_ARM_POLICY_BOOT_FAILURE` (and `node09` drifted on `24.07-prod-1` after `5` abandoned retries); (b) `LnkSta 16GT/s x8` (`16.0 GB/s`) is `1/4` of `LnkCap 32GT/s x16` (`64.0 GB/s`) and `< 25.0 GB/s` needed for `200GbE`; `+88,723,681,792` B (`+88.7 GB`) on `ens1f0` vs `412,992` B on `ens1f1` occurred because both local GPU ranks bound to `mlx5_0`.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) `0x8BDA7C2D` (`2,346,351,661`) gives `mod 5 = 1` and `mod 8 = 5`, while `512`/`1,024`-bucket resilient hashing reassigns only buckets on a failed link; (b) `SLB` loads `(100G, 200G)` (`100%` peak, `~60%` cluster effective BW) whereas `DLB`/Spectrum-X balances `(150G, 150G)` (`75%` util, `~95%` effective BW) by matching `UDP 4791` `BTH` `RDMA Write` opcodes via TCAM ACL (`PER_PACKET_SPRAY`).
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) Arrival `PSNs (200, 202, 203, 201, 205, 204)` DMA directly to `0x7F0000000000 + {0, 8192, 12288, 4096, 20480, 16384}` with `0` `Go-Back-N` retransmits on BlueField-3 (`DDP`) vs `3` `PSN`-gap `NAK` events on a legacy NIC, emitting the `CQE` after `200..205` complete; (b) At `160 KB` queue (`ratio = 0.5`), Spectrum-X telemetry paces `2` flows to `185.0 Gbps` each vs a blind `200.0 Gbps` binary cut.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) `fec_uncorrected=3` is `PHYSICAL_FAULT`, `ECN=47,829 & PFC=412` is `HEALTHY_CONGESTION_CONTROL`, `WJH` `18,402`/`52,100` is `OVERSUBSCRIBED_CUT_OR_HOT_LEAF`, `22%` util is `ECMP_OR_RAIL_IMBALANCE`, and `rx_pfc_prio3` `{leaf03: 210,500, leaf07: 198,200, leaf12: 41,100}` isolates `leaf03` as the origin; (b) `Lab 04` diverged at Layer 3 (wrong `mlx5_1` GID `fe80::...d79d`), and a valid Spectrum-X `NVUE` config requires jumbo `MTU 9000/9216`, `qos roce enable on`, `pfc prio 3`, `ecn`, `adaptive-routing enable on`, and `router bgp` + `l2vpn-evpn`.
