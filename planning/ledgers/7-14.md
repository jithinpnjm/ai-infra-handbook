## DMTF Redfish Specification (DSP0266 v1.21.0) & Resource Guide (DSP2046) Claims
- **`Bios` (`v1.2`), `@Redfish.Settings` (§9.10), and `AttributeRegistry` (`v1.5`):** `/Bios` exposes live attributes and `@Redfish.Settings.SettingsObject` pointing to `/Bios/Settings` (pending). `PATCH` to `/Bios/Settings` stages values with `Base.x.y.ResetRequired` (`UpdatePending` is in the `Update` registry, not `Base`). After `ComputerSystem.Reset`, pending values move to `/Bios` and `/Bios/Settings` clears to `{}`.
- **Boot Override (`ComputerSystem.Boot`):** `BootSourceOverrideEnabled` (`Once`, `Continuous`, `Disabled`), `BootSourceOverrideTarget` (`Pxe`, `UefiHttp`, `Hdd`, `BiosSetup`, `Diags`), and `BootSourceOverrideMode` (`UEFI`, `Legacy`).

## BCM 11 (§14.5), `volume-10/01` & Lab Verification (`code/part07/lifecycle_lab.py`)
- **BCM `biossettings` (§14.5):** Tracks `Configured`, `Pending`, and `Live` states via `bios check` and `bios apply`.
