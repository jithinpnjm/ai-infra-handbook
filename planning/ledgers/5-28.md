## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Service Mesh Architecture, `xDS` APIs, and `SPIFFE` mTLS Identity, `KUR` Ch. 15):** Built-in `Service`s operate at L4 and `Ingress` only at the perimeter; a service mesh intercepts Pod traffic via `iptables`/eBPF into a proxy configured over Envoy `xDS` (`LDS`, `RDS`, `CDS`, `EDS`, `SDS`) with short-lived `spiffe://cluster.local/ns/<ns>/sa/<sa>` X.509 certificates.
2. **Exercise 2 (Comparing Service Mesh Data-Plane Models: `Sidecar` vs. `Ambient` vs. `Cilium eBPF`):** `compute_mesh_fleet_memory_overhead()` shows 5,000 Pods consume ~390 GiB with Envoy sidecars (~88 GiB with Linkerd sidecars) versus ~8.8 GiB in Istio Ambient mode (`ztunnel` per node + shared `Waypoint` proxies only where L7 features are needed).
3. **Exercise 3 (Zero-Trust East-West Security: `PeerAuthentication` and `AuthorizationPolicy`):** `evaluate_mesh_mtls_and_authz()` migrates from `PERMISSIVE` to `STRICT` mTLS and enforces `CUSTOM -> DENY -> ALLOW` order on verified SPIFFE principals (`cluster.local/ns/edge/sa/router`), methods (`POST`), and paths (`/v1/*`).
4. **Exercise 4 (L7 Traffic Management for LLM Serving):** `evaluate_outlier_detection_and_routing()` routes `x-canary: true` to `v2`, balances HTTP/2 & gRPC per request (`LEAST_REQUEST`), ejects pods exceeding `consecutive5xxErrors: 5` (`outlierDetection`), sets `timeout: 600s` for SSE streaming, and sets `retries: { attempts: 0 }` on non-idempotent generation calls.
5. **Exercise 5 (Sidecar Lifecycle Traps, `Sidecar` Config Scoping, and Envoy Response Flags):** Native sidecars (`initContainers` with `restartPolicy: Always`) fix startup races and batch `Job` completion hangs, `Sidecar` CRDs scope `xDS` egress to `./*` and `istio-system/*`, and Envoy response flags (`UF`, `UH`, `UO`, `NR`, `UT`) pinpoint 503/504 root causes.
6. **Exercise 6 (When NOT to Use a Service Mesh on GPU Clusters, `KUR` Ch. 15):** Never route distributed training collectives (`NCCL` over RDMA or TCP) or parallel-filesystem storage traffic through user-space proxies (`sidecar.istio.io/inject: "false"` or `traffic.sidecar.istio.io/excludeOutboundPorts`).
