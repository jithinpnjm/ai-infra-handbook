## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Fair Tree Calculation and Subtree Isolation):** `fairtree_sim.py` computes `LevelFS = S_norm / U_norm` at each sibling level and ranks leaves bottom-up (`alice=1.0`, `bob=0.75`, `carol=0.5`, `dave=0.25`); when `dave` (`Org B`) surges to `5000` GPU-hours, `alice` and `bob` in `Org A` remain completely unaffected above `carol` and `dave`.
2. **Exercise 2 (Multifactor Priority Integer Breakdown (`sprio` Simulator)):** `compute_priority()` sums `int(Weight * Factor)` across Age (`min(age_sec / max_age_sec, 1.0)`), FairShare, JobSize, Partition, QoS, and TRES so operators can verify when QoS priority overrides FairShare.
3. **Exercise 3 (Designing a 3-Tier QoS Hierarchy with Preemption):** `high` (`Priority=1000`, `Preempt=normal,scavenger`, `GrpTRES=gres/gpu=256`), `normal` (`Priority=500`, `Preempt=scavenger`, `MaxTRESPerUser=gres/gpu=64`, `Flags=DenyOnLimit`), and `scavenger` (`Priority=0`, `GraceTime=120`, `UsageFactor=0.0`, `Flags=NoReserve`) prevent scavenger jobs from delaying high-priority backfill reservations or billing user FairShare.
4. **Exercise 4 (Backfill Shadow Timeline (`EASY` Backfill Simulator)):** On an `8`-GPU cluster with `J1` (`4` GPUs until `t=4h`) running and `J2` (`8` GPUs for `6h`) at the head of the queue (`shadow_start=4h`), `J3` (`4` GPUs for `3h`) can backfill at `t=0` without delaying `J2`, whereas `J4` (`4` GPUs for `5h`) cannot start at `t=0` because it would push `J2` to `t=5h`.
5. **Exercise 5 (`AccountingStorageEnforce` and Clearing Inherited Limits (`-1` vs `NONE` vs `0`)):** Setting `AccountingStorageEnforce=associations,limits,qos,safe` enforces association registration, TRES limits, QoS authorization, and prevents jobs from starting when `slurmdbd` is unreachable; clearing a limit requires `-1` (`MaxTRES=gres/gpu=-1`), whereas `0` sets a zero-GPU cap that blocks all GPU jobs (`AssocGrpGRES`).
6. **Exercise 6 (Configuring `TRESBillingWeights`, Decay Half-Life, and `sreport` Auditing):** Setting `TRESBillingWeights="CPU=0.0625,Mem=0.0078125G,GRES/gpu=1.0"` bills a job consuming `100%` of node CPUs (`128`) or RAM (`1024G`) with `0` GPUs at `8.0` billing units (matching an `8`-GPU allocation), `PriorityDecayHalfLife=3-6:30` decays historical usage every `78.5` hours, and `sreport cluster AccountUtilizationByUser -T gres/gpu,billing` audits GPU vs billing utilization.
