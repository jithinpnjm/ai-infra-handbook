## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (The 7-Step Method for Reading Any Kubernetes Operator Source Repository):** Read in order: (1) `api/...` CRD types, (2) `SetupWithManager` watches & predicates, (3) `Reconcile` flow & early returns, (4) state/operand manifest rendering (`assets/` vs `manifests/`), (5) readiness checks (`isDaemonSetReady`), (6) node label gating, and (7) `*_test.go` unit/envtest suites.
2. **Exercise 2 (Tracing `ClusterPolicyReconciler.Reconcile`, `init`, and `step`):** The oldest `ClusterPolicy` by `creationTimestamp` wins the singleton check; `step()` deploys all enabled states without blocking on `NotReady` states (requeuing in `5s`) because per-node init containers enforce ordering on each host.
3. **Exercise 3 (Inside `DaemonSet(n ClusterPolicyController)`, `Transform*` Functions, Hash Annotations, and `isDaemonSetReady`):** `Transform*` mutates static `assets/` YAML in memory, hash annotations (`isDaemonsetSpecChanged`) prevent spurious API updates caused by server-side defaulting, and `is_daemonset_ready()` checks every Pod's `controller-revision-hash` on `OnDelete` driver DaemonSets.
4. **Exercise 4 (Node Gating `gpuStateLabels` and the `/run/nvidia/validations` Init-Container Chain):** `compute_gpu_node_deploy_labels()` maps `nvidia.com/gpu.workload.config` (`container`, `vm-passthrough`, `vm-vgpu`) to `nvidia.com/gpu.deploy.*` labels, while `cmd/nvidia-validator` writes `driver-ready` $\rightarrow$ `toolkit-ready` $\rightarrow$ `plugin-ready` / `cuda-ready` under `/run/nvidia/validations`.
5. **Exercise 5 (Driver Lifecycle: `ClusterPolicy.spec.driver` vs. `NVIDIADriver` CRD, `OnDelete`, and `UpgradeReconciler`):** `NVIDIADriver` enables per-node-pool driver configurations (causing `ClusterPolicy` to orphan-delete its legacy driver DaemonSets), and `UpgradeReconciler` orchestrates rolling `OnDelete` driver restarts via `nvidia.com/gpu-driver-upgrade-state` node labels.
6. **Exercise 6 (Comparing `Mellanox/network-operator` with `NVIDIA/gpu-operator`):** `evaluate_node_operand_gating_and_stale_objects()` contrasts the GPU Operator's `/run/nvidia/validations` init-container gating against the Network Operator's `network.nvidia.com/operator.mofed.wait: "false"` node-selector label and `state_skel.go` stale-object deletion.
