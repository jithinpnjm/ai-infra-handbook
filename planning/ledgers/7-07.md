# Claim Ledger: 7.7 Systems, Chassis and Managers: the core resource model

## DMTF Redfish Specification (DSP0266 v1.21.0) & Resource Guide (DSP2046) Claims
- **`ComputerSystem` (`v1.23.1`), `Chassis` (`v1.26.0`), and `Manager` (`v1.20.0`):** `ComputerSystem` models the logical software-visible computer (`SystemType`: `Physical`, `Virtual`, `PhysicallyPartitioned`, `VirtuallyPartitioned`, `Composed`, `DPU`, and deprecated `OS`; `BootSourceOverrideEnabled`: `Disabled`, `Once`, `Continuous`; `BootSourceOverrideTarget`: `None`, `Pxe`, `Hdd`, `Cd`, `Usb`, `BiosSetup`, `UefiHttp`, etc.; `Processors` with `ProcessorType` `CPU`, `GPU`, `FPGA`, `DSP`, `Accelerator`). `Chassis` models physical enclosures (`ChassisType`: `Rack`, `Blade`, `Enclosure`, `StandAlone`, `RackMount`, `Card`, `Sled`, `Shelf`, `ImmersionTank`, `HeatExchanger`, `PowerStrip`, etc.) and links to `Sensors`, `ThermalSubsystem`, `PowerSubsystem`, `EnvironmentMetrics` (replacing deprecated `Power` and `Thermal`). `Manager` models the controller (`ManagerType`: `BMC`, `EnclosureManager`, `RackManager`, `ManagementController`, `AuxiliaryController`, `Service`) and provides `Manager.Reset` and `Manager.ResetToDefaults` (`ResetAll`, `PreserveNetworkAndUsers`, `PreserveNetwork`).
- **Absent & StandbyOffline Resources (DSP0266 §9.11):** Empty hardware slots remain in the resource tree with `Status.State: "Absent"` and `null` values; sleeping/unreachable devices use `Status.State: "StandbyOffline"`.
