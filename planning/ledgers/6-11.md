## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Linting `#SBATCH` Scripts for Resource Conflicts and Anti-Patterns):** `sbatch_lint.py` catches missing `#!` shebang, mutually exclusive `--mem` + `--mem-per-gpu`, incompatible `--cpus-per-gpu` + `--cpus-per-task`, and `--gpus-per-task` without task count, and warns when `--requeue` is used without `--open-mode=append` or `%j` in `--output`.
2. **Exercise 2 (Simulating a 2-Node GPU Cluster on One Workstation):** Define `NodeName=node6 Port=17016 Gres=gpu:1` and `NodeName=node7 Port=17017 Gres=gpu:1` in `slurm.conf` with `/dev/nvidia6` and `/dev/nvidia7` in `gres.conf`, and verify with `sinfo`, `srun -N 1 hostname`, and `srun -N 2 hostname`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Deriving PyTorch Rendezvous Variables from Slurm):** Export `MASTER_ADDR=$(scontrol show hostnames "$SLURM_JOB_NODELIST" | head -n 1)` and `MASTER_PORT=$((29500 + RANDOM % 1000))` in the batch script, and map `RANK=$SLURM_PROCID` and `LOCAL_RANK=$SLURM_LOCALID` inside the `srun` task subshell (since `SLURM_PROCID`/`SLURM_LOCALID` are only set by `srun`).
4. **Exercise 4 (2D Hyperparameter Grid Search with Job Arrays):** `map_array_task_to_hp(5, [1e-4, 3e-4, 1e-3, 3e-3], [16, 32, 64])` computes `lr_idx = 5 % 4 = 1` (`3e-4`) and `bs_idx = 5 // 4 = 1` (`32`), while `%4` caps concurrent tasks at 4.
5. **Exercise 5 (Preemption-Safe Checkpointing with `SLURMCheckpointer`):** Register `signal.signal(signal.SIGUSR1, ...)` and `signal.signal(signal.SIGTERM, ...)` so both wall-clock warnings (`--signal=B:USR1@300` for the batch shell or `--signal=SIGUSR1@90` for steps) and preemption/cancellation (`SIGTERM`) save a checkpoint on rank 0 and requeue cleanly.
6. **Exercise 6 (Chaining Multi-Segment Training and Evaluation Pipelines):** Chain training segments with `--dependency=afterany:$J1` (since a wall-clock timeout exits non-zero and would block `afterok:$J1`), use `afterok:$J2` for final evaluation, and add `--kill-on-invalid-dep=yes` to auto-cancel orphaned `DependencyNeverSatisfied` jobs.
