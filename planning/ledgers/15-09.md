## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) An 80-layer model executes **`160` AllReduces per token** (`2` per layer), requiring **`2,240` ring steps/token at `TP = 8`** (`14` steps/AllReduce) versus **`4,800` ring steps/token at `TP = 16`** (`30` steps/AllReduce), and moves **`28,672` bytes (`28 KiB`) per AllReduce** (`4.59 MB/token`) per rank at `TP = 8`; (b) `TP = 8` over NVLink 4 (`900 GB/s`, `0.8 us`) adds only **`0.13 ms` per token** (`14.13 ms` total ITL, `< 1%` comm), whereas `TP = 16` over `100GbE` (`12.5 GB/s`, `500 us`) adds **`80+ ms` per token** (`> 85%` comm stall), and switching to `TP = 4, PP = 8` shards inter-stage activation transfers by `1 / TP` to **`2,048` elements (`4,096` bytes) per hop** across `7` stage boundaries.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) DeepSeek-R1 activates **`5.51%` of parameters per token** (`37B / 671B`) with **`3.125%` routed expert density** (`8 / 256 > 3%`), placing **`32` complete routed experts/GPU at `EP = 8`** and **`4` routed experts/GPU at `EP = 64`** on a GB200 NVL72 rack; (b) 4-wave scheduling (`18` GPUs/wave) reduces simultaneous `AllToAll` messages on 72 GPUs from **`5,112` to `306` (`16.71x` reduction)**, and with base capacity `32` at `CF = 1.2` (`38`-token cap), loads `[50, 28, 30, 20]` spill **`12` overflow tokens** while EPLB cloning of the 50-token hot expert cuts peak per-replica load from **`50` to `30` tokens**.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) With `q = 0.6`, rejection sampling accepts with probability **`1.0` (`100%`) when `p = 0.8`** and **`0.6667` (`66.67%`) when `p = 0.4`**; for `K = 4, alpha = 0.8, c_draft = 0.10`, `E[tokens] = 3.3616` and net speedup is **`2.40x`** (`3.3616 / 1.40`), whereas a slow draft model (`K = 4, alpha = 0.6, c_draft = 0.35`) yields `E[tokens] = 2.3056` at cost `2.40` for a **`0.96x` slowdown**; (b) positional acceptance `[0.8, 0.7, 0.6, 0.5, 0.10, 0.02]` yields **`2.8640` tokens at `K = 4`** versus **`2.8811` tokens at `K = 6`**, so steps 5 and 6 add only **`0.0171` expected tokens** while paying two full draft forward passes.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) The `LeaderWorkerSet` (`replicas: 2, size: 2`, `8` GPUs/pod) schedules **`2` leader pods and `2` worker pods (`4` pods, `32` GPUs total)**; naive round-robin at `110 tok/s` vs `240 tok/s` ideal achieves **`45.83%` scaling efficiency** (`73%` GPU/KV spread), while unbounded prefix routing (`3,600` vs `1,100` requests) creates a **`3.27x` load imbalance**; (b) Stage 0 (`0.992`) minus Stage 1 (`0.385`) equals **`0.607` (`60.7%` > `0.40` alert threshold)**, resolved by enabling **`--enable-chunked-prefill`** or switching to **`DP = 2, TP = 8` replicas** (`72%` and `74%` KV usage).
