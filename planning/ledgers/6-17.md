## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Automated PBS-to-Slurm Script Translation):** `pbs2slurm.py` divides `ncpus` (`64`) by `mpiprocs` (`8`) to emit `#SBATCH --cpus-per-task=8`, maps `place=scatter:excl` to `--exclusive`, `-J 0-9%3` to `--array=0-9%3`, and `-m abe` to `--mail-type=FAIL,BEGIN,END`, and flags custom resources (`software=matlab`) and heterogeneous `+` chunks as `# TODO` comments.
2. **Exercise 2 (Configuring Torque Queues, Server Parameters, and `pbs_mom` Epilogue):** Open TCP ports `15001`–`15004`, configure queue `MYCLUSTER` and server parameters with `qmgr` (backed up via `qmgr -c "print server" > /tmp/server.conf`), and use `/var/spool/PBS/mom_priv/epilogue` reading `/var/spool/PBS/aux/$1` to kill stray user processes and clean `/tmp` across nodes.
3. **Exercise 3 (Customizing Torque's Scheduler `sched_config` vs `maui.cfg`):** Configure `round_robin`, `by_queue`, `strict_fifo`, `fair_share`, `help_starving_jobs` (`max_starve`), and `sort_by: multi_sort` (`Table 14-2`) in `/var/spool/PBS/sched_priv/sched_config`, or pair `pbs_server` with Maui/Moab (`maui.cfg`) for enterprise fair-share, backfill, and reservations.
4. **Exercise 4 (Cross-Scheduler Translation Matrix):** Express 2 nodes $\times$ 8 GPUs $\times$ 64 CPUs $\times$ 500 GiB RAM exclusive for 12h as `sbatch -N 2 --gpus-per-node=8 --cpus-per-task=8 --ntasks-per-node=8 --mem=500G --exclusive -t 12:00:00` (Slurm), `qsub -l select=2:ncpus=64:mpiprocs=8:ngpus=8:mem=500gb,place=scatter:excl,walltime=12:00:00` (OpenPBS), `bsub -n 16 -R "span[ptile=8] rusage[mem=512000]" -gpu "num=8" -x -W 12:00` (LSF), and `flux batch -N 2 -n 16 -c 8 --gpus-per-node=8 -x -t 12h` (Flux).
5. **Exercise 5 (Running Nested Hierarchical Scheduling with LLNL Flux Inside Slurm):** `flux batch` launches a child `flux-broker` subinstance inside a Slurm allocation so tens of thousands of ensemble/RL sub-jobs schedule locally inside the subinstance without touching `slurmctld`.
6. **Exercise 6 (Planning a Production Scheduler Migration):** Inventory queues/hooks/scripts, map policies to Slurm partitions/QoS/Fair Tree, convert scripts with `pbs2slurm.py` + `sbatch_lint.py`, seed historical fair-share in `slurmdbd`, run a dual-partition pilot, cut over in node waves, and switch workflow executors to Slurm.
