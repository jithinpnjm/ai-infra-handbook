## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) InfiniBand (`<100K` GPUs, `300 ns`, `LRH/BTH`, `SHARP`), Standard `RoCEv2` (`>100K`, `800 ns`, `UDP 4791`, host `xCCL`), `Spectrum-X` (`>100K`, `800 ns`, `UDP 4791` + hardware `DDP`/AR), and `UET v1.0` (`~1M` GPUs, `800 ns`, `UDP 49150` or `IP 253` with `128 B` parse depth, `UEC INC` over `gRPC`); (b) IB uses `16` VLs (`8..12` active queues) with `64 B` `CBFC` credits (`16`-bit `LID` up to `48,000`/`49,151` nodes), `RoCEv2` uses `8` `PFC` priorities + `DCQCN` (`CE=11` $\rightarrow$ `CNP 01`), and `UEC` uses `32` `CBFC` VCs (`64B/66B` `CtlOS` `CP_Update`) + `RCCC` (`256 B` credits in `24`-bit `ACK_CC`) + `NSCC` + packet trimming + `LLR`.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) `LINK_TYPE_P1=1` selects InfiniBand (`IB`) and `LINK_TYPE_P1=2` selects Ethernet (`ETH`); BCM `cmsh` precedence `category.extra_values < node.extra_values < interface.extra_values` resolves to `interface.extra_values = IB`; (b) `0000:41:00.0` fails because `LnkSta 16GT/s x8` caps PCIe at `16.0 GB/s` (`< 25.0 GB/s` required for `200GbE`), `SYS` crosses the CPU/NUMA host bridge, and `ens1f0` (`+88.7 GB`) vs `ens1f1` (`412,992 B`) proves both local ranks collided on `mlx5_0`.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) `16` `DGX H100 SXM5` nodes (`$285K`/node = `$35,625`/GPU, `$5.26M` CapEx + `3 × $644K` OpEx = `$7.19M` 3-yr TCO) cost `$7.19M / (128 × 8,760 × 3) = $2.14`/GPU-hour (`$22`/TFLOP-year at `107,603 TFLOPS`), vs `$215K`/node (`$26,875`/GPU) for `H100 PCIe`; (b) on `64` GPUs, `400GbE` saves `$7K × 64 = $448,000` upfront; `8%` `AllReduce` loss (`10 ms` vs `2 ms`) adds `13.4 h` (`181.4 h` / `7.56 d`) costing `$1,835`/run, `$44,040`/yr (`24` runs), and `$132,120` over `3` yrs (`+$12,425`/yr for `2` runs at `27%` overhead), requiring `244.14` runs (`10.17` yrs) to break even on compute hours alone.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Downlink demand is `16 × 800 = 12,800 Gb/s`; normal `8×400G = 3,200 Gb/s` gives `4.00:1` oversubscription, and `N-1` `7×400G = 2,800 Gb/s` gives `4.57:1`; (b) on `65,536 B`, `Xoff = 49,152 B` is `75.0%` (`16,384 B` = `25.0%` in-flight headroom) and `Xon = 32,768 B` is `50.0%` (`16,384 B` = `25.0%` hysteresis gap to prevent rapid pause/unpause oscillation).
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) `PCIe Gen5` SSD (`~100 ns`), `IB` (`~300 ns`), and `RoCEv2 NVMe-o-RDMA` (`~800 ns`, `66%` lower write latency than TCP) support `GPUDirect Storage` (`cudaMalloc` + `cuFileBufRegister`) on lossless fabrics (`1:3` oversubscription), whereas `NVMe-o-TCP` runs on lossy IP cores for `>= 40 km` WAN replication without `GDS`; (b) `NVMe-o-RDMA` discovers over `TCP 8009` (`RDMA_PRTYPE = 04` RoCEv2 vs `02` IB / `05` iWARP), establishes QPs via `UDP 4420` `RDMA_CM`, and transfers `1:1` `SQ`/`CQ` capsules over `UDP 4791` `BTH` (controller-initiated `RDMA_READ`/`WRITE`), whereas `NVMe-o-TCP` uses `TCP 8009` `ICReq PDU` and `CapsuleCmd 0x01` / `H2CData 0x03` / `C2HData 0x04` (`PRP1`/`PRP2` or `SGL`).
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) `GATE1_PHYSICAL_LINK_OR_CABLE_FAULT` (`fec_uncorrected=3 == rx_crc_errors=3` on `swp17` `DAC-2m` `ABC123`; replace cable first); (b) `GATE1_DPU_ARM_POLICY_LOAD_FAILURE` (`BlueField` `ECPF` `openvswitch-policy-load.service` failed while `swp14` stayed `up`); (c) `GATE4_QOS_TRUST_MODE_DRIFT_PRIO0_DROPS` (`swp19` set to `pcp` instead of `dscp`, dropping `18,420` `DSCP 46` packets in `prio 0`); (d) `GATE6_OVERSUBSCRIBED_LEAF_UPLINK_TAIL_DROP` (`leaf03` is the downstream pause-tree origin at `210,500` with `52,100` `WJH` tail drops; fix placement/cut capacity).
