## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (The Python Data Model: `FrenchDeck`, `spades_high`, `Vector`, and Context Managers, `FP` Chapter 1):** Implementing `__len__` and `__getitem__` on `FrenchDeck` unlocks `len()` (`52`, which for built-in types reads `PyVarObject.ob_size` in $O(1)$), slicing (`deck[12::13]`), `in`, `reversed()`, `random.choice()`, and `sorted(deck, key=spades_high)` (`0` to `51`); equal objects (`a == b`) must share `hash(a) == hash(b)`, binary dunders return `NotImplemented` on unsupported types to trigger reflected operators, and `__exit__` returning `True` suppresses the exception whereas `False`/`None` propagates it.
2. **Exercise 2 (Sequences, Slicing, `deque`/`array`/`memoryview`, and the `t[2] += [50, 60]` Puzzle, `FP` Chapter 2):** Container sequences (`list`, `tuple`, `deque`) hold object references while flat sequences (`str`, `bytes`, `array.array`, `memoryview`) pack primitive values contiguously; `deque(maxlen=N)` gives $O(1)$ double-ended ring buffering and `bisect` maintains sorted lists; and in `demonstrate_tuple_inplace_add_puzzle()`, `t[2] += [50, 60]` executes `BINARY_SUBSCR`, `INPLACE_ADD` (`list.__iadd__`, mutating `t[2]` in place to `[30, 40, 50, 60]`), and then fails at `STORE_SUBSCR` on the immutable tuple with `TypeError`.
3. **Exercise 3 (Mappings, Sets, `__missing__` in `StrKeyDict0`, `ChainMap`, and `MappingProxyType`, `FP` Chapter 3):** `d.setdefault(k, []).append(v)` and `defaultdict(list)` perform a single hash lookup per insertion (versus two for `d.get` + assignment), and CPython's compact dict layout preserves insertion order; in `StrKeyDict0`, `__missing__` checks `if isinstance(key, str): raise KeyError(key)` before recursing on `self[str(key)]`, and `__contains__` checks `self.keys()` directly to avoid infinite recursion.
4. **Exercise 4 (Object References, `HauntedBus` vs. `TwilightBus`, `weakref.finalize`, Closures, and First-Class Strategy, `FP` Chapters 6, 7, 9, 10):** `HauntedBus` shares a mutable default `passengers=[]` across instances whereas `TwilightBus` aliases the caller's list (`self.passengers = passengers` instead of `list(passengers)`), and `del` deletes a label while `weakref.finalize` fires when the object's refcount hits zero; `make_averager()` uses `nonlocal count, total` for $O(1)$-memory stateful closures, and `@promotion` + `best_promo()` replace stateless Strategy subclasses (`FidelityPromo`, `BulkItemPromo`, `LargeOrderPromo`) with registered functions.
5. **Exercise 5 (Comprehensions, Matrix Operations, and Variable Scope vs. `:=` Leakage, `LP` Chapters 14 & 20):** On 3x3 matrices `M` and `N`, `[row[1] for row in M]` extracts column 1, `[M[i][i] for i in range(len(M))]` extracts the main diagonal, `[[col + 10 for col in row] for row in M]` adds 10 preserving shape, and `[[col1 * col2 for (col1, col2) in zip(row1, row2)] for (row1, row2) in zip(M, N)]` multiplies pairwise; comprehension `for` targets are local to the comprehension (`X = 99` unchanged), whereas walrus assignments (`temp := S[i]`) leak into the enclosing scope.
6. **Exercise 6 (Iterables vs. Single-Pass Iterators, `scramble` vs. `permute`, `myzip`/`mymapPad`, and `yield from` Coroutines, `LP` Ch. 20 & `FP` Ch. 17):** Iterables (`list`, `Countdown`, `Sentence`) return a fresh iterator from `__iter__()`, whereas iterators/generators return `self` (`iter(G) is G` is `True`, single-pass); `scramble1('hack')` yields $N=4$ rotations while `permute2('hack')` lazily yields $N!=24$ permutations (scaling to $10!$ and $50!$ where list-building `permute1` hangs); `myzip_gen` truncates via `while all(lists):` while `mymap_pad_gen` pads via `while any(lists):`; and `delegate_averager()` uses `res = yield from averager_coro()` to forward `.send()` values and capture `Result(count, average)` from `StopIteration.value`.
