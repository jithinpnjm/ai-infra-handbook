## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Inside `kube-scheduler`: Queues, Scheduling Framework, and `MostAllocated` Bin-Packing):** Pods move through `activeQ`, `unschedulablePods`, and `backoffQ` into the serial Scheduling Cycle (`PreFilter -> Filter -> PostFilter -> Score -> Reserve -> Permit`) and async Binding Cycle (`PreBind -> Bind -> PostBind`); `simulate_gpu_placement_and_fragmentation()` shows `MostAllocated` packs small GPU pods onto one node so whole 8-GPU nodes remain free for training.
2. **Exercise 2 (Placement Constraints: `nodeSelector`, `nodeAffinity`, `podAntiAffinity`, `topologySpreadConstraints`, and `Taints`/`Tolerations`):** Dedicated GPU node pools require both a taint (`NoSchedule`) + toleration (to repel CPU pods) and a `nodeSelector`/`nodeAffinity` (to attract GPU pods); `nodeSelectorTerms` are OR'd while `matchExpressions` within a term are AND'd; and `topologySpreadConstraints` (`maxSkew`) avoids the hard node-count ceiling and $O(\text{Pods}\times\text{Nodes})$ cost of `required` `podAntiAffinity`.
3. **Exercise 3 (PriorityClasses, Preemption, `preemptionPolicy: Never`, and Training Disruption Hazards):** `DefaultPreemption` nominates a node (`nominatedNodeName`) by evicting lower-priority pods, which can destroy multi-node training progress if 1 worker pod is killed; setting `preemptionPolicy: Never` on batch training PriorityClasses orders the queue by priority without evicting running jobs.
4. **Exercise 4 (Solving the Gang-Scheduling Deadlock with `Kueue` vs. `Volcano` and `Coscheduling`):** `evaluate_kueue_cohort_gang_admission()` shows Kueue keeps jobs `suspend: true` until the `ClusterQueue` (plus `Cohort` lendable capacity up to `borrowingLimit`) can admit all pods of the workload simultaneously, eliminating partial-allocation deadlocks.
5. **Exercise 5 (Topology-Aware and Device-Aware Scheduling: `NodeResourceTopology`, Kueue `TAS`, and `DRA`):** `allocate_dra_same_pcie_root()` models DRA's `DynamicResources` scheduler plugin evaluating CEL selectors and `matchAttribute: "resource.kubernetes.io/pcieRoot"` constraints against `ResourceSlice`s at schedule time, preventing post-binding `TopologyAffinityError`s.
6. **Exercise 6 (Diagnosing `FailedScheduling` Events and Scaling to 2,000+ Nodes):** `kube-scheduler` compares `resources.requests` against `Node.status.allocatable` (never live `DCGM` utilization), and multi-reason `FailedScheduling` counts overlap across plugins.
