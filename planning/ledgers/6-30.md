## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1 (Reproducing the NVIDIA DGX B200 Rack Power Table):** `facility_calc.py` computes $2 \times (\sqrt{3} \times 415 \times 60 \times 0.80 \times 0.95 / 1000) = 65.6\text{ kW}$ capacity for 4 DGX B200 servers (`57.2 kW` demand), leaving `8.4 kW` stranded power.
2. **Exercise 2 (Evaluating 400 V / 63 A Rack Circuits and Stranded Power):** Two 3-phase `400 V`, `63 A` circuits at `80%` derating and `0.95` PF provide $2 \times 33.17 = 66.3\text{ kW}$, fitting `4` DGX B200 servers (`57.2 kW`) with `9.1 kW` stranded power.
3. **Exercise 3 (Analyzing Server Internal `5-of-6` PSU Redundancy vs Facility Dual-Feed Topologies):** Because a DGX B200 requires 5 of 6 PSUs energized, wiring 3 PSUs to UPS A and 3 to UPS B causes *every* server to lose 3 PSUs and crash when either UPS fails; wiring half the servers (all 6 PSUs) to UPS A and half to UPS B limits a UPS failure to 50% of servers.
4. **Exercise 4 (Calculating Rack Airflow `CFM`, PUE, and Monthly Electricity Cost):** At `150 CFM/kW`, 2 and 4 DGX B200 servers require `4,290` and `8,580 CFM` (and `3.16 * 81,600 / 36 = 7,163 CFM`); at `PUE = 1.35` and `$0.12/kWh`, the 8-node H100 cluster consumes `60,000 kWh/month` (`$7,200/month`, `$86,400/year`).
5. **Exercise 5 (Simulating Staged UPS Cluster Shutdown and Ambient Temperature Protection):** Shedding the 40 idle nodes at $t+30\text{ min}$ extends battery runtime for the 60 busy nodes by 15 minutes (`t+101 min` vs `t+86 min`); `ambtemp.sh` queries `.1.3.6.1.4.1.318.1.1.10.2.3.2.1.4.0` and `upstemp` warns above `25.0` °C and halts above `30.0` °C.
6. **Exercise 6 (Comparing Air Cooling, Cold-Plate DLC, Rear-Door Heat Exchangers, and Immersion Cooling):** Air cooling with hot/cold aisle containment suits $\le 30\text{–}40\text{ kW}$ racks; rear-door heat exchangers retrofit $\sim 20\text{–}40\text{ kW}$ racks; direct-to-chip cold plates with CDUs handle $60\text{–}130+\text{ kW}$ racks (`GB200/GB300`); and single/two-phase immersion submerges whole servers for maximum density at higher operational complexity.
