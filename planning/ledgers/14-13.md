## 4. One-Line Expected Exercise Solutions

1. **Exercise 1:** (a) Across `Mistral-7B` (`0.481 -> 0.875` vs `0.504`/`0.588`), `Llama 2-7B` (`0.353 -> 0.585` vs `0.219`/`0.392`), and `Orca 2-7B` (`0.456 -> 0.876` vs `0.511`/`0.566`), `Base + RAG` beats fine-tuning by **`28` to `36` percentage points** because current-events QA is an information-based task, while `Llama 2-7B` `FT-reg` dropped to `0.219` due to catastrophic forgetting and hallucination; (b) removing `2,000` prompt tokens at `$2.50 / 1M` saves **`$0.005`/request (`$2,500`/year at `500,000` requests/year)**, requiring **`10.0` years (`5,000,000` requests)** to recoup a `$25,000` API fine-tuning fee—**uneconomical (`False`)** for a `2.0`-year model lifespan.
2. **Exercise 2:** (a) In pure 16-bit (`AIE` Ch. 7), `13B` requires **`26.0 GB` weights, `31.2 GB` inference (`1.2x`), and `104.0 GB` full training (`26.0 + 78.0 GB`, or `32.0 GB` with `1B` trainable)**, while `7B` requires **`56.0 GB` (`14.0 + 42.0 GB`)**; under the `16 B/param` mixed-precision baseline (`LEH` Ch. 5), `7B` requires **`112.0 GB`** and `70B` requires **`1,120.0 GB`**; (b) `GPT-3 175B` (`r = 2` on 4 matrices) has **`196,608` params/layer and `18,874,368` (`18.87M`) total params (`37.7 MB` in 16-bit)**, while `Llama 3 8B` has **`41,943,040` (`~42M`, `0.5196%`) params at `r = 16`** across all 7 linear modules and **`88,121,344` params at `r = 32` including `lm_head`**.
3. **Exercise 3:** (a) On a `4,096 x 4,096` (`16.8M`) matrix with `r = 8`, one `(A, B)` pair has **`65,536` parameters**, `100` merged matrices (`Option 1`) require **`1.68B` parameters**, and `1` shared base matrix + `100` `(A, B)` adapters (`Option 2`) require **`23.3M` parameters (`72.1x` storage reduction)**; (b) the **Punica** kernel batches the shared `W_0 X` GEMM across all requests and applies per-request `B_i (A_i x_i)` deltas via segmented gather-scatter, whereas `merge_and_unload()` is preferred when a single adapter's traffic saturates dedicated GPU replicas.
4. **Exercise 4:** (a) Single block-wise quantization (`FP32` per `B_1 = 64`) adds **`0.500` bits/param**, whereas Double Quantization (`FP8` per `B_1 = 64` + `FP32` per `B_2 = 256` blocks) adds **`0.127` bits/param** (`8/64 + 32/16384`), saving **`0.373` bits/param** (`4.127` effective bits/param) and freeing **`3.03 GB` on a `65B` model**; (b) on a `7B` model, QLoRA reduces init VRAM by **`35%` (`14.0 -> 9.1 GB`)** and peak training VRAM by **`40%` (`15.6 -> 9.3 GB`)** at **`~30%` slower training speed**, while `paged_adamw_8bit` pages optimizer states between GPU HBM and CPU DRAM via CUDA Unified Memory during sequence-length spikes.
5. **Exercise 5:** (a) Effective batch sizes are **`16`** (`2 * 1 * 8`) and **`32`** (`4 * 2 * 4`); packing the `8` samples (`1,960` tokens) into `max_seq_length = 1,024` reduces slots from **`8` (`23.93%` utilization) to `2` (`95.70%` utilization, `4.0x` reduction)**; (b) for `4` prompt tokens (loss `2.0`) and `2` response tokens (loss `0.5`), weighted loss is **`0.50` at `0%` weight**, **`0.75` at `10%` weight**, and **`1.50` at `100%` weight**.
6. **Exercise 6:** (a) At `beta = 0.5`, DPO yields **`mean_chosen_reward = +0.75`**, **`mean_rejected_reward = -1.00`**, **`mean_reward_margin = +1.75`**, and **`reward_accuracy = 75.0%` (`3/4`)**; 8B model-state VRAM is **`160.0 GB` (Full PPO)**, **`96.0 GB` (Full GRPO)**, **`80.0 GB` (Full DPO)**, and **`16.64 GB` (LoRA DPO with `ref_model=None`)**; (b) for rewards `[1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 6.0]`, GRPO advantages are **`[-1.069045, -0.534522, 0.0, +1.603567]` (summing to `0.0`)**; DARE at `p = 0.8` rescales by **`5.0x` (`1 / 0.2`)**, `SOLAR 10.7B` has **`48` layers** (`32 * 2 - 16`), and `Goliath-120B` has **`144` layers** (`72 * 2`).
