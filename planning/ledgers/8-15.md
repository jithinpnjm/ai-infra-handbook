- **Scalable Unit (SU) & Rack Sizing:** `1` SU = `8` NVL72 compute racks = `144` compute trays (`18` trays/rack) = `576` B300 GPUs (`4` B300 GPUs + `2` Grace CPUs per tray, `72` GPUs/rack), `~1.2 MW` TDP per SU. - **Four Networks & Bandwidth per SU:** Compute fabric (`4 x ConnectX-8` `800 Gb/s` per tray $\rightarrow$ `460.8 Tb/s` per SU on Quantum-X800 IB fat-tree with UFM and NMX Manager); Storage fabric (`1 x 400 Gb/s` BlueField-3 port per tray $\rightarrow$ `57.6 Tb/s` per SU on Spectrum-4 `SN5600` RoCEv2); In-band network (`>= 200 Gb/s` BlueField-3 port per tray $\rightarrow$ `28.8 Tb/s` per SU, `2 x 100GbE` eBGP edge); OOB network (`SN2201` `48 x 1 Gb/s`).
