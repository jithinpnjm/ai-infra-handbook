# 20.21 Request routing, retries and overload control
Words: ~12,200 (about 18% is the simulator and YAML) | Read time: 81 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-20-nebius-sre-interview/21-request-routing-retries-and-overload-control.md
One-line purpose: Teaches which failures are safe to retry on a streaming LLM API, how timeouts nest, why retries amplify outages, and how budgets, jitter, ejection, shedding (429 versus 503), draining and routing keep a fleet alive during partial failure.
Beginner friendly: no on first read. It assumes HTTP status codes, TCP basics, Kubernetes Services and pod termination, and Envoy or Istio concepts. Read first: Part 2 chapter 06 (TCP in depth, windows and retransmission), Part 2 chapter 10 (HTTP, TLS and PKI basics), Part 2 chapter 20 (load balancing L4 and L7), Part 5 chapter 10 (services, endpoints and kube-proxy), Part 5 chapter 09 (kubelet, probes and eviction), and Part 5 chapter 28 (service mesh). Then 20.19 for the SLO that retries protect.
Nebius rounds served: R3 (primary: "clients retry on failure, what could go wrong" and the design of a retry policy), R2 (incident triage: retry storms, rolling-update stream cuts, 429 versus 503), R4 (deep-dive follow-ups on routing decisions)
