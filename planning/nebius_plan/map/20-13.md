Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- "Design a multi-tenant LLM inference API. You have 45 minutes. Where do you start?" (Expect: clarifying questions, then numbers, not boxes.)
- "How many GPUs do you need for 1,000 requests per second with 1,500 prompt and 300 output tokens?" (Expect the in-flight count of 9,500, KV per request about 0.236 GB, and about 40 GPUs by memory, stated as assumptions.)
- "Your GPU utilisation is 40% but TTFT doubled. What do you check first?" (Expect queue, KV usage, prefix hit rate, ready versus desired replicas, per-tenant token rates; mitigate before root cause.)
- "One tenant sends a few huge prompts and everyone else slows down. How do you rate limit and isolate?" (Expect token buckets, reserve and refund, per-tenant slot caps, priority, dedicated endpoints.)
- "A replica is slow but not failing. What happens, and how do you stop a retry storm?" (Expect routing away, circuit breaker with ejection cap, lenient liveness probe, retry budget with jitter, one retry layer.)
- "An EU customer requires that prompts never leave the EU, and the EU region is down. What does your design do?" (Expect no silent failover; opt-in cross-region failover, spare capacity per region, cost stated.)
