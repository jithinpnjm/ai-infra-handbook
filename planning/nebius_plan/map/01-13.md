# 1.13 LVM, RAID and NVMe
Words: ~15,600 | Read time: ~104 min | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-01-linux/13-lvm-raid-and-nvme.md

One-line purpose: Shows how to pool, grow, snapshot and move disks with LVM, pick a software RAID level and rebuild it, read NVMe health and link state, and decide when local NVMe should cache datasets and checkpoints for GPU jobs.
Beginner friendly: partly. The opener, LVM intro and RAID level table are readable after 1.12 (partitions, filesystems). The device-mapper tables, multipath and NVMe-oF sections are intermediate. Read 1.12 first, and 1.10 (sysfs, `/dev`) for the device names.
Nebius rounds served: R1 (LVM/RAID/disk troubleshooting, `iostat`, `smart-log`), R2 (local volumes and disk-full or degraded-node incidents on Kubernetes), R3 (storage sizing, RAID choice, checkpoint and dataset staging in a design), R4 (storage choices on your own project)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS. Read in full: the LVM "problem" and "Resizing" parts, "Moving, removing and renaming", "Snapshots and thin provisioning", the RAID levels table and "Rebuilds", the `nvme-cli` "Reading `smart-log`" block, the disk-performance method (USE, workload characterisation), section 7 (data path and local NVMe staging), and "How it fails". Skim the `dmsetup` and multipath material for recall only. Skip the Sources list.

Hands-on to do:
- `sudo pvs; sudo vgs; sudo lvs -o +devices,segtype; lsblk -f` on any Linux box, and explain each `Attr` column.
- Build the loop-device LVM drill (lab steps A and B): create a VG, grow an LV with `lvextend -r`, run `pvmove`, then take a snapshot. Use disposable `/tmp` loop images only.
- Build the md RAID 1 drill (lab step D): `mdadm --create`, then `--fail` and `--add`, and watch `cat /proc/mdstat` go from `[U_]` back to `[UU]`.
- Run `iostat -xz 1 5` in one terminal while `fio --rw=randread --bs=4k --iodepth=1` runs in another (lab step H). Compare `r_await` and `%util` at queue depth 1 and 64.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
1. A training node's `/data` LV is full, and the underlying disk has free space. Walk me through the fix, and what you must not do.
2. A cloud disk was resized from 200 GB to 400 GB. What commands do you run, in what order, and why?
3. Compare RAID 0, 5, 6 and 10 for a local scratch volume, a database and a large HDD archive. Which would you use for each?
4. An NVMe drive is 30% slower than its neighbours. What do you check first: `smart-log`, link width, temperature, firmware?
5. `fio` shows 3.5 GB/s on the raw NVMe, but the training job reads only 150 MB/s. How do you find the bottleneck?
6. When would you stage a dataset to local NVMe for epochs 2 to N, and when is that a waste of effort?
