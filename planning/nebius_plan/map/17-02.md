# 17.2 Prometheus and Grafana
Words: 11900 | Read time: 79 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-17-operations/02-prometheus-and-grafana.md
One-line purpose: Explains how Prometheus scrapes, stores and alerts on metrics, how PromQL and recording rules keep dashboards fast, and how Grafana and retention are run as a production service.
Beginner friendly: partly. The pull model, the exporter idea and the PromQL examples are readable, but the Operator CRDs (ServiceMonitor, PrometheusRule) and federation assume Kubernetes comfort. Read 17.1 first (it defines cardinality and the three pillars) and do a short PromQL primer (rate, sum by, histogram_quantile) before section 2.
Nebius rounds served: R2, R3, R4
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (1.1, 1.2, 1.9, 1.10, 1.11, 2.1-2.3, 3.2, 4.1-4.2, 6), because PromQL, alerting with a dead man's switch, recording rules, retention and the six failure modes are what an SRE is asked; skip most of the Operator YAML and the long federation prose, and treat 2.4 showback as optional.

Hands-on to do:
- `curl -s 'http://localhost:9090/api/v1/query?query=up' | jq .` after a port-forward; confirm one result per scrape target and know what up = 0 means.
- Write three PromQL queries from memory: per-model 5xx rate (`sum by (model) (rate(...{status=~"5.."}[5m]))`), p99 with histogram_quantile, and GPU memory fraction (section 2.1-2.2).
- Check retention math: 15 s scrape, 2 bytes per sample, 30 days = about 346 KB per series (section 4.2).
- Run the 7.4 verify step on any cluster you can reach: port-forward and query one exporter metric.
