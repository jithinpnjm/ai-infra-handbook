# 5.23 Autoscaling: HPA, VPA, KEDA and the cluster autoscaler

One-line purpose: How pods and nodes scale in response to load, and why GPU inference and multi-node training need different signals and different node-layer behaviour.
Beginner friendly: partly. The layer diagram and the "relay race" framing are readable. The HPA manifest, KEDA triggers and the autoscaler flags are not, until you have done one HPA in a kind cluster. Read 5.7 (Deployments and probes), 5.9 (graceful shutdown) and 5.18 (scheduling, Pending pods) first.
Nebius rounds served: R2 (Pending pods, HPA not scaling, CA not scaling down), R3 (capacity and cost design for inference, slow start, availability), R4 (what you tuned and why)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (1 layers and the spike walkthrough; 2 algorithm, behaviour tuning, inference scaling signals, metric debugging; 5 CA scale-up/down and blockers, overprovisioning, ProvisioningRequest; 6 workload table; 7 troubleshooting; 9, 10; checkpoints 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 11, 12, 13, 15, 16). Skip the CPA, the CA config YAML, the Karpenter detail beyond its concept, the custom-autoscaling prose and the Sources. The leading-versus-lagging signal and the pause-pod overprovisioning idea are the two ideas to own.

Hands-on to do:
- On kind with metrics-server: a Deployment with `resources.requests.cpu`, an HPA at 50% CPU, then `hey -z 60s -c 50 <url>` and `kubectl describe hpa <name>`. Read the conditions and the events.
- Remove `resources.requests` from the same Deployment and run `kubectl get hpa`. Confirm the `<unknown>` value, then put the requests back.
- Check the custom metrics path with `kubectl get --raw "/apis/custom.metrics.k8s.io/v1beta1/namespaces/<ns>/pods/*/<metric>" | jq .` (if you have prometheus-adapter).
- Install VPA in recommender mode (`updateMode: "Off"`), then `kubectl describe vpa <name>` and compare its Target with your requests.
- On a cloud cluster with CA: `kubectl -n kube-system get cm cluster-autoscaler-status -o yaml`, and find one pod that blocks scale-down.
- Run the chapter's lab: `python3 code/part05/autoscaling_hpa_ca_simulator_lab.py`, and change the tolerance and the stabilisation window to see the replica output change.
