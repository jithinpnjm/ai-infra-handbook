# 2.4 IP addressing, subnetting and CIDR (IPv4 and IPv6), with practice
Words: ~11100 | Read time: ~74 min | File: docs/part-02-networking/04-ip-addressing-subnetting-and-cidr-ipv4-and-ipv6-with-practice.md
One-line purpose: Teaches IPv4 subnetting by hand (block-size method), VLSM and summarisation, special-use ranges, IPv6 notation and address types, the IPv4/IPv6 headers with PMTUD, and how to plan addresses for a GPU cluster.
Beginner friendly: partly. It starts from binary and walks one conversion slowly, so a beginner can follow, but the arithmetic needs drilling before the interview. Prerequisite: 2.2 (layer model). Binary and hex basics help.
Nebius rounds served: R1 (subnetting arithmetic, IPv6, MTU test), R2 (MTU/PMTUD and Linux address config troubleshooting), R3 (pod/service CIDR sizing, IP plan for capacity), R4 (own project's IP plan)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS. Read sections 2 (IPv4 masks, block-size method, same-subnet check), 3 (VLSM and summarisation), 4 (special ranges), 6 (IPv6 notation, address types, /64 structure), 7 (headers, fragmentation and PMTU), 9 (cluster plan), 10 (practice problems) and checkpoints 1-10 and 17. SKIM the rest; SKIP wildcard, IPv6 history and the nine-octet trivia unless asked.

Hands-on to do:
- Work `192.168.10.77/27` and `172.20.133.9/21` on paper, then check with `ipcalc 172.20.133.9/21` or `python3 -c "import ipaddress as i; print(i.ip_interface('172.20.133.9/21').network)"`.
- `ip -br addr`, `ip route get <host in subnet>` and `ip route get <host outside subnet>` on your VM, and work out each interface's network and broadcast.
- `ping -M do -s 1472 -c 2 <host>` (IPv4, should pass at 1500) then `-s 1473` (should fail with frag needed), and `tracepath <host>`.
- IPv6: `ip -6 addr show`, then `ping -6 -c3 fe80::1%eth0` and `ip -6 neigh show`; derive the EUI-64 address of your NIC's MAC by hand.
- Run `python3 code/part02/ip_subnetting_cidr_lab.py` locally (per chapter; the file is in the book's code folder).

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- Subnet 10.0.0.0/16 into 100 equal subnets. What prefix, and how many hosts per subnet?
- What is the broadcast address of 172.16.5.77/27, and is 172.16.5.100 in the same subnet?
- Design the IP plan for a new 512-node GPU cluster with 8 rails and Kubernetes. What do you reserve for growth?
- Why is the pod CIDR hard to change later, and how do you avoid overlaps with on-prem networks and VPC peering?
- Why are IPv6 subnets /64, and how many /64s fit in a /48?
- Small HTTP requests work but large downloads hang. How does PMTUD explain it, and what do you check first?
