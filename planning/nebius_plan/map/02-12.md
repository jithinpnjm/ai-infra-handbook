# 2.12 Link aggregation, LACP, MLAG and ECMP
Words: ~8970 | Read time: ~60 min | File: docs/part-02-networking/12-link-aggregation-lacp-and-mlag.md
One-line purpose: Explains why parallel links balance by flow hash rather than per packet, how LACP and Linux bonding are configured and checked, how MLAG and EVPN multihoming differ, and how routed ECMP behaves under failure, so you can explain why one bond member or one ECMP path carries everything.
Beginner friendly: partly. The "one flow, one link" idea and the bonding-mode table are beginner-reachable. LACP timers, MLAG failure modes and resilient hashing are intermediate. Read 2.11 (VLANs and STP) and 2.5 (routing and multipath) first.
Nebius rounds served: R1 (read /proc/net/bonding, LACP partner and aggregator checks, ip route get for ECMP), R2 (one member carries all traffic, bond flaps, ECMP blackhole after a switch failure), R3 (host attachment design: why compute rails are not bonded, MLAG vs routed to host), R4 (how your project's node networks and bonds were built)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS. Read 1 (flow hashing and why round robin breaks TCP), 2 (LACP actors, active/passive, timers, aggregation), 3 (bonding mode table, xmit_hash_policy, the Netplan block and the /proc/net/bonding checks, and the "Do not bond the rails" paragraph), 4 (MLAG failure modes and the attachment table), 5 (ECMP hash fields, resilient hashing, elephant flows, ip route get), 6 (the five networks, not the topology list), 7 (troubleshooting table and layered steps), 8 (lab steps 1, 2 and 5 and the Python lab) and exercises 1, 2 and 5. SKIM the latency figures and topology comparison in section 6. Reason: bonding and ECMP symptoms are classic R1/R2 questions, and the "do not bond compute rails" rule is a design answer, but cluster topology trivia is outside this reader's needs.

Hands-on to do:
- Read a bond: `cat /proc/net/bonding/bond0` on a host with a bond. Check mode, hash policy, that every member shows the same Aggregator ID, a non-zero Partner Mac Address, and MII Status up.
- Show the hash effect: run `iperf3 -P 8` with `xmit_hash_policy=layer2`, then `layer3+4`, and watch per-member counters with `ip -s link`. Repeat with a single stream and note that one member carries it.
- ECMP next hop: `ip route get 10.200.0.1 ipproto tcp sport 40000 dport 443` and vary sport. Set `net.ipv4.fib_multipath_hash_policy` to 1 and compare.
- Failover timing: run `ping -i 0.1 <peer>` while bringing one bond member down with `ip link set <member> down`, and measure the gap.
- Python lab: `python3 code/part02/lacp_mlag_ecmp_lab.py` (hash spread, 75% vs 25% remap, LACP rules, bonding audit).
