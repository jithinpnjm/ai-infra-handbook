Hands-on to do:
- `cc -o hello hello.c && strace -f -tt ./hello 2>&1 | head -60`, then `strace -c ./hello` and `ltrace ./hello`. Find `execve`, the `ld.so.cache` open, `write`, and `exit_group`.
- Break a library on purpose: build `libfoo.so.1` with `-Wl,-soname`, link `app` with `-L. -lfoo`, see it fail, then use `LD_DEBUG=libs ./app`, then `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=.` once, then `-Wl,-rpath,'$ORIGIN'` and check with `readelf -d app2`.
- Symbol versions: `ldd --version | head -1` and `objdump -T /bin/ls | grep -o 'GLIBC_[0-9.]*' | sort -Vu | tail -3`.
- ELF inspection: `readelf -l hello | grep -E 'INTERP|LOAD'`, `readelf -d hello | grep NEEDED`, `nm -D hello | head`, then strip a copy and compare.
- GPU VM only: `strace -f -e trace=openat python3 -c 'import torch; torch.zeros(1).cuda()' 2>&1 | grep '\.so'`. Check whether the first failing open is an ENOENT (usually harmless) or the final error.

2. A binary fails with `version GLIBC_2.34 not found` on one host and works on another. Why, and how do you prevent it in CI?
3. A CUDA job says `libcudart.so.12: cannot open shared object file`. Walk me through the diagnosis.
4. How do you find which library file a process actually loaded?
5. A training job is hung, GPUs are idle, and the logs are silent. What do you capture, and in what order?
6. What is the difference between `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` and an embedded RPATH or RUNPATH, and which one do you want in a production image?
