# 20.1 The Nebius loop and how to use this volume
Words: ~6600 | Read time: 44 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-20-nebius-sre-interview/01-the-nebius-loop-and-how-to-use-this-volume.md
One-line purpose: Explains the Nebius interview stages (official vs. candidate reports), gives a think-aloud script and a stuck-protocol, and maps every later volume-20 chapter to the round it serves.
Beginner friendly: yes. Mostly process and advice; the few technical terms (distributed system, KV cache, quadratic, two pointers, off-by-one) are defined inline. Optional read-first for the "Worked scenarios" and the architecture block: 20.9 (two pointers / sliding window) before Scenario 1; 20.30 before 20.38 if you want the platform context.
Nebius rounds served: R1, R2, R3, R4 (process, think-aloud method used in every round, recruiter and final-hour questions).
Chapter verdict: MUST-READ-FULL, because it is the navigation spine of the volume and sets the think-aloud script (section 5) that every round depends on; about 45 minutes.

Hands-on to do:
1. Join a Zoom test meeting with your headphones and check your background and microphone on the recording (section 7).
2. Pick one easy problem from chapter 20.11, set a 25-minute timer, and solve it aloud while recording, using the seven steps from section 5.
3. Play the recording back and count silences longer than 30 seconds; the chapter's target is three or fewer.
4. Send the five questions from section 9 to your recruiter and write the answers at the top of your study notes.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- Tell me about yourself and one production system you have run; what was your part in it?
- Why Nebius, and what do you know about Token Factory?
- What is your weakest area for this role, and what have you done about it this month?
- Walk me through how you would approach a problem you have not seen before, out loud.
- You are stuck on this problem. What do you do next?
- Do you have any questions for us?
