# 20.20 Autoscaling GPU inference
Words: ~11,700 (about 17% is the simulator code and YAML) | Read time: 78 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-20-nebius-sre-interview/20-autoscaling-gpu-inference.md
One-line purpose: Explains why CPU and GPU utilisation fail as autoscaling signals for vLLM, which signal to scale on, the HPA ratio formula, flapping and stabilization windows, scale-up latency, floors, and safe scale-down for streams.
Beginner friendly: no on first read. It assumes Kubernetes Deployments, pods, HPA, probes and the cluster autoscaler. Read first: Part 5 chapter 23 (autoscaling: HPA, VPA, KEDA and the cluster autoscaler), Part 15 chapter 15 (autoscaling inference, HPA, KEDA and scale-to-zero), and Part 17 chapter 02 (Prometheus, for the metric names). Also Part 5 chapter 09 (kubelet, probes, QoS and eviction) for the termination and grace-period parts of section 7. Then 20.12 (estimation toolkit) for the per-replica rate and 20.18 (how serving fails) for cold start and restart herds.
Nebius rounds served: R3 (primary: "how does capacity follow load" in the design round), R2 (Kubernetes troubleshooting, scale-down and pending pods), R4 (deep-dive follow-ups on capacity decisions), R1 (only the ideas, not the tools)
