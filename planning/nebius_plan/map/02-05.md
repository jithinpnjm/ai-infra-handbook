# 2.5 Routing on a host: routing tables, default gateways and forwarding
Words: ~10000 | Read time: ~67 min | File: docs/part-02-networking/05-routing-on-a-host-routing-tables-default-gateways-and-forwarding.md
One-line purpose: Teaches how a Linux host picks a route (longest prefix, metric, rules and tables), reading `ip route` and `ip route get`, source selection, the multi-NIC weak-host and rp_filter problem, host-as-router forwarding, ECMP, and a six-network GPU server case study.
Beginner friendly: partly. The routing-table reading and `ip route get` are approachable; policy routing, rp_filter and the multi-NIC case need 2.3 (ARP) and 2.4 (subnets) first, and the case study is advanced.
Nebius rounds served: R1 (reading routes, console troubleshooting), R2 (pod/node routing, multi-NIC debugging), R4 (own project: multi-NIC GPU host design)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS. Read sections 1 (routing decision), 2 (reading the table, longest-prefix and metrics, `ip route get`), 4 (source address), 5 (weak host, rp_filter, SSH-on-one-NIC diagnosis), 3 (tables and rules only), 11 (diagnosis order) and checkpoints 1-4, 8, 11, 12, 19 and 20. SKIM sections 6-10 and 12 for recall; SKIP the ICMP redirect and multipath hash-policy detail unless asked.

Hands-on to do:
- `ip route show`, `ip -6 route show`, `ip rule show` on your VM; for each line say which of the five routing outcomes it implies.
- `ip route get <a host in your subnet>` and `ip route get <a public IP>`, then `ip route get <public IP> from <your NIC address>`.
- Lab: build `hA(10.1.0.2/24) -- r -- hB(10.2.0.2/24)` in namespaces, observe the ping fail with "Network is unreachable", add routes and `ip_forward=1`, then `tcpdump -ni rA` to see TTL 64 to 63 with IPs unchanged.
- `sysctl net.ipv4.conf.all.rp_filter net.ipv4.conf.<if>.rp_filter` and `nstat -az | egrep 'IPReversePathFilter|IpInAddrErrors'` on a multi-NIC host.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- How does a Linux host choose a route when two routes match? Cover longest prefix, metric and policy rules.
- What does `default via 10.0.0.1 dev eth0` mean, and what must the gateway satisfy?
- A server has two NICs on two subnets and the reply leaves the wrong NIC. Why, and how do you fix it?
- What does `ip_forward` do? What else do you need to build a NAT router?
- Explain the `ip route get` output. What is the `src` field, and how is it chosen?
- How do pods reach pods on other nodes in your CNI of choice? Where are the routes?
- Why would you install a blackhole route, and what does it do differently from an unreachable route?
