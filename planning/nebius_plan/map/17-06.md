# 17.6 Alerting, SLOs and health checks for GPU clusters
Words: 8300 | Read time: 56 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-17-operations/06-alerting-slos-and-health-checks-for-gpu-clusters.md
One-line purpose: Turns metrics into pages and promises: SLIs and SLOs, error-budget arithmetic, multi-window burn-rate alerts, Alertmanager routing and inhibition, Kubernetes probes, and a runbook template.
Beginner friendly: partly. The SLO and error-budget sections are the most teachable in the whole part, and the arithmetic is worked out line by line. Section 1.4 (burn rates) is advanced for a beginner, so read 1.1-1.3 first, then 1.4 with a calculator. Probes (3.1-3.2) need basic Kubernetes pod knowledge. Read 17.2 first (Alertmanager and PromQL).
Nebius rounds served: R2, R3, R4
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (1.1-1.4, 2.2, 3.1-3.2, 4.1-4.2, 6, 7.8 only), because SLO and error-budget arithmetic, burn-rate paging, routing and probes are the most likely SRE questions, while the lab and GPU-node script are optional and the cross-referenced token-API chapter 20.19 is not part of this study plan.

Hands-on to do:
- Do the error-budget arithmetic by hand: 99.9% over 30 days = 43.2 minutes; 99.95% = 21.6 minutes; burn rate 14.4 over 1 hour = 2% of a 30-day budget (section 1.3-1.4).
- Work the 100 requests per second example: 259,200,000 requests a month, 259,200 allowed failures, 2% error ratio = 36 hours to empty (section 1.4).
- Run `kubectl describe pod <name>` and read the probe section; then change a liveness path to a non-existent URL in a scratch deployment and watch the restart count (section 3.2).
- Send a synthetic alert to Alertmanager with `curl -X POST .../api/v2/alerts` and confirm the receiver in `/api/v2/alerts` (section 7.5-7.6), if you have a cluster.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- Define SLI, SLO and error budget, then compute the minutes for 99.9% per month.
- Why do burn-rate alerts use two windows instead of one threshold?
- Your liveness probe calls the database and every pod restarts during a database outage. Diagnose it.
- A cluster is down and you get 300 pages. What do you change in Alertmanager?
- What goes in a runbook, and walk me through a GPU node that reports a fatal Xid.
- An SLO graph is red but nobody was paged. Where do you look first?
