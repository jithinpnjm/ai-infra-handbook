# 20.25 PromQL, Grafana and tracing: the traps interviewers test
Words: ~9,070 | Read time: ~60 min (full chapter incl. YAML and tables) | File: docs/part-20-nebius-sre-interview/25-promql-grafana-and-tracing-traps.md
One-line purpose: Fifteen PromQL, alert, dashboard and tracing mistakes an interviewer shows you on a screen, each with a fix you can test with promtool and amtool.
Beginner friendly: partly. The ideas are explained in plain words, but it assumes you know what a metric, a label, a counter and a scrape are. Read 17.2 (Prometheus and PromQL basics) first, and skim 20.19 (SLO and burn-rate) for the alert vocabulary.
Nebius rounds served: R2 (troubleshooting on dashboards), R3 (system design: how you alert and trace), R4 (if your own project has dashboards or alerts)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS. The query traps (counters, rate, histograms, absent, for) are the likely screen-share question. The YAML, Alertmanager routing and the verification log are optional for this reader.
