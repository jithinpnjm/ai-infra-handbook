# 1.12 Disks, partitions and filesystems
Words: ~19,600 | Read time: ~131 min | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-01-linux/12-disks-partitions-and-filesystems.md

One-line purpose: Builds the storage stack from disk types and partitions through filesystems, mounts and fstab, capacity, and the caching and durability model, then applies it to checkpoint storms, data-loader storms and local NVMe on GPU nodes.
Beginner friendly: partly to no. Read 1.3 (inodes, links), 1.6 (permissions) and 1.10 (device files) first. The chapter is long; the mount, fstab, `df` and `ENOSPC` sections are the most useful for a beginner.
Nebius rounds served: R1 (disk full, fstab, mkfs/mount, df/du/df -i, iostat, lsblk), R2 (storage problems on GPU nodes, checkpoint and data-loader I/O), R3 (storage capacity estimation, shared vs local tiers), R4 (storage design story for the project)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS. Read in full: section 1 (stack), 2 (disk types table and "How storage is attached"), 4 (filesystem types and the `mkfs` options), 5 (mount options, UUIDs, fstab, `df`/`du`, "disk is full" table), 8 (file descriptors), 9 (write-back and durability, direct I/O, metadata), 11 (methodology and `iostat` `%util` trap), 12 (AI patterns) and "How it fails". Section 3 only for the 1 MiB alignment rule and the worked GPT example. Skim sections 6, 7 and 10. Skip the rotational-mechanics history, the interface table, the filesystem family history, the special-filesystem details and the quota command list (know that quotas exist).

Hands-on to do:
- Loop-device disk: `sudo dd if=/dev/zero of=/tmp/disk1.img bs=1M count=512`, `losetup --find --show -P`, then `parted -s $LOOP mklabel gpt mkpart data 1MiB 300MiB`, `partprobe`, `parted align-check optimal 1`.
- `sudo mkfs.ext4 -L lab-ext4 ${LOOP}p1`, `sudo mkfs.xfs -L lab-xfs ${LOOP}p2`, `findmnt -o TARGET,SOURCE,FSTYPE,OPTIONS`, then `df -hT` and `df -i` for both. Compare the two.
- Deleted-but-open: `dd` a 100 MB file, `tail -f` it, `rm`, then `df` (space not freed), `sudo lsof +L1`, kill the `tail`, then `df` again.
- fstab safety: add a `LABEL=lab-xfs ... xfs defaults,noatime,nofail 0 2` line, `umount`, `findmnt --verify`, `mount -a`, then remove the line again.
- Cold vs warm and direct: `echo 3 | sudo tee /proc/sys/vm/drop_caches` (lab only), time `cat` twice, then run `fio --direct=1 --ioengine=io_uring` and `iostat -xz 1 3` and `cat /proc/pressure/io`.
