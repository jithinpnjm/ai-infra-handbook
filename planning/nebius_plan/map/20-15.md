# 20.15 Design: an observability pipeline for very high volume
Words: 10380 | Read time: 69 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-20-nebius-sre-interview/15-design-an-observability-pipeline-for-very-high-volume.md
One-line purpose: Designs the telemetry pipeline (metrics, logs, traces, billing ledger) for an inference platform, with cardinality budgets, Prometheus sharding and remote write, recording rules, log and trace sampling, exemplars, drop order and monitoring the monitoring, backed by a cardinality tool, promtool-tested rules and a collector config.
Beginner friendly: partly. The ideas are explained from first principles, but Prometheus series, labels, scrape, PromQL `rate` and recording rule syntax are assumed. Read Part 17 chapters 01 to 03 (observability foundations, Prometheus and Grafana, Loki and OpenTelemetry) first. Read 20.19 before section 10 so SLO thresholds make sense.
Nebius rounds served: R3 (primary: design a telemetry or monitoring pipeline), R2 (Prometheus, Grafana, alerting and Kubernetes troubleshooting), R4 (project deep dive, "a metric that grew out of control" story)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10 intro and table, 12, 13, 14, "How to answer out loud", Question 1 to Question 5, Scenarios 1 and 3). Cardinality, metrics versus logs versus traces, and the metering split are the most-asked parts. Skip the long YAML bodies (sections 8, 10 and 14) on first pass and return to them only for the lab.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- "Design the telemetry pipeline for hundreds of GPU replicas and thousands of tenants. Where do you start?" (Expect: questions first, signals per layer, cardinality budget, pull versus push, sharding, separate billing ledger.)
- "A developer added a request-id label and Prometheus ran out of memory. What do you do now, and how do you stop it happening again?" (Expect: TSDB status to find the series, relabel or drop the label, then fix code; linter at review, scrape limits, churn alert.)
- "Head sampling or tail sampling for traces? What does tail sampling cost?" (Expect: head is cheap and blind, tail keeps errors and slow traces, buffering with decision wait times trace rate, trace-id routing to one collector, memory arithmetic with stated inputs.)
- "The dashboard shows 10.0M tokens and the invoice shows 10.2M. Which is right, and what do you tell the customer?" (Expect: dashboard is sampled and approximate, invoice comes from an exactly-once ledger, reconcile and keep the report.)
- "Your long-term store was down for three hours. Is the data gone?" (Expect: the 2-hour remote write rule, local retention still has it, backfill from local blocks, and the alert that should fire earlier.)
- "How would you know your monitoring has gone silent?" (Expect: watchdog that pages on silence, alerts on collector refusals and queues, a second Prometheus, and a synthetic probe through the whole path.)
