One-line purpose: Covers security for a Linux and GPU-cluster network: threat vocabulary, defense in depth, firewalls, SSH hardening, scanning, control-plane and Layer-2 protection, zero trust with Kubernetes NetworkPolicy, and AI-cluster specifics.
Beginner friendly: partly. Needs 2.6 (TCP handshake), 2.10 (TLS and PKI), 2.9 (NAT) and basic Linux users and permissions first. Sections 7 (SSH) and 8 (scanning) are hands-on and approachable; sections 10-11 (BGP and switch security) and 13 (InfiniBand P_Key) are dense.
Nebius rounds served: R1 (SSH, firewalls, scanning, practical Linux troubleshooting), R2 (NetworkPolicy, secrets, incident response in Kubernetes), R3 (threat model and tenant isolation in a system design)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12, 13 intro, 14, 16, 17). SSH and NetworkPolicy are the most testable practical items. BGP, Layer-2, and InfiniBand subsections are SKIP-for-recall unless the interviewer goes into network-device security.

Hands-on to do:
- Exposure audit on a disposable VM: `ss -tulpn`, then `nmap -p- -sV localhost`, then scan from a second host. Compare the two lists (section 8, lab step 1).
- SSH hardening: set keys-only login, `AllowGroups`, and `PermitRootLogin no`. Run `sudo sshd -t` while a second session stays open. Confirm a password login fails, and read the negotiation with `ssh -vvv` (lab step 3).
- Host key mismatch: regenerate a test server's host keys, read the warning on the client, then verify the fingerprint with `ssh-keygen -lf` and clear the entry with `ssh-keygen -R host` (lab step 5).
- Bastion setup: `ssh -J bastion user@gpu01`, plus a `~/.ssh/config` `Host gpu-*` block with `ProxyJump` and `ControlPersist` (section 7.4).
- NetworkPolicy: in a kind cluster with Calico or Cilium, apply default-deny and allow-gateway-to-triton, then test with `kubectl exec ... curl` (lab step 9).
- Firewall: a default-deny nftables ruleset that allows SSH only from the management subnet, and check it with nmap from inside and outside (lab step 2, uses chapter 2.23).
