Hands-on to do:
- `uname -r; lsmod | grep -E '^(nvidia|nouveau)'; modinfo nvidia | grep -E 'version|vermagic|signer'; cat /proc/cmdline` (state check on a GPU or any module-capable VM).
- `sudo modprobe -c | grep -i nvidia; systemd-analyze cat-config modprobe.d` (see the merged modprobe configuration and where each line came from).
- `cat /sys/module/nvidia/refcnt; ls /sys/module/nvidia/holders; sudo lsof /dev/nvidia*` (find why unload fails) - do not unload on a live node.
- Caution: 1.22 says `kernel.yama.ptrace_scope` should be 0 where MPI needs it, while 1.21 says keep it at 1 and verify MPI. Know both positions and the trade-off.
- Sysctl: `sudo sysctl -w vm.max_map_count=262144` then `echo 'vm.max_map_count = 262144' | sudo tee /etc/sysctl.d/90-gpu.conf; sudo sysctl --system`, and show the difference between runtime-only and persistent.
