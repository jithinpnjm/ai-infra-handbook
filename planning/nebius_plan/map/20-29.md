## Plan-versus-14-day conflicts (for this reader, 25 Nov to 8 Dec, interview 9 Dec)

Week and day structure of this chapter:
- **8-week plan** (section 2): Weeks 1-2 Linux console (20.1-20.7); week 3 coding (20.8-20.11); weeks 4-5 system design and SLOs (20.12-20.16, 20.19, 20.25); week 6 inference SRE (20.18, 20.20-20.22); week 7 stress (20.23, 20.24, 20.26); week 8 final round (20.27, 20.28, light pass of 20.30-20.33, plus 20.29). The chapter's ratio is 40% reading and labs, 30% drills, 30% speaking.
- **3-week plan** (section 2): week 1 Linux and coding; week 2 design and inference SRE; week 3 stress and final.
- **10-day plan** (section 2): days 1-2 Linux (20.3, 20.4, 20.5); days 3-4 coding (20.8, 20.9); days 5-6 design (20.12, 20.13, 20.19); days 7-8 stress (20.18, 20.21, 20.26); days 9-10 final week (20.28, 20.29).
- **Generator output for your dates**: 10-day template, 4 spare days 25-28 Nov, blocks 29-30 Nov, 1-2 Dec, 3-4 Dec, 5-6 Dec, 7-8 Dec (final week, 1.5 h), 9 Dec interview.
- **Daily habits**: 20 minutes of flashcards every day; one timed coding problem every second day from week 3.
- **Mock protocol**: one mock per round type from week 3, then one a week in the last three; stress mock at week 7 at the latest.
- **Final-week rules**: days out 7-4 two full mocks on weak rounds and no new topic; days out 3-2 one light mock and read the cheat sheet; day out 1 cards in box 1 only, no full mock; morning, no learning.

What in it conflicts with the 14-day plan in STUDY_PLAN.md:
1. **Length and fit.** The chapter's 8-week plan (56 days) and 3-week plan cannot fit in 14 days. Only the 10-day template fits, and the generator selects it automatically. Its 2-day blocks give about 3 hours each at 10 hours per week, which is far less than STUDY_PLAN's 4 to 5 hours a day.
2. **Hours.** The chapter's default is 10 hours a week and the split is 40/30/30. STUDY_PLAN assumes 4 hours reading plus 1 hour practice every day (about 35 hours a week). The chapter does not give a 14-day schedule at that rate.
3. **No Kubernetes or networking refresh in the 10-day template.** The template has Linux, coding, design, stress and final only. Kubernetes refresh (5.3, 5.8, 5.9, 5.10, 5.11, 5.18, 5.25) and networking depth (2.1, 2.6, 2.8, 2.22, 2.30) are scheduled in STUDY_PLAN days 3-8 but absent here. The chapter's 8-week plan puts Kubernetes only in week 7 (20.23 failure drills) and Terraform (20.24), which is cut first under time pressure. For a reader who needs a Kubernetes refresh, this is a real gap.
4. **Python refresh.** The chapter covers coding practice (20.8 to 20.11) but no Python refresh chapter. STUDY_PLAN day 5 schedules 3.6 (Python) and 20.8 to 20.10.
5. **Coding volume.** The chapter asks for one timed problem every second day from week 3 and a 20-minute daily slice. STUDY_PLAN day 5 onwards asks for 4 to 5 problems a day from 20.11 (40 problems). That is more than the chapter asks for; it is a choice you make, not a chapter rule.
6. **Order.** The chapter runs Linux, coding, design, inference, stress, final. STUDY_PLAN runs Linux, Python, Kubernetes, observability, autoscaling, design, incidents, final. The STUDY_PLAN order front-loads Kubernetes, which the chapter does not do.
7. **Mocks start too late in STUDY_PLAN.** The chapter asks for one mock per round type from week 3 (roughly day 15 of an 8-week plan, earlier on a compressed plan) and a stress mock by week 7. STUDY_PLAN has one mock, on day 14. For 14 days, the chapter's rubric bar (8 or more on two mocks in a row) needs mocks from about day 4 onwards.
8. **New reading in the last two days.** The chapter says no new topic in the last week, no long chapter in days out 3 to 2, and no new material on the day before. STUDY_PLAN day 13 (7 Dec, two days out) reads 20.26 to 20.28 and 20.30, and day 14 (8 Dec, the day before) reads 20.32 and 20.36 and includes a mock. Those conflict with the chapter's rules for days out 3 to 2 and day out 1.
9. **Flashcards missing.** STUDY_PLAN has no daily 20-minute flashcard review. The chapter's Leitner deck (about 110 priority questions first) is absent from STUDY_PLAN.
10. **Interview date.** The generator computes 14 days from 25 Nov to 9 Dec, which is the 10-day template with 4 spare days. STUDY_PLAN's day 1 is 25 Nov, so under the chapter's rule the first 4 days (25-28 Nov) are spare days for setup and weak chapters, and the 10-day blocks start on 29 Nov.

Suggested reconciliation (my reading, not the chapter's): use the 10-day template's stage order for the two-day blocks, keep the STUDY_PLAN Kubernetes and networking refresh inside the Linux and design blocks, run one mock per round by 3 Dec, keep 20 minutes of flashcards daily, and move 20.32 and 20.36 out of the last two days.
