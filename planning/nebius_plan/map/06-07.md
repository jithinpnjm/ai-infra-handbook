One-line purpose: Gives the Slurm daemon model (controller, node agent, step daemon, accounting, munge), the life of one job, the HA and state model, and the install order and failure patterns that let you reason about a Slurm failure you have never seen.

Beginner friendly: partly. It is intermediate. Read 6.1 section 5 and 6.2 section 6 first, and have Part 1 (users, systemd) and Part 2 (ports, DNS) in your head. Sections 4, 7 and 8's install tables are heavy for a first pass.

Nebius rounds served: R2 (Slurm-on-Kubernetes troubleshooting and the daemon model), R3 (control-plane failover, split-brain, state persistence), R1 (install and verify commands, munge and time sync, log reading), R4 (upgrade order and team-lead ownership talk).

Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (1 workload manager ideas, 2 daemons, 3 life of a job, 5 state and HA, 6 munge, 8 upgrade order, 9 account tree intro, 11 install order and verification, 12 failure modes 1-2, 13 log patterns) plus SKIM-FOR-RECALL for 4, 7, 10 and the rest of 12; SKIP the long checkpoint and history. Reason: the interview tests the daemon model, the job path, what survives a controller failure, and munge/time failures, and these sections carry all of that.

Hands-on to do:
- Lab A (single VM, Ubuntu 24.04 per the chapter): `sudo apt-get install -y munge mariadb-server slurm-wlm slurmdbd`, then `sudo /usr/sbin/mungekey --create --force` and `munge -n | unmunge`.
- Start two fake nodes on one machine (`sudo slurmd -N vn1`, `sudo slurmd -N vn2`), then run `sinfo`, `srun -N2 hostname`, `sbatch --wrap="sleep 20"` and watch with `squeue`.
- Build the account tree with `sacctmgr add cluster`, `add account`, `add user`, then `sacctmgr show tree`. Turn on `AccountingStorageEnforce=associations` and submit as a user with and without an association; read the rejection.
- Diagnose on purpose (Lab C): stop munged on one node, or change the clock 10 minutes, then read `scontrol show node` and `sinfo -R` for the Reason and write down the log line.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- Walk me through what happens from `sbatch` to the accounting record. Where does munge act, and what is off the data path?
- The controller dies in the middle of a training run. Which jobs keep running, what stops, and what state do you need to recover?
- What does split-brain mean for slurmctld HA, and how do you prevent it or recover from it?
- A node registers and then goes `invalid` or drains with "Low RealMemory". What is the cause and the fix?
- What order do you upgrade Slurm in, and what error do you see if you get it wrong?
- Users get "Invalid account" after you turned on accounting enforcement. What do you check?
