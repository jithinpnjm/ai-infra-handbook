# 20.14 Design: dedicated endpoints and safe model rollout
Words: 9190 | Read time: 61 | File: /Users/jithinpjoseph/Documents/GitHub/ai-infra-handbook/docs/part-20-nebius-sre-interview/14-design-dedicated-endpoints-and-safe-model-rollout.md
One-line purpose: Designs a dedicated (single-customer) inference endpoint and a safe rollout of a custom checkpoint, covering control vs data plane, GPU sizing, cold start, probes, rolling vs blue/green vs canary when GPUs are scarce, quality gates, rollback, version skew and scale-to-zero.
Beginner friendly: partly. The ideas are explained from first principles, but the Deployment YAML, rolling-update fields (maxSurge, maxUnavailable), probe types and pod lifecycle assume Kubernetes basics. Read Part 5 (Kubernetes: Deployments, probes, rolling updates) first, then Part 15 chapter 10 (prefill and decode) and 20.13 for the control and data plane split.
Nebius rounds served: R3 (primary: design with rollout and rollback), R2 (Kubernetes Deployment, probes, rolling update, ProgressDeadlineExceeded), R4 (follow-ups on how your own project shipped model or config changes)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, "How to answer out loud", Scenarios A and B). These are the rollout and probe reasoning an SRE will be pushed on. Sections 4, 10 and 11 are worth a skim for recall; the Python code is optional beyond `decide` and `max_gpus`.

Likely interview questions from this chapter:
- "Design a dedicated endpoint for a customer's fine-tuned model. What does the control plane own and what does the data plane do?" (Expect: desired-state spec, lifecycle actions, regional data plane that keeps serving if the control plane is down.)
- "We have one spare GPU and 8 replicas. How do you roll out a new engine image?" (Expect: why blue/green needs 2x, surge 1 with unavailable 0, canary gate, ProgressDeadlineExceeded alert if the spare is gone.)
- "Your canary's latency is green but users report cut-off answers. What gates did you miss, and what do you do?" (Expect: quality eval and finish_reason length share; roll back to the previous version id.)
- "How should liveness, readiness and startup probes differ for a vLLM-style engine?" (Expect: startup covers load and compile, liveness cheap and tolerant, readiness means a real one-token request.)
- "Should this endpoint scale to zero?" (Expect: cold request waits the full cold start; SLA implies minimum one; zero only for dev or batch.)
- "A customer wants a 70B FP16 checkpoint live within the hour. What is your time estimate and what do you tell them?" (Expect: 140 GB of weights, stage-by-stage time, pre-staging, measure the first replica before promising the rest.)
