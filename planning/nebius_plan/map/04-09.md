# 4.9 A real playbook end to end: deploying an application
Words: ~10,300 | Read time: 69 min | File: docs/part-04-automation/09-a-real-playbook-end-to-end-deploying-an-application.md
One-line purpose: Puts the earlier pieces together into one multi-tier deployment (OS baseline, PostgreSQL, a FastAPI app behind nginx with TLS, verification) and shows the release-directory pattern for zero-downtime rollout and rollback.
Beginner friendly: partly. Read it after 4.3 to 4.8, which the chapter says are prerequisites. The first two sections are conceptual and easy to follow; the playbooks are long and assume the handler, `serial` and `block` patterns from 4.6 and 4.8.
Nebius rounds served: R4 (primary: a deploy with rollback, verification and secret handling, which is the deep-dive story), R2 (playbook structure, zero-downtime rollout, nginx and systemd troubleshooting), R3 (tier separation and the load-balanced app layout as a small system-design example)
Chapter verdict: READ-SECTIONS (1 "Target architecture", 3 "Variables", 5 "The application play: release directories and an atomic switch" incl. its "Four release habits" and the two migration and artefact paragraphs, 6 "The web tier: nginx, TLS and rate limiting" (the upstream block and validate trap only), 7 "Verification play", 8 "Idempotency and safety checks", 10 "Troubleshooting a failed deploy", 12, 13; checkpoint answers 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 12, 14, 16, 18, 19, 20). Skim section 1's Mezzanine history and section 2 (layout) for recall only; skip the container variant (section 9) except the digest-pinning point; skip checkpoints 1, 4, 6, 10, 11, 13, 15, 17 unless time allows.

Hands-on to do:
- `python3 code/part04/ansible_app_deployment_lab.py` from the handbook root (the folder exists there). It needs no servers.
- Build the layout in section 2 against 2 or 3 Ubuntu VMs. Run `ansible-playbook playbooks/site.yml --syntax-check`, `ansible-lint playbooks/`, then the real run, then a second run that should show `changed=0` apart from intentional tasks.
- Canary and rollback: `ansible-playbook playbooks/app.yml -e app_version=<new> --limit app01`, then `-e app_version=<old>` and time the rollback.
- Break the readiness check on purpose (stop the app or point `/readyz` at a bad path). Confirm the play fails before `post_tasks` puts the host back in rotation.
- Write a bad nginx site file and watch the `validate:` trap. Then compare with `nginx -t` on the full config, and with `notify: Reload nginx`.
