# 5.7 Pods and workloads: Deployments, StatefulSets, DaemonSets and Jobs
Words: 23,600 | Read time: 157 | File: docs/part-05-kubernetes/07-pods-and-workloads-deployments-statefulsets-daemonsets-and-jobs.md
One-line purpose: The pod spec as a set of decisions (containers, init and sidecars, resources and QoS, volumes, security, lifecycle, scheduling), plus choosing the right workload controller and debugging pods that will not start or keep failing.
Beginner friendly: partly. Read 5.1 (containers and images) and 5.3 and 5.6 first, as the chapter says; "namespace" and "cgroup" must be clear before section 2 (QoS, OOM). Sections 1 and 3 are readable after 5.3.

Must read: section 1 (pod), containers and init containers, resources and QoS, lifecycle and termination, workload controller choice and the inference Deployment, Job patterns, section 4 (operating pods, phases and the symptom table), section 5 (GPU sizing), common mistakes and interview angles. Hands-on to do:
- Pod and QoS: `kubectl run kuard --image=gcr.io/kuar-demo/kuard-amd64:blue`, then `kubectl get pod kuard -o jsonpath='{.status.qosClass}{"\n"}'`, then `kubectl describe pod kuard`. Repeat with a Guaranteed and a BestEffort pod and compare.
- Failure reading: apply the `oneshot` Job from the chapter with `--keygen-exit-code=1`, then `kubectl get pod -l job-name=oneshot`, `kubectl logs <pod> --previous`, and `kubectl describe pod <pod>`. Watch the CrashLoopBackOff restarts.
- Rollout: `kubectl rollout history deployment kuard`, `kubectl rollout undo deployments kuard --to-revision=<n>`, then `kubectl get replicasets -o wide` to see the replica counts move.
- Debug without shell: `kubectl debug -it trainer-0 --image=nicolaka/netshoot --target=trainer` (on a distroless pod), and `kubectl debug node/<node> -it --image=ubuntu` then `chroot /host`.
- Offline: `python3 code/part05/pod_qos_workload_validator_lab.py` (no cluster needed; checks QoS class, oom_score_adj and init-container effective request).
