Hands-on to do:
- `ssh-keygen -t ed25519 -C "..."`, then `ssh-copy-id -i ~/.ssh/id_ed25519.pub user@host`, then `ssh -G host | head -20`.
- Break and fix: `chmod 777 ~/.ssh` on the server, try `ssh -vvv user@host`, read the auth log, then restore 700 and 600.
- Tunnel: `ssh -N -L 8000:localhost:8000 host`, then open `http://localhost:8000`, and check with `ss -ltnp`.
- rsync trailing slash: `rsync -avhn src dest/` versus `rsync -avhn src/ dest/`, and compare the listed paths.
- Fan-out: `pdsh -w host[1-2] 'hostname'` or `clush -w host[1-2] -b 'uptime'`, and watch how identical output is grouped.
