## 2. Outline to follow
(taken from the current draft; keep this order, improve the depth)
1. The PyTorch 2.x Compiler Stack (`torch.compile`) | topics: Stage 1 — TorchDynamo: CPython bytecode capture, FX Graphs, guards, and compiler stances; Stage 2 — AOTAutograd: Ahead-of-time joint forward and backward graph fusion; Stage 3 — PrimTorch IR: Decomposing ATen operators into functional primitives; Stage 4 — TorchInductor: Define-by-run loop IR, compilation modes, AOTInductor, TE, and FlexAttention
2. Dynamic Shapes, Graph Breaks, Compiler Logging, and Numerical Determinism | topics: Dynamic shapes: SymPy, `mark_dynamic`, and the bucket-padding rule; Diagnosing and eliminating graph breaks (`torch._dynamo.explain`, `torch.where`, `torch.cond`, and DDP/FSDP); Compiler logging (Table 14-1), `TORCH_COMPILE_DEBUG=1`, `tlparse`, and numerical determinism
3. OpenAI Triton: SPMD Programming, PyTorch Op Registration, Autotuning, TMA Descriptors, and Persistent GEMMs | topics: Triton's SPMD programming model: `vector_add_kernel` and `BLOCK_SIZE` vs `num_warps`; Registering custom Triton kernels with PyTorch (`@triton_op`, `wrap_triton`, and `register_autograd`); Autotuning (`@triton.autotune`), Warp Specialization, TMA Descriptors, and Persistent GEMM (`matmul_kernel_persistent`)
4. Profiling with Triton Proton and the PyTorch XLA (OpenXLA) Backend | topics: Triton Proton profiler (`8,192 × 8,192 × 512` GEMM benchmark) and portable cache hints; PyTorch XLA (OpenXLA) vs. TorchInductor
5. Commands and Configurations That Matter | topics: Inspect `torch.compile` graph breaks, generated Triton/PTX code, `tlparse` traces, and bitwise cuBLAS determinism
6. How It Fails: Six `torch.compile`, Triton, and OpenXLA Failure Modes
7. Hands-On Lab: Verify the PyTorch Compiler Stack, `dynamo.explain`, `TORCH_LOGS`, Triton SPMD & TMA Rules, and Proton Benchmarks
