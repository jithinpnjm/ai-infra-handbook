## Research standard
1. Search widely and deeply, and fetch pages rather than trusting snippets: nebius.com (blog, newsroom, careers, interview pages, product pages, legal), docs.nebius.com, docs.tokenfactory.nebius.com (llms.txt index), github.com/nebius (list repos with https://api.github.com/orgs/nebius/repos?per_page=100 and read READMEs, docs, protobuf, Go and Python source of the repos relevant to your chapter), SEC EDGAR filings of Nebius Group N.V., investor relations materials, conference talks and videos (GTC, KubeCon, Ray Summit, vLLM meetups, SemiAnalysis, MLPerf result pages; fetch transcripts where available), press and trade coverage, engineer-authored posts, Hacker News, Reddit, Habr and other forums for engineering-culture signals. Prefer primary sources. Use WebSearch in extended mode for hard-to-find items.
2. Write your raw notes to planning/research/nebius_<topic>.md: every URL fetched, the date, what it says in your own words (quotes under 15 words), and a reliability tag. Tags: [official] Nebius docs or legal, [vendor] Nebius marketing or blog, [filing] SEC or investor document, [source-code] public repo, [third-party] independent analysis, [report] anecdote, [inference] your reasoning. Record what you looked for and did NOT find.
3. Then write the chapter per planning/VOLUME_20_PROMPT.md (structure, truth rules, voice, drill questions in the form `<details><summary>1. Q?</summary>` newline answer newline `</details>` with a blank line after; chain gate 1.5 or lower; 7,000 words of prose at most; prefix code files c<NN>_ in code/part20/ with pytest; do not run sync_code_blocks.py, validate_chapter.py, git or npm). Adapt the 'What the interviewer is testing' section to 'Why an SRE here should know this' and keep 'How to answer out loud' for the questions an interviewer could ask about it ('what do you know about our platform', 'how would you design X on our infrastructure'). Never claim anything about Nebius internals that the sources do not state; separate [official] facts from [vendor] claims from [inference] design reasoning; where Nebius has not published something, say so and give a labelled reasonable design.
4. Marketing numbers (throughput, cost reductions, uptime) are quoted only with the tag and the workload they came from. Financial and company facts come from filings only, with dates; give no investment opinion and no advice about securities.
5. Add a 'Questions to ask the interviewer' section grounded in what you learned (five to eight questions) and a 'What I could not find' list. Add a Verification log at the end of Sources (sources fetched, claims checked, confirmed, corrected, removed, still unverified).
6. Ground every chapter in the SRE job: reliability, failure modes, observability, operations. Connect each Nebius fact to what it means for operating it (what breaks, what you would monitor, what you would ask).
