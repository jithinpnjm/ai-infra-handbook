| **1. KV Cache Mechanics, Fragmentation & PagedAttention Block Table** | **Prefill vs Decode complexity (`DAI` Ch. 6):** Prefill processes $L_2$ prompt tokens in parallel with self-attention complexity $O(L_2^2 \cdot D)$ (compute-bound GEMM). Decode generates $1$ token per step ($B \times 1 \times D$): without KV cache, $O(L_{\text{total}}^2 \cdot D)$ per step and $O(T^3 \cdot D)$ over $T$ tokens; with KV cache, $O(L_{\text{total}} \cdot D)$ per step and $O(T^2 \cdot D)$ total (memory-bandwidth bound GEMV). **KV Cache per-token formula (`V12` Ch. 8):** $\text{Bytes/token} = 2 \times L \times H_{kv} \times D_{\text{head}} \times P$. For **Llama-3-70B** ($L=80$, $H_q=64$, $H_{kv}=8$ GQA $8:1$, $D_{\text{head}}=128$, $P=2$ bytes BF16): $2 \times 80 \times 8 \times 128 \times 2 = 327,680\text{ B} = 320\text{ KiB/token}$ ($8\times$ smaller than MHA $H_{kv}=64$ at $2,621,440\text{ B} = 2.56\text{ MiB/token}$, an $87.5\%$ reduction). At $S=4,096$: $1.25\text{ GiB}$/seq; at $S=128,000$: $39.06\text{ GiB}$/seq (vs $312.5\text{ GiB}$ MHA). **Fragmentation (`V12` Ch. 6 & 8, `DAI` Ch. 6):** Contiguous allocation reserves $S_{\max}$ ($4,096$ or $32,768$), wasting **$60\%$ to $80\%$** of GPU VRAM across internal fragmentation, external fragmentation, and reservation waste. **PagedAttention:** Partitions KV cache into fixed physical blocks of size $B_{\text{size}} = 16$ (or $32$) tokens. Blocks needed for sequence $i$: $N_i = \lceil (L_2^{(i)} + t^{(i)}) / B_{\text{size}} \rceil$. External fragmentation is **$0\%$**; internal fragmentation is strictly bounded to the final block: $< B_{\text{size}} / S_{\text{actual}}$ (`< 4%` overall; for $S_{\text{actual}}=500, B=16$, max wasted tokens $= 15$, internal fragmentation $= 15/500 = 3.0\% < 3.2\%$, vs $(4096-500)/4096 = 87.79\% > 87\%$ contiguous waste). Enables **$2\times\text{–}4\times$** more concurrent requests. **Zero Padding FLOPs (`DAI` Ch. 6 Fig. 6.9):** Batching $3$ requests of lengths $[4, 7, 3]$ with contiguous padding computes $7 \times 3 = 21$ positions ($14$ real tokens, $7$ pad tokens = **$33.33\%$ (`33%`) wasted attention FLOPs**); PagedAttention iterates only over allocated blocks ($14$ tokens, **$0\%$ padding FLOPs**). | `DAI` Ch. 6, `V12` Ch. 6 & Ch. 8 |
| **2. Copy-on-Write (CoW), Automatic Prefix Caching (APC) & RadixAttention** | **Copy-on-Write (`V12` Ch. 6):** For parallel sampling (`n > 1`) and beam search (`"use_beam_search": true, "n": 4`), child branches share parent physical blocks via reference counting ($\text{ref\_count} \leftarrow \text{ref\_count} + 1$). A physical block is copied only when a branch writes a new token into a shared incomplete block, saving shared prompt KV memory. **vLLM APC vs SGLang RadixAttention (`V12` Ch. 6 & 8, `HLS` Ch. 6 & 8, `ASP` Ch. 18):** vLLM APC hashes full physical blocks (e.g., SHA-256 over token IDs at discrete $16$-token block boundaries) in a global hash table with LRU block eviction (`get_computed_blocks()`), cutting shared-prefix TTFT by up to **$80\%\text{–}90\%$**. SGLang RadixAttention maintains a compressed prefix tree (Radix Tree) matching arbitrary token sub-sequences with leaf-first LRU eviction. **RAG vs Long-Context CAG (`HLS` Ch. 7):** RAG ($500$ cached system + $10 \times 500 = 5,000$ RAG + $500$ user = $5,500$ uncached prefill tokens, `$0.007`/req at `$1.25/1M` regular and `$0.125/1M` cached) vs CAG ($100,500$ cached + $500$ uncached user = $11\times$ fewer prefill tokens, TTFT drops $10\times$ from $5.0\text{ s}$ to $0.5\text{ s}$ at `$0.013`/req). **Multi-tenant prefix isolation (`HLS` Ch. 6):** Inject `<id> {user_id}` after `<system>` before `<context>` to prevent cross-tenant timing side-channel enumeration while still sharing `<system>`. | `V12` Ch. 6 & Ch. 8, `HLS` Ch. 6, 7 & 8, `ASP` Ch. 18 |
| **3. Continuous Batching, Chunked Prefill & Preemption (Swap vs Recompute)** | **Continuous Batching (`V12` Ch. 7, `DAI` Ch. 6, `HLS` Ch. 8):** Orca iteration-level scheduling evicts sequences emitting `<EOS>` at step $t$ and slots waiting requests at step $t+1$ (`0%` padding waste vs `40%–65%` dynamic and `>70%` static; reduced p99 ITL from `420 ms` to `19.1 ms` with `3.8x` throughput gain). **Chunked Prefill (`V12` Ch. 7 & 8, `DAI` Ch. 6, `HLS` Ch. 8):** Slices long prompts ($N=8,192$ or $12,288$) into chunks of size $C$ ($512$ or $1,024$, controlled by `long_prefill_token_threshold` and `--max-num-batched-tokens 2048`), enforcing $\sum 1_{\text{decode}} + \sum C_j \le \text{MaxTokensPerIter}$. Prevents a $12,288$-token unchunked prefill (`320 ms` GEMM) from spiking p99 ITL to `340 ms` (or `380 ms`), bounding p99 ITL to `21.4 ms` (`< 25 ms`). Enabled by default in vLLM V1. **Preemption Swap vs Recompute (`V12` Ch. 6, 7, 8):** Round-trip PCIe swap time $t_{\text{swap}} = 2 \times \text{KV\_Bytes} / B_{\text{PCIe}}$ (moving $4\text{ GB}$ at $64\text{ GB/s}$ takes $62.5\text{ ms}$ one-way / $125\text{ ms}$ round-trip; moving $28\text{ GB}$ across $12$ requests stalls GPUs for $4\text{–}8\text{ s}$ and degrades ITL $4,000\%$ from $20\text{ ms}$ to $850\text{ ms}$). Prefill recompute time $t_{\text{recompute}} = (2 L S^2 H + 12 L S H^2) / T_{\text{GPU}}$ ($989\text{ TFLOPS}$ on H100). Recompute is faster ($t_{\text{recompute}} < t_{\text{swap}}$) for $S < 8,192$ tokens; production latency-critical deployments set `--swap-space 0` and `preemption_mode="recompute"`. | `V12` Ch. 6, 7 & 8, `DAI` Ch. 6, `HLS` Ch. 8 |
