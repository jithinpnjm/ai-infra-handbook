## Checklist of Concepts, Numbers, and Patterns to Cover
1. **The `kube-scheduler` Scheduling Framework & Extension Points (`volume-03/01-k8s-control-plane-scheduling-masterclass.md` §5):**
   - Two-phase cycle: **Scheduling Cycle** (`QueueSort -> PreFilter -> Filter -> PostFilter (preemption) -> PreScore -> Score -> Reserve -> Permit`) and **Binding Cycle** (`WaitOnPermit -> PreBind -> Bind -> PostBind`).
   - How the **Coscheduling plugin (`PodGroup` CRD, `scheduling.x-k8s.io/v1alpha1`)** hooks into `PreFilter`, `Permit` (`Wait` with `scheduleTimeoutSeconds: 300`), and `Reserve` (`Unreserve` all peers on timeout) to implement all-or-nothing gang scheduling inside the default `kube-scheduler` (`minMember: 4`).
   - `PriorityClass` preemption (`critical-inference`, `value: 1000000` in `volume-03`) vs Kueue `WorkloadPriorityClass` (`production-priority`, `value: 10000` in `GAK` Example 7-9).
2. **Kueue Architecture in Depth (`GAK` Ch. 7 Examples 7-4, 7-6, 7-7, 7-8, 7-9; `volume-03` §7.1; `SKI` Ch. 2):**
   - Why Kueue is an **admission controller and queue manager** (sits *above* the scheduler, holding `spec.suspend: true` on `Job`, `PyTorchJob`, `TrainJob`, `RayJob`, `JobSet`, or `MPIJob` and creating a lightweight `Workload` object until quota is reserved: `type: Admitted, status: "True", reason: "QuotaReserved"` vs `"InsufficientQuota"`).
   - Four core CRDs and personas:
     1. **`ResourceFlavor` (`kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta1`):** Maps hardware tiers via `nodeLabels` and `tolerations` (e.g., `gpu-nvlink-infiniband` with `gpu-interconnect: nvlink`, `network-fabric: infiniband`, `gpu-type: nvidia-h100` vs `gpu-standard-ethernet` with `gpu-interconnect: pcie`, `network-fabric: ethernet`, `gpu-type: nvidia-h100`).
     2. **`ClusterQueue`:** Defines cluster-wide resource pools (`gpu-training` with `32` H100 GPUs, `gpu-development` with `8` GPUs, `gpu-spot`, or `cluster-queue-h100` with `1000` CPUs, `4000Gi` memory, `128` H100 GPUs; or `topology-aware-cluster-queue` with `16` `gpu-nvlink-infiniband` + `32` `gpu-standard-ethernet` GPUs).
     3. **`LocalQueue`:** Namespace-scoped queue (`team-queue` or `team-a-training-queue`) bound by data scientists via label `kueue.x-k8s.io/queue-name`.
     4. **`Workload` & `WorkloadPriorityClass`:** Tracks `podSetAssignments` (`head: 1`, `worker: 1`) and priority (`production-priority: 10000`).
   - **Cohorts, Borrowing, Flavor Fungibility, and Preemption (`GAK` Example 7-7 & 7-9):**
     - `cohort: shared-gpu-cohort`: Groups multiple `ClusterQueue`s (`Team A`, `Team B`, `Team C`). If `team-a-queue` has `nominalQuota: 10` and `borrowingLimit: 22`, Team A can burst up to `10 + 22 = 32` GPUs when cohort peers are idle.
     - `flavorFungibility`: `whenCanBorrow: TryNextFlavor` vs `MayStopSearch` (default), and `whenCanPreempt: Preempt`.
     - `preemption`: `reclaimWithinCohort: Any` (reclaims borrowed GPUs immediately when the lending team submits work within its nominal quota) and `withinClusterQueue: LowerPriority` (high-priority job preempts lower-priority job in the same queue), plus `queueingStrategy: BestEffortFIFO` vs `StrictFIFO`.
3. **Volcano Architecture in Depth (`GAK` Ch. 7, `volume-06` §2.2, `SKI` Ch. 2):**
   - Monolithic HPC/batch scheduler replacement (`schedulerName: volcano`) with `Job` (`batch.volcano.sh/v1alpha1`), `PodGroup` (`minAvailable: 8`), and `Queue` CRDs.
   - Lifecycle policies (`event: PodEvicted -> action: RestartJob`), plugins (`gang`, `proportion` weighted queue sharing, `drf` Dominant Resource Fairness, `binpack`, `predicates`, `nodeorder`, and topology scoring), and trade-offs vs Kueue (`SKI` Ch. 2: Volcano is monolithic and replaces `kube-scheduler`, whereas Kueue is SIG-Scheduling native and delegates pod binding).
4. **Layering Kueue + Specialized Schedulers & APF Control-Plane Protection (`GAK` Ch. 7, `volume-03` §10):**
   - Combining Kueue (admission control + `ResourceFlavor` quota/borrowing) with Coscheduling `PodGroup`, Volcano, or NVIDIA KAI Scheduler (atomic gang placement + intra-rack/NVSwitch topology).
   - Protecting `kube-apiserver` with `PriorityLevelConfiguration` (`ai-workload-level`, `nominalConcurrencyShares: 50`, `queues: 100`, `handSize: 5`, `queueLengthLimit: 200`) and monitoring `etcd_disk_wal_fsync_duration_seconds` and `scheduler_scheduling_algorithm_duration_seconds`.
