| **2. Goodput-Adjusted Cost per 1M Tokens under P99 TTFT and ITL/TPOT SLOs** | **Goodput Definition (`AIE` Ch. 9, `ASP` Ch. 1, Ch. 17):** Goodput is the throughput of requests (or tokens) per second that simultaneously satisfy both latency Service Level Objectives: **`TTFT <= SLO_TTFT`** (e.g., `P95/P99 < 200 ms` interactive chat, `450 ms` MLPerf v5.0 `Llama-2-70B`, `< 500 ms` `V21` SLA) and **`ITL / TPOT <= SLO_ITL`** (e.g., `P95/P99 < 25–40 ms/tok`, faster than human reading speed `120 ms/tok` / `6–8 tok/s`). If raw completed request rate is `R_raw` and only fraction `g_slo in (0, 1]` meets both latency SLOs, **`Goodput_RPS = g_slo * R_raw`** and **`Goodput_Cost_per_1M = Raw_Cost_per_1M / g_slo`**. **Concrete Example (`AIE` Ch. 9 Fig. 9-4):** A service completes `100 req/min` (`10 RPS`) under heavy batching, but only `30 req/min` (`3 RPS`, `g_slo = 0.30`) meet `TTFT <= 200 ms` and `TPOT <= 100 ms`; goodput is `30 RPM` / `3 RPS`, and effective cost per SLO-compliant token is **`3.33x` higher** than raw throughput suggests. **Open-Loop vs Closed-Loop Knee of the Curve (`V12` Ch. 10):** Closed-loop load testing caps queue depth at client concurrency `C` (`120 tok/s`), hiding admission queue blowup; open-loop Poisson arrivals (`lambda` RPS) expose the saturation knee where `vllm:num_requests_waiting` jumps from `2` to `340`, `gpu_cache_usage_perc` hits `0.99`, preemptions (`vllm:num_preempted_requests_total`) trigger recomputation loops, and `P99 TTFT` explodes from `180 ms` to `18,200 ms`.
