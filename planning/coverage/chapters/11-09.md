## Checklist of Concepts, Numbers, and Patterns to Cover
1. **Why raw Kubernetes `Job` and `StatefulSet` are not enough for multi-node AI:**
   - Distributed AI requires coordinated multi-role topologies (`Master`/`Worker`, `Launcher`/`Worker`, `Leader`/`Worker`), automatic headless DNS service discovery (`master-0.pytorch-job.svc.cluster.local`), automatic rank/world-size environment injection (`MASTER_ADDR`, `MASTER_PORT`, `WORLD_SIZE`, `RANK`, `LOCAL_RANK`), and coordinated failure domains (restarting the whole replica group or JobSet when one node fails).
2. **Kubeflow Training Operator (`PyTorchJob`, `kubeflow.org/v1`) and Kubeflow Trainer v2 (`TrainJob` & `ClusterTrainingRuntime`, `trainer.kubeflow.org/v1alpha1`):**
   - **Dissecting `PyTorchJob` (`volume-06` §8.2 & `GAK` Ch. 7 Examples 7-5, 7-8, 7-9, 7-11):**
     1. Role identification (`Master` `replicas: 1` and `Worker` `replicas: N-1`).
     2. Dynamic Headless Service creation (`master-0.pytorch-job.svc.cluster.local`) for `c10d` rendezvous.
     3. Automatic environment variable injection (`MASTER_ADDR`, `MASTER_PORT`, `WORLD_SIZE`, `RANK`).
     4. Coordinated lifecycle management (`restartPolicy`, gang integration with Kueue `kueue.x-k8s.io/queue-name` or KAI Scheduler `schedulerName: kai-scheduler`).
   - **Kubeflow Trainer v2 (`v2.1.0`, `GAK` Ch. 6 Examples 6-3 through 6-9):**
     - Persona split: Platform Admin creates `TrainingRuntime` / `ClusterTrainingRuntime` (`my-torch-distributed-runtime`, label `trainer.kubeflow.org/framework: torch`, `mlPolicy.numNodes: 1`, `torch.numProcPerNode: auto`, `replicatedJobs: [{name: node}]`, image `pytorch/pytorch:2.7.1-cuda12.8-cudnn9-runtime`), while Data Scientist uses `TrainerClient` and `CustomTrainer` (or `BuiltinTrainer` like `TorchTune`) to submit a `TrainJob` (`num_nodes=8`, `cpu: 4`, `memory: "64Gi"`, `nvidia.com/gpu: 1` for `Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` PEFT LoRA; or `Llama-3.2-3B` on `openai/gsm8k` with `num_epochs: 3`, `learning_rate: 2.0e-4`, `checkpoint-100`).
     - How `TrainJob` merges with `ClusterTrainingRuntime` to generate a **`JobSet`** CR under the hood, and how `client.train(func=my_custom_trainer)` serializes the Python function directly into the `TrainJob` CR without requiring a Docker image rebuild (unlike KubeRay `RayJob`).
     - Full fine-tuning (`100,000s` of examples, `100%` of weights modified) vs PEFT / LoRA (`100–1,000` labeled examples, `< 1%` of parameters tuned on `8B` model, `1%–10%` adapter size, Small Language Models `8B–16B`, DeepSpeed `deepspeed.init_distributed(dist_backend="nccl")` with `batch_size=16`).
3. **`JobSet` (`jobset.x-k8s.io/v1alpha2`) — The Unified Multi-Job Abstraction (`GAK` Ch. 6 & 7):**
   - A `JobSet` represents a coordinated group of Kubernetes Indexed `Job`s (`replicatedJobs`, e.g., `node`, or `initializer` + `trainer`) managed as a single atomic unit with shared headless DNS, coordinated startup ordering, and configurable `failurePolicy` (restarting all child Jobs when any member Job fails).
4. **`LeaderWorkerSet` (`LWS`, `leaderworkerset.x-k8s.io/v1`) — Multi-Node Sharded LLM Inference (`GAK` Ch. 7 Note):**
   - Why `PyTorchJob` / `JobSet` targets batch run-to-completion **training**, whereas **`LeaderWorkerSet` (`LWS`)** targets long-running **multi-host / multi-node sharded inference** (e.g., serving a `175B+` or `405B` LLM with vLLM/Ray or TGI across `2+` nodes).
   - `LWS` groups `1` Leader Pod (`rank 0`) + `size - 1` Worker Pods into an atomic replica group (`replicas` groups × `size` pods per group), providing gang scheduling per replica group, all-or-nothing group restart when any pod in a group fails, and topology-aware co-location per replica group.
5. **The Kubeflow `MPIJob` Operator (`kubeflow.org/v2beta1`) (`volume-06` §2.4):**
   - Architecture: `Launcher` Pod (`replicas: 1`) + `Worker` Pods (`replicas: 2`, `slotsPerWorker: 8`, `nvidia.com/gpu: 8` -> `16` total GPU ranks).
   - How the MPI Operator automatically generates SSH keypairs, mounts `/etc/mpi/hostfile` listing worker Pod DNS names and slot counts (`slots=8`), and executes `mpirun -np 16 /opt/nccl-tests/build/all_reduce_perf -b 8 -e 128M -f 2` with `cleanPodPolicy: Running`.
