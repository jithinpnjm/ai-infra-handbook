| **2. Disaggregated System Architectures: DistServe, Splitwise, TetriInfer, Mooncake, SGLang & NVIDIA Dynamo** | **Two Control/Data Plane Paradigms (`ASP` Ch. 17 & 18, `DAI` Ch. 7, `HLS` Ch. 7):** (1) **Router-Centric / Direct-Channel Design (`DistServe`, `SGLang`, `vLLM` + `LMCache`, `llm-d`):** Ingress router dispatches the raw prompt to a prefill worker (`control plane`) and notifies the selected decode worker; the prefill worker pushes/pulls KV blocks directly to the decode worker over RDMA (`Mooncake` or `NIXL` `data plane`), bypassing the router, and the decode worker streams tokens back. | **3. Prefill-to-Decode Worker Ratio Sizing (`N_P : N_D`) & Asymmetric Parallelism** | **First-Principles Worker Ratio Equation:** Given arrival rate `lambda` (RPS), effective uncached prompt length `L_in = S_prompt * (1 - r_cache)`, output length `L_out`, per-worker prefill token throughput `T_P` (tokens/s per prefill worker under the TTFT SLO), and per-worker decode token throughput `T_D` (tokens/s per decode worker at max batch `B_D` under the TPOT SLO): `N_P = ceil(lambda * L_in / T_P)` and `N_D = ceil(lambda * L_out / T_D)`. The ideal ratio is **`N_P / N_D = (L_in / L_out) * (T_D / T_P)`** (`= tau_P / tau_D`, where `tau_P` and `tau_D` are per-request GPU-seconds in prefill and decode). **Workload Regimes (`DAI` Ch. 7, `ASP` Ch. 17 & 18):** (1) **Prefill-heavy RAG / Document Summarization (`L_in = 8,192`, `L_out = 128`, ratio `64:1`):** Requires **`2:1` or `3:1` prefill-to-decode workers** (e.g., DistServe `2P1D` where `5.6 RPS` per prefill worker vs `10.0 RPS` decode worker yields `N_P : N_D = 10.0 / 5.6 = 1.79 -> 2:1`).
