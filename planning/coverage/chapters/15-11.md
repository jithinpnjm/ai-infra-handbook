| **1. Split-KV Parallel Decode Attention & Fused Decode Megakernels (`FlashDecoding`, `FlashInfer`, `XQA`, `FlashMLA`, `ThunderMLA`, `FlexDecoding`, `POD-Attention`)** | **Why standard `FlashAttention-2` underutilizes SMs during decode (`V12` Ch. 5):** During single-token decode (`S_query = 1`), assigning 1 CTA per query/KV head (`H_kv = 8` on `Llama-3-70B`, or `1` per TP rank at `TP=8`) leaves a `132`-SM H100 severely under-occupied while each CTA scans `S_kv` sequentially. **Split-KV Parallel Decode (`FlashDecoding` & `FlashInfer`):** Splits the historical KV cache of length $S_{\text{kv}}$ into $K_{\text{split}} = \lceil S_{\text{kv}} / C_{\text{split}} \rceil$ chunks (e.g., $C_{\text{split}} = 256$ tokens), launching $B \cdot H_{\text{kv}} \cdot K_{\text{split}}$ CTAs in Stage 1 to compute partial outputs $\hat{O}_k$ and log-sum-exp scalars $l_k = m_k + \log \sum \exp(s - m_k)$, followed by a Stage 2 online softmax reduction $O = \sum_k \exp(l_k - l_{\max}) \hat{O}_k / \sum_k \exp(l_k - l_{\max})$, achieving up to **`8x` faster decode** on long contexts. **`XQA` & `MHARunner` (`V12` Ch. 5):** TensorRT-LLM's `MHARunner` dispatches `XQA` (eXtended Query Attention) kernels that pack all $G = H_q / H_{\text{kv}}$ query heads sharing a KV head (e.g., $G = 8$ on `Llama-3-70B`) into a single Tensor Core MMA tile during decode. **`FlashMLA` & `ThunderMLA` (`ASP` Ch. 18):** DeepSeek's `FlashMLA` fuses single-token decode projections over compressed latent KV vectors ($d_c = 512, d_R = 64$), raising arithmetic intensity toward the H100 compute roofline; Stanford's `ThunderMLA` fuses attention decode and dynamic stream-packing into a single megakernel to eliminate tail effects when variable-length sequences finish early, delivering **`20%–35%` faster decode throughput** than `FlashMLA`. **`FlexDecoding` (`ASP` Ch. 18):** PyTorch `torch.nn.attention.flex_attention` decode backend (`Q_len = 1`) compiled with `torch.compile(mode="max-autotune")`, supporting **Nested Jagged Tensors (`NJT`)** for zero-padding ragged batches and `BlockMask` mapping logical PagedAttention / `LMCache` pages (`64–128` tokens) to physical memory without recompilation. **`POD-Attention` (`ASP` Ch. 18):** Uses SM-aware CTA runtime operation binding to colocate compute-bound prefill CTAs and memory-bound decode CTAs on the same SM, smoothing HBM traffic and improving attention performance by **up to `29%`**. | `ASP` Ch. 18, `V12` Ch. 5, `HLS` Ch. 6 |
| **2. Chunked Prefill Token-Budget Tuning, Decode-Maximal Batching, and Prefix-Cache Interaction** | **Prefill Bubble vs Token Budget (`V12` Ch. 7 & 8, `ASP` Ch. 19):** Without chunking, a `12,288`-token prompt monopolizes `4x H100` SMs for `320 ms` (or `380 ms` on `8,192` tokens), spiking `p99 ITL` from `18 ms` to `340 ms` across `45` active decode streams. **Sarathi-Lean Token Budget Equation:** $\text{Tokens}_{\text{iter}} = M_{\text{decode}} + \sum_j C_j \le \text{MaxTokensPerIter}$ (`--max-num-batched-tokens 2048`). With $M_{\text{decode}} = 512$ active decode streams ($1$ token each) and $C_{\text{prefill}} = 1,536$ prefill tokens, total tokens = $2,048$, bounding step time to `21.4–28.0 ms` (`< 25 ms` voice SLA) and improving throughput by **`1.3x–1.9x`** (`SARATHI`, e.g., slicing a `10,000`-token prompt into five `2,000`-token chunks of `50–100 ms`). **Over-Chunking Pitfall (`V12` Ch. 7):** Setting `max_num_batched_tokens = 512` (`8 ms` prefill chunk + `2 ms` decode step) triggers `450` scheduler context switches/sec, degrading `p99 ITL` from `28 ms` to `45 ms` and `TTFT` from `180 ms` to `320 ms`; raising `max_num_batched_tokens` to `1,024–2,048` eliminates thrashing. **Chunked Prefill vs Prefix Caching Pitfall (`V12` Ch. 8):** Naive whole-sequence prefix hashing drops prefix-cache hit rate from `45%` to `8%` (`5.6x` drop) when chunked prefill is enabled; aligning prefix hashes to chunk/block boundaries (`16`, `64`, or `512` tokens) or using `SGLang` `RadixAttention` restores full hit rates. | `V12` Ch. 7 & 8, `ASP` Ch. 16 & 19 |
| **3. KV Cache Quantization (`FP8`, `INT4`, `HQQ`), Sliding Window, StreamingLLM Sinks, and `H2O` / `SnapKV` / `LazyLLM` Eviction** | **Exact KV Cache Footprint Equations (`ASP` Ch. 17 & 18, `V12` Ch. 5 & 8):** $M_{\text{token}} = 2 \cdot L \cdot H_{\text{kv}} \cdot D_{\text{head}} \cdot P_{\text{bytes}}$. (1) **`70B` MHA at `250,000` tokens (`ASP` Ch. 18):** $L=80, H=32, D_{\text{head}}=128 \Rightarrow 4,096$ hidden dim $\times 2 = 8,192$ floats/layer $\times 80 = 655,360$ floats/token $= 1,310,720\text{ B}$ (`1.31 MB/token`) in FP16 $\Rightarrow$ **`327.68 GB` (`~328 GB`)** for `250,000` tokens, reduced to **`100–150 GB`** via `FP8` + selective-layer caching. (2) **`Llama-3-70B` GQA (`V12` Ch. 5 & 8):** $L=80, H_q=64, H_{\text{kv}}=8, D_{\text{head}}=128 \Rightarrow 163,840$ elements/token $= 327,680\text{ B}$ (`320 KiB/tok`) in FP16 (`1.31 GB` / `1.25 GiB` at $S=4,096$; `39.06 GiB` at $S=128,000$ vs `312.5 GiB` MHA) and `163,840 B` (`160 KiB/tok`, `0.65 GB` at $S=4,096$) in `FP8` (`E4M3`/`E5M2`). Reducing `tokens_per_block` from `128` to `32` (cutting `129`-token internal fragmentation from `127/256 = 49.61%` to `31/160 = 19.38%`), enabling `FP8` KV cache, and setting `kv_cache_free_gpu_memory_fraction = 0.85` scales concurrent sessions on `4x A100` (`TP=4`) from **`64` (`52%` util) to `210` (`91%` util)**. (3) **`Llama-13B` at `4,096` tokens (`ASP` Ch. 17):** $L=40, H=40, D_{\text{head}}=128 \Rightarrow 0.8192\text{ MB/tok}$ (`3.36 GB` MHA FP16, `1.68 GB` MHA FP8, `0.671 GB` GQA-8 FP16, `0.336 GB` GQA-8 FP8, `0.084 GB` MQA FP16). **Real-Time Policy Switching (`ASP` Ch. 19):** Hugging Face `QuantizedCache` (`backend="hqq"` or `"quanto"`, `q_group_size=64`, `residual_length=128` recent tokens in `BF16`/`FP16`) dynamically drops older tokens from `start_bits=8` (`INT8`) to `fallback_bits=4` (`INT4`) when smoothed GPU memory usage (`0.8 * old + 0.2 * new`) crosses `memory_threshold = 0.90` (or `0.80` high-watermark). **Windowed & Selective Eviction (`ASP` Ch. 18 & 19):** **Sliding Window Attention (`SWA`)** caps KV cache to window $W$ (e.g., $4,096$ tokens); **Attention Sinks (`StreamingLLM`)** retain $N_{\text{sink}}$ initial tokens (e.g., $4$ sink tokens) + $W$ recent tokens ($N_{\text{sink}} + W$ total) to prevent softmax denominator collapse when initial tokens scroll out of the window; **Heavy-Hitter / Observation-Window Eviction (`H2O`, `SnapKV`) & `LazyLLM` Pruning:** Retain sink tokens + top heavy-hitter tokens (highest cumulative attention scores over an observation window) + recent local window, or prune low-impact tokens dynamically (`LazyLLM` achieves **`20%–30%` end-to-end latency reduction** on long contexts). | `ASP` Ch. 17, 18, 19; `V12` Ch. 5 & 8 |
