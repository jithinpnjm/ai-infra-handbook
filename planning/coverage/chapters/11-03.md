## Checklist of Concepts, Numbers, and Patterns to Cover
1. **Why quantity is not a platform contract:**
   - A `10`-node T4 inference pool and a `10`-node H100 training pool both satisfy `nvidia.com/gpu: 1`.
   - Raw hardware inventory vs platform capability: why exposing every SKU string directly to application manifests creates brittle migrations.
   - Quantifying SKU-affinity blast radius: `80` GPU nodes across `3` generations (`30` `NVIDIA-A100-80GB`, `30` `NVIDIA-H100-80GB-HBM3`, `20` `NVIDIA-A100-40GB`) acquired over `18` months — retiring the `20` oldest nodes forces `N` manifest migrations under SKU coupling vs `1` platform controller config update (`0` application manifest edits) under a governed service class.
2. **Node Feature Discovery (NFD) architecture and label format:**
   - `nfd-worker` DaemonSet reading `/sys`, `/proc`, CPUID, and PCIe bus vs `nfd-master` updating `Node` labels (`42 label(s), 3 changed since last run` on `gpu-node-03`).
   - Standard label prefix `feature.node.kubernetes.io/` and `<class>_<vendor>` PCI convention: `pci-0302_10de.present: "true"` (`0302` = 3D controller, `10de` = NVIDIA; compare `1002` = AMD, `8086` = Intel, and `pci-0300_1d0f.present: "true"` where `0300` = VGA controller and `1d0f` = AWS EC2).
   - CPU, kernel, and SELinux NFD labels from *GAK* Example 3-2 and *V10* Ch 5: `cpu-cpuid.AVX512F=true`, `cpu-hardware_multithreading: "true"`, `cpu-model.family: "6"`, `cpu-model.id: "85"`, `cpu-model.vendor_id: Intel`, `kernel-selinux.enabled: "true"`, `kernel-version.full: 5.14.0-427.62.1.el9_4.x86_64`.
   - Kustomize installation (`kubectl apply -k https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/node-feature-discovery/deployment/overlays/default`) and GPU Operator `nfd.enabled: true` trigger (`pci-10de.present=true`).
3. **GPU Feature Discovery (GFD) architecture and Table 3-1 taxonomy:**
   - GFD DaemonSet querying NVML / `nvidia-smi` to publish detailed `nvidia.com/*` labels:
     - `nvidia.com/gpu.count` (`4` or `8`, or `14` on two A100s in `single` `1g.5gb` MIG mode, or `80` when `replicas: 8` on `10` GPUs)
     - `nvidia.com/gpu.product` (`A100-SXM4-40GB`, `NVIDIA-A100-SXM4-40GB`, `NVIDIA-H100-80GB-HBM3`, `Tesla-T4`, `-SHARED` suffix in time-slicing, `-MIG-1g.5gb` suffix in single MIG)
     - `nvidia.com/gpu.memory` (`40537` MiB or `40960` MiB on 40GB A100; `81920` MiB on 80GB A100/H100)
     - `nvidia.com/gpu.family` (`ampere`, `hopper`, `turing`)
     - `nvidia.com/cuda.driver-version.full` (`525.105.17` or `550.90.07`)
     - `nvidia.com/cuda.runtime-version.full` / `cuda.runtime.version.full` (`12.2` or `12.4`)
     - `nvidia.com/gpu.compute.major` (`8` for Ampere, `9` for Hopper) and `nvidia.com/gpu.compute.minor` (`0`)
     - `nvidia.com/gpu.machine` (`dgx-a100`)
     - `nvidia.com/mig.capable` (`true`), `nvidia.com/mig.strategy` (`single` or `mixed`), `nvidia.com/mig-<profile>.count` (`2` for `nvidia.com/mig-1g.5gb.count`), `nvidia.com/gpu.replicas` (`8`)
4. **Facts vs Lifecycle Assertions vs Service Classes & Scheduling Patterns:**
   - Three-tier label governance table: Discovered fact (owned by NFD/GFD), Lifecycle assertion (`gpu-validation=passed`, owned by platform validation controller), Service class (`gpu.platform.example/class=training-topology` or `inference-general` or `mig-serving`, owned by platform engineering).
   - Label-based scheduling patterns: `nodeSelector` (exact match, e.g., `Tesla-T4` or `compute.major: "8"`), `nodeAffinity` (`requiredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution` with `Gt` `"40000"` MiB + `preferredDuringSchedulingIgnoredDuringExecution` `weight: 1` `In: ["hopper"]`), and `taints`/`tolerations` (`nvidia.com/gpu=true:NoSchedule` or `nvidia.com/gpu=present:NoSchedule`).
5. **Discovery Drift and Troubleshooting:**
   - Stale labels (`gpu-node-19` NFD log at `08:40:11` with `0 label(s) changed` vs MIG maintenance at `09:15`).
   - Pool asymmetry (`gpu-node-19` has `mig.strategy=single` while `gpu-node-13` is missing `mig.strategy`).
   - Missing service class (`0/40 nodes are available: 40 node(s) didn't match Pod's node affinity/selector: key "gpu.platform.example/class" value "training-topology" not found`, even when `25` of `40` nodes have qualified GPUs).
