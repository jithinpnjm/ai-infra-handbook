| **4. Speculative Decoding Math, Medusa, EAGLE, MTP & Lookahead** | **Rejection Sampling & Expected Tokens (`ASP` Ch. 15, `HLS` Ch. 7, `DAI` Ch. 6):** Draft model $q(x)$ proposes $K$ tokens; target model $p(x)$ verifies all $K$ tokens in **1 parallel forward pass**. Token $i$ is accepted with probability $\min(1, p(x_i) / q(x_i))$ (e.g., $p=0.8, q=0.6 \Rightarrow 100\%$ accept; $p=0.4, q=0.6 \Rightarrow 66.7\%$ accept). On first rejection, remaining draft tokens are discarded and 1 corrected token is sampled from $\text{norm}(\max(0, p(x) - q(x)))$, guaranteeing **lossless target distribution** and at least $1$ token/step. For mean acceptance rate $\alpha$, expected tokens per verification step is $E[\text{tokens}] = \frac{1 - \alpha^{K+1}}{1 - \alpha}$, and net speedup is $S = \frac{E[\text{tokens}]}{1 + K \cdot c_{\text{draft}}}$ where $c_{\text{draft}} = t_{\text{draft}} / t_{\text{target}}$ (draft must be $\ge 4\times$ faster, i.e., $c_{\text{draft}} \le 0.25$). **Architecture Taxonomy:** (1) **Two-Model Draft-Verify** (`1.5x–2.5x` typical, `3x–4x` on code/JSON); (2) **Medusa** (`2.2x–3.6x` across Medusa-1/2, single-model multiple heads with depth-2 binary tree of `4` tokens cutting `100` tokens from `100` to `25` passes); (3) **EAGLE-1/2/3** (feature-level autoregression + dynamic draft tree + multi-layer feature fusion: EAGLE-1 `3.5x` on `4`-token draft, EAGLE-2 `+20%–40%`, EAGLE-3 `1.4x` over EAGLE-2 and up to `6.5x` over baseline; on `Qwen3-32B` in vLLM, `eagle3` with `K=3` reaches `56.5 tok/s` vs `28.9 tok/s` vanilla = **`1.96x` speedup** at `C=1`); (4) **Multi-Token Prediction (`MTP`)** (DeepSeek-V3/R1 native sequential MTP modules trained jointly with the main model, yielding `80%–90%` acceptance without external draft weights); (5) **Lookahead / N-gram Prompt Lookup** (zero-weight Jacobi/n-gram matching from prompt/context; `ngram` `K=4, min=2, max=128` gives `+16%` at `C=1` on `Qwen3-32B`, but degrades at `C=16` when decode becomes compute-bound); (6) **Self-Speculative Layer Skipping** (`~2x` speedup) & **Consistent Decoding** (`~3x` speedup). | `ASP` Ch. 15; `HLS` Ch. 7; `DAI` Ch. 6, 11 |
| **5. Conversely, pure prefix hashing without load bounds creates a **`3.27x` hotspot** (`Node 0: 3,600 reqs / 280K tokens` vs `Node 1: 1,100 reqs / 95K tokens`). Production routers (`llm-d`, NVIDIA Dynamo KV Router, SGLang Gateway) combine prefix-hit ratio, KV cache usage (`< 85%`), queue depth (`< 5`), and bounded-load failover (`1.25x–1.5x` balance threshold) or hot-prefix replication. | `V12` Ch. 9; `GAK` Ch. 3, 7; `HLS` Ch. 6, 7; `ASP` Ch. 15 |
