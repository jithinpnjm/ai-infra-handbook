## Checklist of Concepts, Numbers, and Patterns to Cover
1. **Hierarchical Queues (`Department -> Project`) and the Three Quota Parameters in KAI Scheduler:**
   - Why flat namespace `ResourceQuota` fails and how KAI Scheduler structures a two-level (or multi-level) **Hierarchical Fair Queuing (`HFQ`)** tree (`Department Queue -> Project / Team Queue`):
     - **`quota` (Nominal / Deserved Quota):** The guaranteed GPU baseline (`12.8`, `8.0`, `6.4`, `3.2`, `1.6` GPUs across Groups `A–E` on a `32`-GPU cluster, summing to `32.0` GPUs / `100.0%`).
     - **`overQuotaWeight`:** Proportional weight governing how idle/spare cluster GPUs are divided among multiple competing queues that burst above their nominal `quota`.
     - **`limit` (Max Allowed Quota with Borrowing):** Hard ceiling on `nominal + borrowed` GPUs (`-1` for unrestricted borrowing of idle GPUs, or a bounded cap).
   - How intra-department borrowing works before inter-department borrowing: idle GPUs in `Project A1` are lent first to sibling `Project A2` under the same `Department A` parent queue before spilling over to `Department B`.
2. **Proportional Over-Quota Fair-Share Mathematics:**
   - Given total cluster capacity $C$, each active queue $i$ with nominal quota $q_i$ and over-quota weight $w_i$ receives a **fair share**:
     $$\text{FairShare}_i = q_i + \frac{w_i}{\sum_{j \in \text{Active}} w_j} \times \left(C - \sum_{k} q_k\right)$$
     (and when some queues are idle—using $u_m < q_m$—their unused nominal capacity $\sum (q_m - u_m)$ is distributed across active bursting queues in proportion to $w_i$).
   - Worked 5-group (`A–E`) `32`-GPU hierarchical fair-share calculation (`volume-24/chapter-12`):
     - Shares: Group A (`5` researchers, `40%` = `12.8` GPUs, `307.2` GPUh), Group B (`4` researchers, `25%` = `8.0` GPUs, `192.0` GPUh), Group C (`3` researchers, `20%` = `6.4` GPUs, `153.6` GPUh), Group D (`2` researchers, `10%` = `3.2` GPUs, `76.8` GPUh), Group E (`1` researcher, `5%` = `1.6` GPUs, `38.4` GPUh; sum = `768.0` GPUh).
     - `2`-week capacity math: `32 GPUs * 14 days * 24 hours = 10,752` total GPU-hours (`768 / 10,752 = 7.14%` / `7.1%`; typical cadence `~2,100` GPUh = `~19.5%`), proving why **Level 3 Backfill** (`~8,900` GPU-hours of opportunistic/backfill jobs) is mandatory to reach **`> 90%` cluster utilization**.
3. **Capacity Planning Formula, Headroom Reserves, and Chargeback (`volume-11/chapter-09`):**
   - The `48%` utilization planning incident: why average GPU activity hides MIG profile geometry fragmentation and maintenance reserves.
   - Auditable node-sizing formula:
     $$N = \lceil (D + F) / (A \times (1 - H)) \rceil$$
     where $D$ is concurrent demand allocations, $F$ is fragmentation allowance, $A$ is usable allocations per node, and $H$ is the non-sellable headroom fraction (split across **5 named reserves**: `Node-failure`, `Maintenance`, `Rollout`, `Demand-variance`, and `Fragmentation`).
   - Showback vs Chargeback and billing by **Allocation Contract** (`MIG 3g.40gb` = `50%` of GPU cost even if `0%` compute utilization; `1/10` time-slice = `10%` rate; `volume-24/chapter-12` normal queue `$0.20`/GPU-hr vs priority queue `$0.40`/GPU-hr `2x` rate: Group A `2 * 8 * 48 * $0.20 = $153.60/week`, Group E `50 * 1 * (10/60) * $0.20 = $1.67/week`).
