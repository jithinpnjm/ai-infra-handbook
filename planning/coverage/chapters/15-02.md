| **Prefill vs Decode Roofline & Arithmetic Intensity** | **Prefill (Compute-Bound GEMM):** Processes all $s$ prompt tokens in parallel. For linear projection $[s, h] \times [h, h]$ in FP16 ($2$ bytes/element), $\text{FLOPs} = 2 \cdot s \cdot h^2$, $\text{Bytes} = 2 \cdot (2 \cdot s \cdot h + h^2)$, so $\text{AI}_{\text{prefill}} = \frac{s \cdot h^2}{2 \cdot s \cdot h + h^2}$. For $h = 4096$: $s = 64 \Rightarrow 62.06\text{ FLOPS/B}$, $s = 512 \Rightarrow 409.60\text{ FLOPS/B}$, $s = 4096 \Rightarrow 1,365.33\text{ FLOPS/B}$. **Decode (Memory-Bandwidth-Bound GEMV):** Generates $1$ token per request ($s_{\text{decode}} = 1$). At $B = 1$, $\text{AI}_{\text{decode}} = \frac{h^2}{2 \cdot h + h^2} \approx 1.0\text{ FLOPS/B}$ (and $\approx B\text{ FLOPS/B}$ for batch size $B$). **GPU Ridge Points:** `L40S` (`362 TFLOPS / 864 GB/s = 419 FLOPS/B`), `H100 SXM` (`1,979 TFLOPS / 3,350 GB/s = 590.7 FLOPS/B` in FP16; `3,958 TFLOPS / 3,350 GB/s = 1,181.5 FLOPS/B` in FP8), `H200 SXM` (`1,979 TFLOPS / 4,800 GB/s = 412.3 FLOPS/B`), `A100 SXM` (`312 TFLOPS / 1,935 GB/s = 161.2 FLOPS/B`). **MBU Formula (`AIE` Ch. 9):** $\text{Bandwidth Used} = \Psi \cdot P_{\text{bytes}} \cdot \text{TPS}$; $\text{MBU} = \frac{\Psi \cdot P_{\text{bytes}} \cdot \text{TPS}}{\text{Peak HBM Bandwidth}}$. | `HLS` Ch. 5, `AIE` Ch. 9, `VL5` Ch. 2, `V12` Ch. 7 |
| **Static, Dynamic & Continuous (In-Flight) Batching + Chunked Prefill** | **Static Batching:** Waits for $B$ requests and runs until the longest sequence $L_{\max}$ completes. **Dynamic Request Batching:** Queues requests up to `max_batch_size` $B$ or `max_queue_delay_microseconds` $\tau$ (e.g., `5,000 us` = `5 ms` conservative vs `20,000–50,000 us` aggressive). Suffers from **Padding Waste Ratio** $= 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^B L_i}{B \cdot \max_i L_i}$ (often $> 65\%$) and **Tail-Latency Locking** ($31$ requests of $10$ tokens trapped for $1,990$ extra steps by $1$ request of $2,000$ tokens; p99 ITL spikes to `420 ms` and TTFT to `14.2 s` at $18\%$ SM occupancy). **Continuous / In-Flight Batching (Orca):** Evaluates the batch at every single token iteration step $t$; completed sequences emitting `<EOS>` are evicted immediately and queued requests are slotted in with $0\%$ padding waste (`3.8x` to `23x` throughput gain, p99 ITL `420 ms -> 19.1 ms`). **Chunked Prefill (Sarathi-Lean):** Slices long prompts (e.g., $12,288$ tokens causing a `320–380 ms` prefill bubble and `340 ms` p99 ITL spike) into chunks $C$ (e.g., `512` or `1,024` tokens) bounded by $\sum_{i \in \text{Decode}} 1 + \sum_{j \in \text{Prefill}} C_j \le \text{MaxTokensPerIter}$ (`--max-num-batched-tokens 2048`), bounding step latency to `< 25–28 ms` (p99 ITL `340 ms -> 21.4 ms`). | `V12` Ch. 7 & Lab 02, `VL5` Ch. 2, `HLS` Ch. 2 & 6, `AIE` Ch. 9 |
