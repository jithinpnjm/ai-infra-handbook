# Coverage process: no important topic gets dropped

Source outlines (every h1–h4 heading in each book): `planning/source-outlines/<book>.md`, generated by `tools/coverage/extract_outlines.py`.

For every chapter or chapter-group in the book, create `planning/coverage/<part>-<chapter>.md` **before writing**:

1. List the source chapters used.
2. Copy their section headings (from the outlines) into a table: `Source heading | Status | Where`.
3. Status is one of: `covered` (with the section in our chapter), `moved` (to another named chapter), `skipped` (with a one-line reason: out of scope for AI infra, obsolete, duplicate), `gap-doc` (books thin; filled from NVIDIA/official docs, flagged).
4. Add "Beyond the books": topics from `INTERVIEW_TOPICS.md` or the JD that no source covers.
5. After writing, re-check: every heading has a status, and `skipped` items are reviewed by the author.

Quality bar for explanation depth: the books teach by building understanding (motivation, then mechanism, then worked example, then pitfalls). Our chapters must do the same and may not be shorter or shallower on a topic that the curriculum marks "must cover".
