## Sources fetched and what they say

1. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/digest-november-2024 [vendor]. Hardware R&D leads gave an OCP Global Summit talk on in-house server and rack design. Says designs rest on "several concepts and principles from the Open Compute Project" and that Nebius brought its own developments back to OCP. No rack power, cooling or density numbers. Talk title found via search of the OCP 2024 listing (not opened): "Designing In-House Server Solution for Hosting Modern GPUs" [third-party/unverified]. No OCP contribution named.
2. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926052948/nbis-20251231x20f.htm [filing]. Nebius Group N.V. annual report on Form 20-F for the year to 2025-12-31 (a 20-F, not a 10-K). Item 3 risk: relies on Nvidia for GPUs and on a limited number of other suppliers for key components; "limited availability of and access to the latest components"; limits on power availability, transmission and distribution; tariffs and export controls may affect sourcing; delays opening data centres can hold back revenue; "lack of available capacity in our existing data centers". Item 4: servers and racks designed in-house, proprietary server firmware, toolless rack design, components swapped "in minutes rather than hours", one engineer can manage thousands of servers (all qualitative, no uptime figure). Servers engineered for inlet temperatures up to 40 C versus the ASHRAE 27 C figure the filing cites; air cooling said to suffice at the Finland owned site. Internal tests said to show about 20% less power than comparable third-party hardware. GPU systems listed: GB300 NVL72, GB200 NVL72, HGX B200, HGX B300, HGX H100, RTX PRO 6000. H200 not listed in the section I read. Interconnect: NVIDIA InfiniBand NDR/XDR, no bandwidth figures. Storage: block, shared file and object, mixing in-house and third-party providers, no hardware spec. Active power about 170 MW at 2025-12-31, more than 2 GW contracted by Feb 2026 (not used in chapter). Read only the parts of the document returned by three fetch chunks; the last 55,000 characters and part of Item 4.D were not reviewed.
3. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926094568/tm2622968d1_ex99-2.htm [filing]. Shareholder letter dated 2026-08-12 for Q2 2026. "Capacity remains a binding constraint on AI adoption". First Vera Rubin NVL72 systems received and being validated. Early adoption of Vera CPUs. Prepayments now standard to secure capacity. Contracted-power and capex figures exist; not used. Q3 2026 results are due 2026-11-12 (nebius.com newsroom date notice, search result), so no Q3 numbers exist yet.
4. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/first-live-nvidia-gb300-nvl72-deployment-in-europe [vendor], 2025-12-17. Describes GB300 NVL72 as one rack-scale system of 72 GPUs, with NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand between racks. Says nothing on cooling, power, storage, or any Nebius design contribution.
5. https://nebius.com/compute/gb300 [vendor]. "Fully liquid-cooled, rack-scale system". 72 Blackwell Ultra GPUs, 36 Grace CPUs. 1.8 TB/s NVLink per GPU, 130 TB/s per rack. Quantum-X800 at 800 Gb/s per GPU across racks. Says servers are "in-house designed", clusters have auto-healing. Not self-service: sold through sales. Orchestration via Managed Kubernetes or Managed Soperator.
6. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/ai-cloud-aether-3-1 [vendor], 2025-12-17. HGX B300 offered. New sites use Quantum-X800 InfiniBand, 800 Gb/s. ConnectX-8 SuperNICs offload networking. Shared filesystem tested to scale performance linearly up to 4 PB. Capacity Dashboard and Capacity API in private preview then, public preview planned Q1 2026.
7. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-platinum-clustermax-3-0 [vendor quoting third party], 2026-09-23. SemiAnalysis ClusterMAX 3.0 Platinum. Tested burn-ins, storage under concurrent load, node and full-rack recovery on GB300 and HGX B300. SemiAnalysis found slow 4 KiB sequential allocating writes and metadata consistency problems for concurrent directory creation at 128 or more ranks; Nebius retuned the filesystem and it was retested. A synthetic error injected into a GB300 NVL72 rack: Nebius detected it and returned the node to service without manual action; SemiAnalysis said other GB300 NVL72 racks it tested did not automatically reboot and return failed nodes. Rack-level recovery results not given. Synthetic error, not a real fault. I did not read the SemiAnalysis report itself.
8. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-on-nvidia-gb300-for-training [vendor], 2026-04-29. Exemplar Cloud means NVIDIA checks performance, resiliency and total cost of ownership using its benchmarking recipes. Nebius reports reaching a 95% threshold against NVIDIA's reference architecture on all recipes; metric not defined. H200 training status dated 2025-09-29.
9. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/nebius-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-on-nvidia-hgx-b300-for-training [vendor], 2026-10-08. B300 training status. "Each GPU platform requires its own validation". Training only. No scores, no regions.
10. https://nebius.com/blog/posts/mlperf-inference-v6-1-results and https://nebius.com/blog/posts/mlperf-training-v6-0-results [vendor]. Result tables exist; the inference post says the GB300 NVL72 ran on bare metal and the other systems ran as VMs. MLPerf commentary is out of scope for the chapter; the VM-versus-bare-metal remark is the only operational detail. Vera Rubin NVL72 appeared as a preview-category result.
11. https://nebius.com/storage [vendor]. "proprietary hardware designs for most AI infrastructure components", naming NVMe-based storage servers. Shared filesystem all-flash NVMe. "over 500 GB/s aggregate read", footnoted as burst on a 254-GPU, 254-CPU-host customer cluster. No durability figure. Names WEKA as a partner.
12. https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-to-triple-capacity-at-finland-data-center-to-75-mw [vendor], 2024-10-08. Own data centre in Finland, free cooling, 40 C server inlet claim (about 15% more energy saved), H100 installed, H200 from November 2024, Blackwell planned 2025. Site and PUE detail not used.
13. https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-accelerates-us-expansion-adding-up-to-300-mw-capacity-at-new-data-center-in-new-jersey [vendor]. States the New Jersey facility "will be built to Nebius's own design". Not used further.
14. https://docs.nebius.com/kubernetes/maintenance/health-checks.md [official], also saved at /private/tmp/claude-501/v20/v33/. NebiusGPUError, five-minute checks, cordon, wait for GPUs, drain up to one hour, stop and start VM, uncordon. NebiusBootDiskIOError after 30 s of boot-disk I/O errors. Ready false over five minutes or Unknown over 15 minutes leads to node deletion and replacement.
15. https://docs.nebius.com/compute/virtual-machines/maintenance-reasons.md [official]. Reason codes: HW_NODE_OFFLINE, HW_GPU_PCI_FALLEN_OFF_BUS (GPU or NVSwitch off the bus, "usually" overheating or power), HW_GPU_PCI_CONFIG_ERROR, HW_GPU_NVLINK_DOWN (Blackwell or newer), HW_GPU_XID_62, HW_GPU_XID_109, HW_GPU_XID_119, HW_GPU_FW_VERSION_UNAVAILABLE, HW_GPU_DRIVER_INIT_FAILED, HW_IB_LINK_DOWN (physically down over 3 minutes), HW_IB_PCI_FALLEN_OFF_BUS, HW_IB_PCI_CONFIG_ERROR, OTHER.
16. https://docs.nebius.com/slurm-soperator/worker-nodes-health.md [official]. Scheduled critical checks: NCCL AllReduce with and without InfiniBand, CUDA samples, DCGM diagnostics, GPU stress, memory bandwidth and latency. Failure path: drain with `[node_problem]`, suspicious reservation, extensive hardware tests, `[hardware_problem]` drain and node re-creation. A Compute maintenance event during checks stops them and the node is drained and recreated. Custom prolog or epilog checks are not auto-healed by default. Page names no Xid, ECC, NVLink or thermal checks and gives no intervals.
17. https://docs.nebius.com/compute/virtual-machines/maintenance.md [official], chapter 33 cites it. Stop and start before the due date; reschedule twice, up to seven days each.
18. Handbook chapters derived from gem books, read in full for the parts used: docs/part-09-nvidia-systems/08 (book superpod-gb300-ra) and 07 (book superpod-b200-dc). GB300 SuperPOD scalable unit: 8 racks, 144 compute trays, 576 GPUs, 1.2 MW TDP, 64 power shelves of 33 kW, hybrid direct-liquid plus air cooling, 8 Q3400-RD leaf switches as 4 rails x 2, 72 downlinks and 72 uplinks per leaf, SN5600D compute leaves at 1:3 blocking, storage leaves non-blocking, out-of-band SN2201, Mission Control with dcgmi and nvsm health checks that isolate degraded trays. B200: six PSUs per node with five required, five power wiring models; dual interleaved is a mistake. Per the handbook chapter, the deriving of 72+72 per leaf is the chapter's own inference.
19. Book *AI Data Center Network Design and Technologies*, chapters "Network Design Considerations", "Optics and Cable Management", "Monitoring and Telemetry", "Thermal and Power Efficiency Considerations", read via tools/study_rag/bookch.py. Rail-optimised design: GPU n of every server on leaf n; same-rail traffic one hop, cross-rail goes through the spine; oversubscription applied at the super spine for large clusters; monitor ECN counts, leaf-spine utilisation; FEC corrects optics errors.
20. Book *AI Factory* chapter list read; it covers industrial AI transformation, digital twins and systems engineering, and has no data-centre hardware content. Not used. Stated in the chapter.
