## Worst 15 files

1. part-09-nvidia-systems/07: "mandatory before a single crate arrives on the loading dock". Instead: State the real risk: "If a PDU fails, three of six supplies drop on every server." Drop "massive air volume" and give the airflow figure with its source.
2. part-09-nvidia-systems/05: "multiplies per-GPU throughput by more than thirty times and cuts first-token latency to 50 milliseconds". Instead: Attribute it: "NVIDIA reports about 30x on one benchmark." Say what the benchmark was; replace the "Therefore" opener.
3. part-09-nvidia-systems/01: "known as the Whitebox Fallacy". Instead: Drop the invented name and the engine metaphor: "A server built from loose parts has not been tested as a whole."
4. part-09-nvidia-systems/08: "Network engineers and distributed training users feel this penalty when collective operations cross extra switch hops". Instead: Vary the opener and say what happens: "Each extra switch hop adds latency to every all-reduce."
5. part-09-nvidia-systems/12: "Distributed framework and memory sharding layers include PyTorch DDP, DeepSpeed ZeRO, Megatron-Core, and DLPack.". Instead: Pick one example and show a launch: "torchrun starts one process per GPU." Put the product list in a table.
6. part-09-nvidia-systems/14: "keep cloud clusters running at line rate". Instead: "These pages show what a cloud GPU node exposes and what you must still configure."
7. part-09-nvidia-systems/06: "System 1 is High-Performance Storage, or HPS, which is a resilient, POSIX-compliant parallel file system". Instead: Split in three sentences; move partner names and requirements to a table.
8. part-18-security/05: "Write an alert that fires whenever a node's IOMMU is disabled, so the gap never goes unnoticed.". Instead: Give one worked alert with its rule, then ask for a different one. Six look-alike exercises add nothing.
9. part-13-ai-storage/02: "Compute the lifetime TBW rating for one drive and for the eight-drive array.". Instead: One exercise, one question, with the inputs in a short block; split the six-step number walls.
10. part-13-ai-storage/03: "Calculate the copy or DMA time, the total fetch time, and the copy speedup factor.". Instead: Ask one question per step and give the numbers once in a table.
11. part-09-nvidia-systems/09: "Dedicated ports provide independent out-of-band management for the DPU cards.". Instead: Use a table of component and port; keep prose for why management traffic is kept apart.
12. part-09-nvidia-systems/02: "A result around 43 percent sits cleanly inside the 40 to 60 percent target band". Instead: Name the calculation first: "Our run reached 43 percent Model FLOPs Utilization. Well-tuned clusters land between 40 and 60."
13. part-20-nebius-sre-interview/15: "which gives 800 combinations, times 12 gateway pods, giving 9,600 series.". Instead: Put the steps in a small table and say in one sentence that tenant labels multiply series by about 100.
14. part-20-nebius-sre-interview/37: "(the pre-4.9.0 spelling; dcgm-exporter 4.9.0 renamed the utilisation field ..., see 17.4, and I could not confirm ...)". Instead: Move the rename to a footnote or table note; keep one plain sentence in the body.
