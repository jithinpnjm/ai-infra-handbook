Part 12: Complete AI networking

24 chapters. Default priority tier 1.

📄️12.1 AI traffic: collectives, incast and elephant flows

AI traffic: collectives, incast and elephant flows

📄️12.2 RDMA fundamentals: verbs, queue pairs and memory registration

RDMA fundamentals from first principles: kernel bypass vs TCP/IP, Verbs object hierarchy, Protection Domains, Memory Regions, lkey/rkey and MTT page pinning, Queue Pair state machine, Send/Receive vs one-sided RDMA Write/Read, and BTH/RETH/AETH packet decoding.

📄️12.3 RoCEv2 transport and congestion management

RoCEv2 encapsulation (UDP port 4791 vs RoCEv1 0x8915), GID table semantics, DSCP 26 / CoS 3 QoS trust, the 6 Clos congestion points, ECN, PFC, DCQCN, PFC Watchdog, Go-Back-N vs Selective Repeat, Source Flow Control (IEEE 802.1Qdw), and Congestion Signaling (CSIG).

📄️12.4 PFC, ECN and DCQCN in depth

IEEE 802.1Qbb Priority Flow Control, RFC 3168 ECN marking, DCQCN closed-loop rate control, buffer headroom math, PFC Watchdog, SFC, and CSIG.

📄️12.5 Data center bridging and QoS for AI

IEEE 802.1Qaz ETS and DCBX, DSCP-to-Priority mapping, QoS trust boundaries, buffer pool carving, and drift detection for AI Ethernet fabrics.

📄️12.6 InfiniBand fundamentals and addressing

InfiniBand physical signaling and encoding, GUID, LID, LMC, GID, and directed-route addressing, P_Key partitioning, Virtual Lanes and 64-byte credit-based flow control, IPoIB, frame formats, and verbs diagnostics.

📄️12.7 InfiniBand subnet manager, routing, topologies and rails

OpenSM discovery, priority master election, Directed Route SMPs, LFT and MFT programming, minhop, updn, ftree, dor, and dfsssp routing engines, Fat-Tree oversubscription math, rail-optimized topologies, and AI fabric routing.

📄️12.8 InfiniBand adaptive routing and congestion control

InfiniBand credit-based flow control, congestion trees, Adaptive Routing (AR), SHIELD autonomous self-healing, OOO packet reordering, and FECN/BECN/CCTI congestion control.

📄️12.9 InfiniBand diagnostics, UFM and operations

Complete OFED/MFT InfiniBand diagnostic workflow (ibstat, ibstatus, ibnetdiscover, iblinkinfo, perfquery, ibqueryerrors, mlxlink, ibdiagnet, smpquery, saquery, ibportstate), NVIDIA UFM Enterprise HA/Telemetry/Cyber-AI operations, and 7-gate production incident triage.

📄️12.10 HDR, NDR, XDR, optics and cabling

InfiniBand link generations from SDR to HDR, NDR, XDR, and GDR, PAM4 SerDes and DSP physics, MMF/SMF/DWDM fibers, VR/SR/DR/FR/LR/ZR/CR transceivers, MPO/LC/DAC/AEC/AOC cabling, QSFP-DD vs OSFP form factors, and LPO/LRO/CPO optics.

📄️12.11 NVIDIA Spectrum-X and Spectrum switches

NVIDIA Spectrum-4 (SN5600/SN5600D 51.2 Tbps 64x800G / 128x400G) and SN2201 switches, Spectrum-X end-to-end architecture with BlueField-3 SuperNICs, Adaptive Routing and per-packet RoCEv2 spraying (sDLB), hardware out-of-order Direct Data Placement (DDP), hardware telemetry-driven congestion control, WJH drop sampling, and Cumulus Linux NVUE automation.

📄️12.12 Ethernet versus InfiniBand: a design decision guide

Architectural, operational, and economic decision guide comparing InfiniBand, Standard RoCEv2, NVIDIA Spectrum-X, and Ultra Ethernet across AI training, inference, and storage fabrics.

📄️12.13 Fabric design: topology, oversubscription and scale

End-to-end AI data center fabric architecture: Rail-Optimized. Rail-Only, and Rail-Unified designs, ToR/MoR/EoR leaf placement, 3-stage and 5-stage Clos oversubscription scaling, Scheduled Fabric VOQ cell spraying, Dragonfly, Torus, Multi-Planar breakout fabrics, and rack thermal/power engineering.

📄️12.14 Load balancing and routing for AI fabrics

Per-flow and per-packet load balancing (SLB, DLB, GLB NNHN, TELB, sDLB) and IP routing protocols (eBGP unnumbered, BGP DPF, RIFT, IS-IS Flex Algo, EVPN-VXLAN multi-tenancy, and SRv6 uSID) for AI/ML data center fabrics.

📄️12.16 NCCL: topology, channels, transports and environment variables

📄️12.17 nccl-tests and fabric benchmarking

Layered fabric validation from nvbandwidth and RDMA perftest to nccl-tests, algbw/busbw math, straggler bisection, and distributed training/inference benchmarking.

📄️12.18 NCCL debugging: hangs, timeouts and errors

NCCL debugging: hangs, timeouts and errors

📄️12.19 GPUDirect RDMA and GPUDirect Storage

Eliminate the host CPU bounce-buffer across network and storage data paths using GPUDirect RDMA (BAR1 P2P DMA, nvidia-peermem, dma-buf, NCCL_NET_GDR_LEVEL, PCIe ACS) and GPUDirect Storage (cuFile, nvidia-fs.ko, gdsio, NVMe-oF).

📄️12.20 SHARP and in-network computing

Offload collective reductions and broadcasts into NVSwitch (NVLS) and Quantum InfiniBand switch silicon (SHARP / CollNet) to cut endpoint traffic by (N - 1)x, overlap AllGather with ReduceScatter, and achieve 2x to 5x collective speedups at scale.

📄️12.21 Storage networks, Ultra Ethernet and scale-up fabrics

Design and verify AI storage networks (NVMe-o-TCP, NVMe-o-RDMA, parallel file systems, InfiniBand storage), Ultra Ethernet Consortium (UEC v1.0 UET, PDS/SES, ROD/RUD/RUDI/UUD, NSCC, RCCC, CBFC, LLR, INC), and open scale-up fabrics (OCP SUE-T/ESUN and UALink 1.0).

📄️12.22 AI network telemetry and KPIs

AI data center performance KPIs (JCT. Time-to-train, throughput, tail latency, accuracy. PUE, FLOPS/watt, strong/weak scaling), MLCommons. MLPerf benchmarking (Training, Inference LoadGen Offline/Server. Storage, Closed/Open divisions, MLCube), streaming telemetry (gRPC/gNMI periodic. On-change), In-Band Flow Analyzer (IFA initiator/transit/terminator header and 32-byte metadata stack), InfiniBand and RoCEv2 counter correlation, and closed-loop corrective actions.

📄️12.23 Fabric validation and capacity planning

Multi-stage cluster acceptance gating (L1 physical/DOM/BER to L5 burn-in), 2-tier and 3-tier switch/transceiver/cable BOM calculation, bottleneck cut and N-1 capacity modeling, and Topograph/LLDP/ibnetdiscover cable matrix validation.

📄️12.24 AI networking troubleshooting playbook

Production incident playbook for AI fabrics: bottom-up 7-gate InfiniBand and 6-gate RoCEv2 decision trees, PCIe/DMA/NVLink/Xid hardware triage, NCCL timeout classification, O(log2 N) straggler bisection, and cross-layer diagnosis when metrics lie.
